Peer and Near-Peer Competition

The Commissioner's editorial ranking (Draft Issue). It is a judgment, published as one. Taken from Draft Power Rankings in the Draft Issue, as corrected in that issue.

#1 Los Bandidos (Bandit) model #1

Trevor Lawrence at a 37-pick discount and Josh Jacobs late (who is now no longer on the roster). The design holds one assumption per capability area instead of five, which is why the bill stayed small. The Fairbairn kicker premium (minus-80) is the stain on the team's positivity.

#2 Dave model #8

model has them 6 spots lower

Somehow, the sole roster to get a positive value vs. ADP. Is my methodology flawed? Who knows. Regardless, please lord this over the rest of the Skunks and wolves next week.

#3 Swifty Mahomey (Pappie) model #6

model has them 3 spots lower

Landed the actual Patrick Mahomes at pick 114 after wearing his name all August, then built a four-piece Rams block around Stafford anyway, which is hedging your identity crisis at market rates. Two stacks show real theory of victory. The Rams defense at minus-84 paid for it.

#4 SHACtin' a Fool 🐻 (SHAC) model #4

Bijan Robinson plus an Atlanta triple is a concentrated bet on one offense, and Jaxson Dart 23 picks late hedges the quarterback risk for cheap. Two defenses is one more than doctrine requires, and Seattle at minus-68 was the expensive one.

#5 Wild SeeKats (Seabass/Kats) model #3

model has them 2 spots higher

The most conventional force structure in the league: one of everything, nothing exotic, no assumption stacked on another. Minus-143 almost entirely from paying early at kicker (Cam Little, minus-73). The design is sound; the parts procurement needs work.

#6 Gary model #2

model has them 4 spots higher

Jared Goff 28 picks late was clean value. Jason Myers 95 picks early is the single largest kicker premium in the league, and at this table that is a contested title. One design choice is carrying half the deficit.

#7 Swanson model #5

model has them 2 spots higher

Two different city triples (Chicago and Denver), which is a wargame where two scenarios decide everything. Caleb Williams 29 picks late funds part of it. High variance, priced accordingly.

#8 Statistical Anomalies (McLovin) model #7

model has them 1 spot higher

My design, and the review board goes hardest here because I chair it. I assumed tight end was optional until round 14, the league median said round 7, and my reward was Juwan Johnson. Joe Burrow 16 late and a plus-16 start could have anchored something; the Dicker kicker buy at minus-80 and the tight end timing gave it all back. Eighth is generous and I will not be appealing.

#9 L’entente Discordiale (Borat/Jack) model #9

The coalition committed fully: three 49ers, three Bills, and Jordan James 131 picks early with the afterburner lit. Two NFL locker rooms now carry an entire fantasy season, which is the most honest force design in the league and also the most flammable. Minus-413 is what conviction costs before the shooting starts, and the shooting starts Thursday.

#10 MJS HURT Locker (MJ) model #10

Amon-Ra St. Brown, Sam LaPorta, and Jake Bates is all-in on Detroit as the future operating environment. If Detroit is the future, this is the best draft in the league; the minus-330 is the premium for deciding that in August, with Bates at minus-91 the steepest single line item.
