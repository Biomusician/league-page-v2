Reach/Steal compares each pick with the league's stored reference board (FantasyPros Expert Consensus Rank (redraft, superflex) (retrieved 2026-08-29)). REACH means the player went at least one full league round (12 picks here) earlier than reference; STEAL means at least one full round later. Color deepens with the size of the deviation and saturates at three rounds. This measures draft-market value against a reference board — not whether the pick ultimately succeeds. Headline Reach and Steal cover QB, RB, WR and TE only: kickers and defenses sit below the draftable range on that board while lineups force everyone to draft one, so their deviations measure the board's shape rather than anybody's judgment, and a pick whose reference rank falls past the end of the draft carries no deviation at all.
