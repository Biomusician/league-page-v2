2021, reconstructed

Reconstructed, not recorded. Nobody wrote these results down. Each one is worked out from the records printed in the weekly previews: a team that was 3-1 going into week 5 and 4-1 going into week 6 won in week 5. A game only appears here when exactly one side gained a win and the other gained a loss, so a week the newsletters disagree about produces nothing rather than a guess.

McLovin led the recovered weeks at 9-2. The masthead records Babe as the 2021 champion, so the season did not end the way the regular season ran.

52 games recovered across weeks 1–13. Weeks 7, 8 are missing because the issue on either side of them contradicts itself, and a week that cannot be trusted is left out rather than guessed at.
