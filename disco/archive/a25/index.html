2021 Disco Week 6

Disco Chat Vol 3.VI

The Lowdown Air Force Edition I’m stuck at Neptune Eagle here at Nellis, which is why this week’s newsletter is late. Some of you are stuck in denial on why your fantasy teams are bad, which is why this newsletter exists. Someone needs to speak truth to power, and also speak truth to your old, disadvantaged, limping-along B-52 corpse of a team. “But McLovin,” you might say, “the B-52 can do plenty!” Sure, if your roster is loaded up with weapons that have the potential to explode, but you don’t manage your team to a successful ingress, with escort, SEAD, and all the things, none of your team’s destructive potential will be realized, and you’ll be the smoking crater, not your targets.

Matchup Roundup FFW EMCO (3-2) vs HOP (3-2) 68/32 BYEs: Mike Davis (HOP), Deebo (EMCO) Both teams have put up solid Thursday night performances, with Leonard Fournette blowing up like an explosive-laden drone against overwhelmed defenses provided by a solid showing (but terrible IRL showing) by Jalen Hurts on HOP’s side. Chris Godwin RTB’d early without employing ordnance, and Zach Ertz just CAP’d for a few hours before reporting “NSTR” on his way to Arizona. Will he get double points if he manages to play this Sunday? Who knows? Maybe he’d be breaking crew rest to do it. Anyway, this is turning into an 8vX against an EMCO threat that includes JT, Mixon, Chase, and Diontae, so HOP will need great pK to successfully defend his >0.500 record. The Dude (2-3) vs Juan (0-5) 55/45 BYEs: Aiyuk, Elijah Moore, and Kittle (The Dude) Have you ever seen an E-3 try to dogfight an F-15C? Neither have I, but i imagine it’s like watching Juan’s team try to win against any opponent this year. The Adams/Rodgers stack is like being able to participate in J-Voice: It’s kinda cool, but no one really cares all that much, and it’s not a game changer for most weeks. Gio Bernard busted airspace and was sent home early with only 1.4 points, which is why it’s gonna be an uphill battle for Juan even if DeAndre Hopkins manages to banzai an entire first wave made up of a two ship of Darrel(l)s, which is probably the most ridiculous running back lineup ever started. It’s like sending a KC-10 and KC-135 into battle together with a ROBE kit, and an AMRAAM strapped to the boom. Good luck The Dude; you’ll need it with your band of misfits. Fingers (4-1) vs Pitch (1-4) 62/38 BYEs: Sermon and MT (Pitch), Jameis (Pitch) If last match was an E-3 vs F-15C, this one is F-22 vs KC-46. Fingers’ Brady has been dominant, and even though Mike Evans was the “any airman” who called a KIO this week, putting up 3.7 fantasy points, the Sniffers are favored this week against a Browns team that is truly brown-staining it up by starting Gronk as their FLEX, even though he was ruled out early in the week. Pathetic! That’s like showing up to an exercise with 2 NMC jets and then surprised pikachu facing when you support 1/12 VULs. Jared Goff is a C-17, and even though you have elite weapons in Zeke, Pollard (gross combo tho), and Waller, it’s going to be tough sailing or you against King Henry, who is like a 4 ship of F-35s (gigantic, slow, and effective), and Travis Kelce, who is like an F-15C (old, highly competent, and effective). Lots of explosions in this one, but heavily tilted in Fingers’ favor. DIP (3-2) vs Sealed (3-2) 73/27 BYEs: Cordaralle Patterson (Sealed), Matt Ryan (DIP) Both teams are 3-2 here, but Sealed started Jalen Reagor, who was shot down by a friendly Patriot before even making it to his working area, putting up zero points, and putting Sealed way behind the power curve. The ground alert DCA are scrambling, but T-Law, Dalvin, Javonte, and even DJ Moore probably won’t get it done against the adversary, who’s rocking Patty Mahomes, Gibson, Stefon Diggs, and the new kid on the block Kadarius Toney, who’s like the SCRAM-K of the WR world. IYKYKIYDYD (sorry Fingers and HOP). Starting Devontae Booker at RB isn’t ideal, but Saquon has an FMC rate of like 31%, so you need to power up the spare and hope it’s g2g. Damien Harris and Jamaal Williams round out an unimpressive FLEX matchup, but at least he’s not starting Nelson Agholor. McLovin (4-1) vs Babe (2-3) 59/41 BYEs: Kamara (Babe), Calvin Ridley and Elijah Mitchell (McLovin) Before AB put up a ridiculous 19.8 fantasy points, which is like an MQ-9 shooting down a fighter, I was favored to win by even more. Fortunately, DeVonta Smith ORM’d out of the fight with 4.1 points, because he didn’t take a fucking nap. Better luck next time to the Slim Reaper. Josh Allen at TEN should ball this week, with an optimized loft profile and supersonic speed against Justin Herbert. Chuba continues to be a solid option for Babe, but he also has to start “Smooches” McKissic and Marvin Jones this week to complete his lineup, which can’t feel great. This feels like a strike package made up of A-10s, B-52s, and B-1s - if you can find the endzone and score a few TDs you’ll be fine, but if you get all your players shut down, it’s game over.
