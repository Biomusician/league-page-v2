2021 Daddy Week 2 Preview

Daddy Issues Vol III.II

The Lowdown Begun, the Fantasy Wars Have Already a couple injuries, already a couple upsets, and a ton of confirmation bias/massively unlucky turns, depending on how your team did the first week. Crushed it? All skill. Got beat? All luck. In any case, wins don’t come to the teams that lie back and wait for them, so get after it - unless, that is, you’re tanking for the next Tua or T-Law. Then again, considering how those are working out, maybe it’s better that you try anyway.

This Week’s Matchups Choose Your Own Adventure Return of the Jedi: Bazinga (1-0) vs McLovin (0-0) 55/45 I’m no Luke Skywalker (more like old Ben or just Uncle Owen tbh), but someone’s gotta defeat the Emperor. Unfortunately, if my Aarons can’t get their shit together, there’s no hope. If Zeke is washed, there’s no hope. But, if Waller doesn’t get 19 targets again, there’s a New Hope. Fortunately, I’ve found the exhaust port for Bazinga’s Deathstar, and am using his own NE Patriots DST against him, while he’s choosing to believe that LASIK Jameis isn’t a thing, and chancing a play at the Panthers DST. It’s boring to talk about Bazinga’s team: Kamara, Cook, Metcalf, Justin Jefferson, and Travis Kelce, who is basically a Gronk who never retired-then-came back, and wears a red vs RWB uniform. Elite options, blah blah blah. Praying Amari Cooper has fewer than a bajillion fantasy points like he did week 1. Breaking Bad: Karen (0-1) vs OK Zoomer (1-0) 50/50 Once upon a time, Karen was a contender. She had a nice team, a nice fantasy football life, and then she learned that her team had cancer, and she had to rebuild it from the ground up. It cost everything, and now she’s learning that the road to recovery isn’t easy. Starring Kyle Pitts as Mike Ehrmantraut, Ja’Marr Chase as Jesse, Najee Harris as Huell Babineaux, and Dak Prescott as Saul Goodman. Also starring Walter White Jr. as Tyler Bass and the WFT DST as Skyler. She’s up against a host of bad hombres, including Justin Herbert as Tuco, MGIII as Hector, and DeAndre Hopkins as Gus Fring. Also starring Mark Andrews as Hank Shrader, and Saquon Barkley as Tortuga, that guy who got broken in half and then was never the same again. Step Brothers: Distro (0-1) vs Ross (0-1) 55/45 “Two losers still living at home are forced against their will to fight in week two, when they are given an ultimatum to win a fantasy football game within a month.” That’s (almost) literally the movie rundown from IMDB. Ross is hoping for the Josh Allen of old, not the accuracy-challenged version we saw week 1. Distro’s hoping for the Patty Mahomes of perpetuity, and that CEH finds a way to be relevant as one of his RB2s opposite old man Carson. Starring George Kittle as Will Ferrel and Mike Evans as John C. Reilly. There are no other actors from this movie I recognize, and I’ve never seen it, so we’ll leave it there. On paper, Ross smashes Distro’s team, but if Josh Allen and Kittle drop the ball, Distro’s right back in it. John Wick 3: Kelsey (1-0) vs DIP (1-0) 70/30 After DIP killed Kelsey’s beagle Daisy (Cam Akers) after his wife Helen (Travis Etienne) died, Kelsey (Keanu Reeves) is fighting back against the mafia. He’s lost everything, but he’s still got a lot of guns and bullets (played by CMC, Swift, Wilson, Lockett, and Godwin). He’s even got an old, experienced, basically retired man on his side: Antonio Brown (Winston, the Continental Manager). DIP’s got no shortage of hitmen out to win big on his side, including Lamar, the double Mi/yles combo, Adams/Ridley, and the double Chase. Even in pairs, however, they’re not enough. Kelsey is a man on a mission, out to kick some Butker, and no one will stand in his way. May the best Godwin! Fight Club: Basic (1-0) vs Jolly (0-1) 65/35 Two sides of the same coin slugging it out in darkness. Neither team was favored in their week 1 matchups, but one emerged victorious over McLovin, and one lost it all to the Zoomers. Basic’s apartment/team exploded, leaving him starting players like Elijah Mitchell, Nelson Agholor, and James Robinson. So they moved to Basic’s place, where they enjoyed such amenities as Christian Kirk and MVS. Both players saw the elite talent (Ekeler, Kyler, Chubb, D-Henry), but their anti-materialist, anti-corporate organization, Project Mayhem, doesn’t allow them to fully take advantage of their talent. Both teams are kind of the same team, and they ultimately end this year shooting themselves while collapsing several credit card company buildings around them. Damn, that movie was weird. Starring Gronk as Brad Pitt and Adam Thielen as Edward Norton.

SIGEVENTS YGBFSM Worst Decisions Ross starting Brandon Aiyuk, who scored zero points. Will the rebuild continue?! McLovin started Aaron Rodgers, Aaron Jones, and Zeke, who combined for fewer than 10 points. Old and broken. Boomers. Top Rookies Trevor Lawrence (McLovin) 22, who I’ll probably not include again since he’s a starting QB. His 3 TDs and 3 INTs are promising and worrisome. Ja’Marr Chase (Commish) 21, who overcame accusations of drops and domestic violence for 101 and 1 on 5 catches, 7 targets Devonta Smith (Ross) 19, who put up 71 yards on 6 catches for a TD. The Slim Reaper doesn’t have any issues with separation, despite his smaller frame. Zach Wilson (Kelsey) 18 , who threw 257 yards on 37 passes (20 completions) and 2 TDs Elijah Mitchell (Jolly) 16, who was a surprise start for the 49ers with Mostert’s injury and Sermon’s inactivity. He could end up being the steal of the rookie draft!
