2023 Disco Week 7

TNF Disco Chat Vol 4.VI

The Lowdown - TNF Touchdowns, Not Field Goals On average, it takes a little more than two months for something to become a habit. That’s why we can say that the Dude and EMCO are making a habit of losing, and HOP has an annoying habit of getting lucky. I mean, c’mon: He has the 7th-most points in the league, and is undefeated. Somehow, he hasn’t even had the hardest schedule. That dubious honor goes to last year’s champion, the fatties, whose opponents have scored an average of 154 points against him every week. Some leagues deal with this by playing an extra game against the league median, to even out these bumpy roads. Those leagues are dumb. Fantasy football is supposed to create these inequities. You’re either riding high hurling insults at your lessers, cursing injuries, schedule, or idiotic NFL coaches, or marinating in the coulis of mediocrity that you have created for yourself - because ultimately, no matter how lucky or unlucky you are to this point, it’s the choices you made that led you and your team to the valley of the shadow of death, the path of victory, and everywhere in between.

Week 7

HOP (6-0) vs PITCH (3-3) - 44/56 Significant BYEs: Thielen (HOP), Stefon Diggs (Juan) Perhaps Juan can succeed where I failed, but he’ll have to do it without Stefon Diggs, and HOP has Davante Adams back. I feel like we’re just circling HOP like a room of thugs attacking a hero in a martial arts movie, attacking him one at a time, fruitlessly. Somehow, HOP has done this without Tua the last few weeks, and with Zach Wilson. Somehow, he’s done with Jonathan Taylor out, and with CEH being terrible. Somehow, he’s done it with Robbie Anderson on his team, who he’s starting on TNF with the expected result (currently 0 points). Will Robbie leave HOP hanging just like he left the Panthers’ sideline last week? Signs point to yes. Plus, Juan’s got Dak back for CeeDee Lamb, and for every TD that Davante may catch, he’s got Derek Carr picking up a few points back. Not a bad way to hedge your bets.

Sealed (3-3) vs Fingers (2-4) - 62/38 Significant BYEs: Higbee (Fingers) I’ll give you two words that explain why Fingers only has two wins: Russell. Wilson. Apparently, he has a million injuries, but you still don’t want to trust him as your second QB. However, it’s the only advantage he has against Sealed, who’s starting someone about as good as Olamide Zaccheaus in his SF spot. By the way, Sealed has a superstar on his team who has the most receptions on his team: If you guessed that it’s Zay Jones, you’d be correct. Here’s hoping the Jags find their way and bring Geno, Ekeler, Pollard, and JuJu to victory for Sealed. Just kidding: Kelce is the only reason his team is 3-3. For Fingers, it’s easy to see where his team went wrong: Keenan Allen hasn’t played since week one, Robert Woods hasn’t blossomed in Tennessee, and Derrick Henry hasn’t really gone full yeti mode all that much this season. Sure, he’s got Khalil Herbert and recently risen from the grave Gesicki, but he’s gotta hope Latavius Murray off the street overcomes MG3 and Mike Boone to take over the Broncos backfield to make up ground this week.

DIP (4-2) vs Double (4-2) - 66/34 Significant BYEs: Josh Allen (DIP), Jalen Hurts (DIP), Gabe Davis (DIP), Dallas Goedert (DIP), Miles Sanders (Double) In what is quite possibly the biggest BYE week gap this week, DIP is trying to take on Double without his top 2 QBs, his TE1, and one of his best WRs. It’s not looking good for him, with Goff taking at least 3 sacks from the DAL pass rush, and Heinicke trying to fight against the angry hornets nest that is GB coming off their loss. Daniel Bellinger fills in at TE, while Jerry Jeudy continues to fill a flex spot for him, like a clump of hair clogging a bathtub. Meanwhile, Double’s feeling pretty good, despite James Robinson losing touches to ETN. Tyreek’s been amazing, and is getting Tua back. Godwin’s healthy again. Amari Cooper’s performed in CLE. Jakobi Meyers has been secretly solid in NE with (zap zap!) Bailey. And Kyler Murray might just figure it out tonight, even though it’s halfway through the 3rd quarter and he only has 10 points. Woof. Well, at least he has Joe Burrow. Way to beat a man when he’s down, Double - if, that is, you can manage it.

Babe (2-4) vs EMCO (1-5) – 73/27 Significant BYEs: Kupp (EMCO), AJB (Babe) Both teams have WR1s on BYE, but… you know how when Brady loses a game he was favored to win, he always comes back and crushes fools the next week? I feel like Babe is going to bring the pain this week against EMCO. He’s got Trevor Lawrence, who’s looked 100% better without Urban Meyer. He’s got Lamar Jackson and Mark Andrews, one of the league’s premiere stacks, and he’s got David Njoku, a surprisingly consistent choice for FLEX over in Cleveland. Sure, Dobbins hasn’t really broken out yet, and Mixon and Montgomery are ho-hum, but he’s got some weapons. For EMCO, she finally has Dak back, but unlike the Cowboys, she’s 1-5 not 4-2 without her star QB. Speaking of star QBs, Justin Fields has gone nova, but in, like, a bad way - like he was a star but he exploded and is terrible, and she still has to start him. She went heavy into the Bears too, and has Kmet as well, who she also might have to start. And Elijah Moore, who wants a trade, and is complaining on Twitter that he’s not getting targets. Overall, EMCO’s flex spots are a barren wasteland, a skid row of fantasy talent. Dontrell Hilliar and Parris Campbell stare sunken-eyed from their fantasy lean-tos, begging you to take another chance on them. Don’t do it!

The Dude (1-5) vs Pitch (3-3) – 58/42 Significant BYEs: Cousins (The Dude), Justin Jefferson (The Dude), DeVonta Smith (Pitch) Another week, another favored matchup for The Dude, even with one of his QBs and his WR1 resting up this week. How is this man 1-5 with Saquon? With Hockenson? With Ken Walker III?! Josh Jacobs? Sure, Aaron Jones has sucked this year, but he picked up Eno Benjamin. Cousins and Tannehill aren’t great QB options, but there’s plenty of roster upside there. For Pitch, DK has been awesome with Geno, Brady has not been classic Brady, CMC has been great, but not amazing, and Kyle Pitts has been a huge disappointment, despite his lone TD last week. Kittle, too, hasn’t really stepped up. Some huge misses at TE haven’t stopped her from reaching 0.500, and even though the algorithms don’t favor her, we all know that The Dude is fated to lose to another blow-up week from his opponent. You’re welcome, Pitch.

POP (3-3) vs McLovin (4-2) – 73/27 Significant BYEs: Dalvin Cook (McLovin), Matthew Stafford (McLovin) Mercy! POP finally has Hopkins back, and it’s happening on a week when I don’t have my RB1 or QB1. Sure, Stafford’s been awful, but he’s way better than Mike Boone in my SF spot, which is who I’ve been forced to pivot to. I’m facing Mahomes and Rodgers, Kamara and Rhamondre, and Waddle/Diontae/DeAndre. If Najee had lived up to his preseason hype, then POP’s team would be a juggernaut, instead, it’s more like the drunk Pikachu avatar that he’s chosen for this season: Unevolved, intoxicated, but still pretty shocking. If Breece and Ja’Marr can score 6 TDs for me this week, that’d be great. Fun Fact: I’ve invested heavily in Chases: Ja’Marr, Claypool, and Edmonds. If any of you have any more Chases to chase, I’m down to trade. I need all the help I can get.
