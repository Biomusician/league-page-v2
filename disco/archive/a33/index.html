2021 Disco Week 14

Disco Chat Vol 3.XIV

The Lowdown Fantasy is Dumb Taysom Hill, Tom Brady, David Montgomery, and Jonathan Taylor played crucial roles for those of us still in the playoff hunt. On the flip side, Saquon Barkley, Ezekiel Elliott, Darrell Henderson, Boston Scott, and Tyreek Hill definitely hurt us (and not for the last time!). Here’s the deal: If you’re HOP, Juan, or Pitch, you’re playing spoiler for your matchup this week. Playoffs are not on the table for you, and you’re just trying not to get last. If you’re DIP, the Dude, or Fingers, then you’re still fighting for a playoff spot, kind of. DIP has to win this week to have a chance, and he also has to score 80.24 points more than Fingers if Fingers loses, and 108.38 more points than the Dude if the Dude loses. Basically, DIP’s out of the playoffs as well unless there’s a miracle. Everyone else? Welp, Sealed, Babe, EMCO, and me are guaranteed playoff spots. I’m guaranteed a first-round BYE. If EMCO loses and Babe outscores her by 230.94 poinst, he can lock up a first-round BYE. Probably not likely. Even less likely is that Sealed outscores her by 429.16 points to close the gap. So go all out this week, but know that it probably doesn’t affect your chances of playoffs, just your seeding into the toilet bowl and championship for 2021. Get your name on a trophy that I have yet to order, or the Disco Downbeat award, which I have yet to mail to Pitch. I’m two vodka cokes into the evening, but here goes the rollup. Enjoy, y’all.

Matchup Roundup

The Dude (7-6) vs HOP (5-8) 70/30 The Dude BYE/INJ: NSTR HOP BYE/INJ: Jalen Hurts, Michael Pittman Give up already, Dude. CMC is out for the season. Why burn an IR slot on him? This is a match for pride more than anything, as HOP is out of the playoffs, and The Dude’s in. Still, there’s a chance for HOP to climb closer to a 0.500 win rate with a win against one of the league’s early championship favorites. Still, unless Lamar Jackson comes out strong for HOP, and Dak puts up another stinker for The Dude, it’s going to be an uphill battle. Cooper Kupp, CeeDee Lamb, and George Kittle are all formidable obstacles for HOP to overcome, and strong weapons for the Dude. Their FLEX options are all terrible though: Emmanuel Sanders, Higbee, Kenny G, Fant, and Laviska are all on the table, and HOP doesn’t have a QB2 to start in his SF spot, so there’s that… Fingers (7-6) vs Juan (3-10) 62/38 Fingers BYE/INJ: NSTR Juan BYE/INJ: Waddle, Miles Sanders, Adam Thielen It’s an understatement at this point, but Juan’s team are underdogs against Fingers’ puppets. Just like the rest of the year, it has to do with him being incredibly unlucky - Adam Thielen is out, and his Waddle/Sanders combo is on BYE. He’d be feeling the pain if he were in the playoff hunt, but as it is, he’s got a fine chance to play spoiler with his Rodgers/Adams stack, and DeAndre Hopkins. Those three can be week winners on their own, and the ragtag crew that Fingers has at RB with Josh Jacobs and D’Onta Foreman probably won’t cut it, even alongside boom/bust Mike Evans and Marquise Brown. Also, just like the Dude, Fingers is holding onto Derrick Henry for no reason. EMCO (9-4) vs DIP (6-7) 60/40 EMCO BYE/INJ: JT, Gesicki DIP BYE/INJ: Tua, Damien Harris, Corey Davis Now it’s officially a trend: DIP is holding onto Chris Carson, even though he’s been declared out for the season. Clearly a sign that he’s already given up. Against the top team by points scored, can we blame him? EMCO is favored against DIP’s team even with her best RB out on BYE, resting up for EMCO first BYE next week. Unless Patrick Mahomes or the Hock go off, then EMCO’s got this game on lock with Mixon, Fournette, and Chubb as top RB options even without JT. It’s actually gross how much depth she has at running back, while players like DIP are forced to start Devonta Freeman and Alexander Mattison. She even has AJ Dillon, who is an RB2/3 this week against a Bears team that has seen better days on run defense. Again, this game has potential playoff implications for DIP, so he’s trying to maximize his point total, starting boom/bust options over low/medium floor players. Unfortunately, Allen Robinson, Zach Wilson, Rashod Bateman, Rondale Moore, and Kadarius Toney are unlikely to be the week winning plays that DIP needs to secure a spot in the playoffs, and make him safe from the Disco Downbeat this year. Sealed (8-5) vs McLovin (11-2) 51/49 Sealed BYE/INJ: NSTR McLovin BYE/INJ: Wentz, Rhamondre, Ridley This one is going to be close, especially if Eli “Missile” Mitchell is unavailable for me with knee and concussion concerns. With MGIII out, Sealed has a top option at RB with Javonte Williams, and he also has Tyreek and DJ Moore at WR, which are positional advantages over Lockett and Cooks for me. Flexing Dalvin Cook doesn’t hurt for Sealed either, and he has Claypool and Keenan available as well. While Josh Allen is a potential week winner for me, you can’t dismiss Russell Wilson’s chance at resurgence against Houston this week, and my Najee against his Cordarelle (revenge game, since I traded him away for a broken Mike Williams) doesn’t feel like a lock, since Patterson is PFF’s highest-graded pass catching RB through week 13. Pretty good for an RB in his 30s, right? Anyway, this matchup doesn’t matter for playoff BYE/making it considerations, but damn if I’d feel a lot better beating a team that has a chance to make it to/beat me in the championships. Babe (8-5) vs Pitch (1-12) 80/20 Babe BYE/INJ: Mac Jones, AB Pitch BYE/INJ: Her entire bench “Hello? JAG? Yeah, I’d like to report a case of domestic abuse. I have a playoff team about to beat the shit out of a team that was last year’s worst team, and looks like they can’t defend themselves against consecutive last-place finishes. It could get ugly.” Sorry Pitch. Unless Kyler, Zeke, Tee, Gronk, and your other usually-non-factor players go off, you’re not going to win against Ekeler, Kamara, J-Jeff, and Babe’s lineup. We’re all rooting for you, because who doesn’t like to root for the underdog? It’s going to be tough sledding for you though, as it has been all season. Don’t worry though - it’s definitely because you’ve had the worst luck, not because you’re bad at FF. We definitely want you back next year, along with your $20 entry fee. Plus, then I won’t have to mail the Disco Downbeat award to a different person this year - I’ll just mail it to you after you “win” the toilet bowl!
