Week 5: Post 1 - The 1st of Few

Your favorite formerly-weekly fantasy football newsletters are back! I am settled at HAF A5/7 Futures at the five-sided funhouse here in northern Virginia, the family is settled, the house is unpacked (lie), and as I wait for my SIPR and JWICS to clear (probably post-government shutdown at this point), I have time to bang out some mildly-entertaining content for y'all to enjoy - or if not enjoy, at least read while you're bored. Having Sleeper access at work is a beautiful thing y'all. Without further ado…

Juan (3-5) vs. Babe (2-6) Odds: 54/56 The Fatties have kept their name constant during our six-year league history, just like Dak has continued to perform as a fantasy QB (injuries notwithstanding), despite the loss of CeeDee. Babe'll be looking for him to continue to put up points against the Jets, after somehow tying up the vaunted Packers defense in week 4. Apart from that, he's got rookie JJ talents Jeanty and Judkins, ARSB, a hopeful Tre McMillan, and the dawg Tyler Warren putting up points for his team. And if the Cardinals can figure out how to make MHJ catch passes, maybe he'll contribute too.

On Juan's side of the ball, he'll be rooting for all Dem Boys TDs to come on the ground through Javonte or Blue, despite owning neither player. Josh Allen with his rushing upside should provide relative value vs. Dak, and Cook and Pollard probably won't keep up with Fatties' J&J combo, but maybe BTJ and Devonta Smith can finally deliver on their draft capital and move the needle for his team in week 5. Both teams are pretty full-up on players this week, so it should be a relatively even showing. Maybe a player like Tre Tucker will put up a Darren Waller-like performance and score two random TDs that swing the matchup one way or another. Notable BYEs: Juan: Full-up Babe: Aaron Rodgers

EMCO (3-5) vs. Double (3-5) Odds: 61/39 Double had a plan this year before injuries to Joe Burrow and George Kittle punched him in the face, knocking out his QB1 and TE1. Browning browned his pants the last two weeks, but he was QB14 his first start week 2 with a 70% snap percentage, so Double will be hoping he more than doubles his recent scores of 7.9 and 5.4 fantasy points. While EMCO has the advantage at QB and RB (Gibbs and Kamara vs. Henry and Montgomery, who both disappeared from their offenses week 4), Double has depth at WR and TE (Ferguson surprise TE1!). You may be asking why I didn't include Chase Brown for double in the RB matchup above, and that's because Chase Brown is a fantasy RB3 now that Burrow's down, and has put up scores of 7.5, 4.0, and 8.6. If your fantasy scores are lower than my kids' ages (all <12), we have problems my dude. EMCO's hoping Mark Andrews can find some old man energy and return to form to contribute meaningfully for her team, hopefully with a TD. Same for fellow old man Hopkins, who seems to have lost a step in Baltimore, and may be without QB Lamar Jackson this week. EMCO's hopes hang on Lamar coming back this week; otherwise she'll be rooting for backup QB Cooper Rush, and let me tell you as a former Cowboys fan who watched Rush rush when Dak was out last year - that's not a promising prospect. Notable BYEs: EMCO: Full-up. Jayden Reed would be notable if he weren't on IR. Double: Penix

The Dude (6-2) vs. DIP (7-1) Odds: 25/75 Battle of the Ds! I putting the curse of favorable odds on a 7-1 DIP team, who has had easy (George) Pickens of his opponents through the first four weeks, on the backs of performances from Bill (Jacory Croskey-Merritt), Ja'Marr Chase (not last week), Emeka Egbuka, and Omarion Hampton. CMC is somehow still healthy, despite everyone around him falling into injury, and Daniel Jones has been surprisingly decent as his QB1. Will the walls come crashing down on him, or will he continue to cause the managers of Anthony Richardson (e.g. The Dude) to wail in despair? Against a surprisingly effective Raiders defense, this could be the week of disaster for the Colts. While DIP has the advantage at RB, Puka and J-Jeff are clear winners vs. Pickens and Egbuka, while Courtland Sutton provides weekly TD upside for The Dude in the flex position. And while Trey McBride for DIP went significantly higher than Darren Waller (undrafted), it's hard to beat two touchdowns from a retired rapper. Zay Flowers and Cooper Kupp round out a deep WR bench for The Dude that may provide the extra kick needed to secure a week 5 victory against the league powerhouse. Notable BYEs: The Dude: Drake London. Woof. DIP: #4 TE Tucker Kraft. Kaleb Johnson I guess. Just kidding he has 2.1 points on the season.

HOP (6-2) vs. Pop (5-3) Odds: 50/50 Everyone's favorite Dr. Suess matchup continues and by golly it's gonna be close, especially after Pop lost both Nabers and Tyreek in week 4, and his WR aren't complete Boutte, but they're close. Deebo and Ladd have underperformed expectations, and Diggs isn't the same player he used to be. Still, he's got the stack in NE with Drake Maye as his QB1, with Jayden Daniels providing rushing upside in his other QB slot. You think I hate Pop's receivers, but that's because YYY is starting AJB and Jacobi Meyers, who are fine I guess. But AJB just turned 9 targets into 2 receptions for 7 yards and 1.7 fantasy points, so you don't feel great about it. And if Diggs has lost a step, so has Kelce, whether due to old age or Taylor Swift vampiring his energy. Hopefully he can shake it off, because we know all too well how a cruel summer without blank space, full of a love story, can impact one's playing style and prevent a TE from achieving his wildest dreams. Everything else on his team is uninteresting but startable, kind of like the Chief's receiving corps without Rashee Rice and Xavier Worthy. Notable BYEs: HOP: Bijan Pop: Pitts and Swift

Fingers (3-5) vs. Sealed (3-5) Odds: 15/85 Have you ever had a nightmare where you showed up to work without a hat, pants, or OCP top? Well, that nightmare is coming true for Finger this week, as he can't even field a full lineup. He's like the 49ers of teams. The CA high speed rail of teams. The bridge to nowhere of teams. The Project Habakkuk or Palace of the Soviets of teams. I could go on, but just look at the number of players he has on BYE below and you'll see why my faith in his team is lower than my faith in congress to pass a budget. He better hope Lamar Jackson plays. Meanwhile, Sealed's team is largely intact, minus Rome Odunze, most definitely The Dude in his offense. Fortunately, he's got a high quality backup in Malik Washington or Tryo Franklin - well, let's go with "startable" vs. high quality. Sam Darnold has been a surprise blessing for this team, and he'll look to lead the Seahawks against the Bucs on Sunday, just like Sealed is hoping that Sam LaPorta will come out of his recent slump and be the TE1 we all know he can be, like he was two years ago. Notable BYEs: Fingers: Jordan Love, Josh Jacobs, Luther Burden, Matthew Golden, DJ Moore, Colston Loveland Sealed: Rome Odunze

McLovin (5-3) vs. Pitch (2-6) Odds: 75/25 I'm really setting myself up for failure with those odds, but it's not without reason. Pitch, I love your team - from its creative name (HMaWC), to your mix of young players (Skattebo, Henderson, Bech) and old ones (Chubb, Flacco, and all your TEs). Hunter Henry has been surprisingly impressive thus far and is currently the top TE after four weeks of football, just like all the experts predicted for this NE offense. Still, you may be flexing Njoku and Strange this week (and my goodness you have another TE on BYE in Njoku), and starting Dillon Gabriel at QB (When will Sanders get a chance in CLE?!). My expectations are tempered, but my fear of an upset is real. So to even the karma, I wish you great luck and hope your team wins. You and former Brown Baker Mayfield can do it! Notable BYEs: Pitch: Romeo Doubs, Caleb Williams McLovin: DK Metcalf, Jaylen Warren
