2023 Disco Week 9

Disco Chat Vol 4.VIII

The Lowdown - 10(ish) Things I Hate About You And Maybe a Nice Thing Holy buckets, we’ve got six teams on BYE, and some of your teams look like the Panthers and the Texans smashed together, eviscerated, chewed up, and spit out. Some of you don’t even have complete starting rosters, while others do in name only. Those of us starting Sam Ehlinger are doing it for the same reason people resort to cannibalism or drink seawater in survival situations - we feel like we have no other choice. It’s these lose/lose situations that separate the losers from the even worse losers; you may be the Melvin Gordon of fantasy football, but at least you’re not the ghost of AJ Green. In any case, your team looks as good as can be expected coming out of the gauntlet of byes and injuries, which for some of you is about as good as Russell Wilson’s looked this year. But hey, keep doing those fantasy high knees, and we’ll continue to mock you behind your back.

Week H8 9

HOP (6-2) vs Sealed (4-4) 48/52 Hop BYEs: Deebo Sealed BYEs: Pollard, Slayton Hop, I hate that your team is somehow 6-2 with your roster, that you’re starting Taysom Hill at TE, and that Marquise Goodwin and Sam Ehlinger are potential options for you as well. You probably hate how average JT has been this year, but at least you have Tua in your QB1 spot. Sealed, I hate that Caleb Huntley may make a spot start for you at RB, that you may have to turn to Jameis Winston or an even worse option for your SF spot, and that Olamide Zaccheaus is once again cracking into your starting lineup like a streak of mold on mozzarella. Again, you lucked out on QB this year, and Geno Smith is somehow amazing, as if the last 8 years didn’t even happen, where his best annual QB fantasy finish was 20th.

Juan (4-4) vs EMCO (2-6) - 75/25 Juan BYEs: CeeDee, Aiyuk, Pickens EMCO BYEs: Dak, Chubb Juan, I hate that you may be forced to flex a mediocre TE in Tyler Conklin, because your bench depth is terrible. I hate that you’re carrying Kenny G and DeSean Jackson on your roster, raising its average age by at least 5 years. Let it go man - they will never be fantasy relevant again. I hate that you still think OBJ is going to get signed somewhere and be relevant after his ACL injury. But it’s not all doom and gloom for you, especially not with Justin Herbert maybe getting Keenan back eventually, Dameon Pierce being good even on a terrible team, ETN stepping into his own, and Rondale Moore stepping in behind DHop in Arizona. EMCO, I hate that Mitch Trubisky is still on your roster somehow after being replaced by Pickett, and that two of your flex options are backups: Alexander Mattison and Dontrell Hilliard? Ew. But I guess that’s what happens when your team’s namesake goes on BYE and you’re forced to start Devin Singletary and AJ Dillon too. Cooper Kupp and Zach Ertz are somehow your bright spots, along with Justin Fields, who appears to have turned the corner. But KJ Osborn? Parris Campbell? What is your team this week?!

Babe (3-5) vs Fingers (3-5) - 66/34 Babe BYEs: Gallup, Njoku Fingers BYEs: Russ, Pickett, Latavius Murray Babe, are you seriously considering starting Marvin and Julio Jones alongside Marquez Callaway? I guess when your other options are Jarvis Landry and Logan Thomas, you have to. If Mark Andrews doesn’t play this week, you’re completely screwed at TE. I also hate that you may have to play Drake London, since Arthur Smith doesn’t believe in throwing the football in ATL. Your QBs are a bright spot, with Lamar and Trevor showing solidly, and you have AJB too. For Fingers? Where do I start? Russell. Wilson. Matt Ryan. Kenny Pickett. You have three nigh-unusable QBs on your roster doing nothing for you. You’re starting the ghost of Robert Woods in TEN, maybe Keenan Allen if he plays, and if he doesn’t, then someone like Sammy Watkins or Corey Davis. Yuck. Gesicki or Juwan Johnson? No thanks. You don’t have a 2nd QB, so you’re superflexing someone gross as well. Not a great week for you, and an uphill battle. Still, Derrick Henry cures many ills, so maybe you have a chance.

The Dude (3-5) vs POP (5-3) – 58/42 The Dude BYEs: Saquon POP BYEs: Najee, Diontae J, Pat Freiermuth The Dude, I hate that Saquon is on BYE and you’re still somehow favored to win, after lucking into KW3 being super-relevant, Higgins benefiting from the Chase injury, GB remembering how to use Aaron Jones, and Josh Jacobs having a career year. I hate that you either lucked into all these players outperforming ADP or that you were right on so many of your calls. Ryan Tannehill in your SF isn’t great, but it’s far from the worst we’ve seen this week. But Hunter Henry? Zack Moss? What are they doing for you? Meanwhile, we’ve got POP holding onto Eli Mitchell for no reason, Noah Brown for who knows why, and Deshaun Watson (boo!). Najee Harris, meanwhile, hasn’t scored more than 12 fantasy points this year, so maybe it’s a blessing that he’s on BYE forcing you to start Rhamondre and Samaje. I hate that you lucked into D’Onta Foreman being somehow good on the Panthers. Mahomes, Kamara, DeAndre, Waddle - they almost make up for all of the rest.

DIP (4-4) vs Me (4-4) – 85/15 DIP BYEs: MG3, Zeke, Jeudy, Wan’Dale My BYEs: Danny Dimes, Courtland Sutton Finally, a spot for me to vent about how I’ve lost Trey Lance, Breece Hall, and Ja’Marr Chase. My team is basically the Black Knight from Monty Python at this point, and it’s just depressing that I’m contemplating starting PJ Walker and Khalil Shakir, and stashing Kyren Williams hoping that he is relevant coming off his injury. The only bright spots on my team are Amon-Ra and Dalvin Cook, and they’re floaties on the fat man wearing concrete boots that is my team. DIP, meanwhile, gets Josh Allen and Jalen Hurts. He should hate to start Darrell Henderson, because it seems like the Rams do, and also feel gross for rostering the shell of MG3, but I guess it’s worth seeing how the backfield shakes out with Chase Edmonds making an appearance. But, if you’re losing playing time to Latavius Murray, it’s probably not a good sign. Anyway, I’m probably boned this week.

Double (6-2) vs Pitch (4-4) – 85/15 Double BYEs: Jimmy G, Brissett, Amari Cooper Pitch BYEs: CMC, K-Hunt, DPJ, Kittle, Harrison Bryant Is there anyone on Pitch’s team not on BYE? Her fantasy team may as well be, because it looks like Double is walking off with a win here. Somehow, even without all her TEs, Pitch still has Pitts to start, but she’s hoping that he scores again because ATL throws like 10 passes a game. I also hate that you’re starting Pacheco and McKissic and your RB1/2, and that AJ Green is making an appearance in your roster. Halloween’s over, and I’m terrified of your roster. Tom Brady? Marcus Mariota? Mariota/Pitts stack has got to be one of the worst. My favorite part of your team is CMC and he’s on BYE with the Niners. Double, meanwhile, is rocking Tyreek and Kyler, and has Godwin to fill in for Amari Cooper as his WR2. James Robinson and Hunter Renfrow, however, are terrible options that you’ve been forced to start. Also, why are you holding Desmond Ridder? Even when he plays he only throws 13 pass attempts/10 completions. Cam Akers? Donezo. Will Dissly? Irrelevant. Find some upside guys on the waiver wire.
