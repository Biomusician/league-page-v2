2021 Disco Week 3

Disco Chat Vol 3.III 3-3! It’s TTP Time!

The Lowdown Having Fun Yet? If one week of confirmation bias wasn’t enough, two weeks is basically enough to cement your perspective on players. George Kittle? Bust. OBJ? Old and broken and irrelevant? Saquon? Samesies. Rondale Moore? Better than Justin Jefferson, DK Metcalf, and Calvin Ridley combined. Hope you have one of the early moonshots, and they continue their meteoric rise. If it’s anything like officer promotions, then the first quarter of their season will probably define the rest of it. Speaking of officers, PITCH or Babe DM me your Tinker address so I can send you the loser trophy. Ditto the Dude, so I can send you a freshly-minted winner’s trophy.

Matchup Roundup Yeehaw Battle Royale: McLovin (2-0) vs DIP (2-0) 55/45 Only one team can emerge undefeated. Will it be DIP, led by Mahomes, Diggs, and the Hock? Or will it be McLovin, led by Josh Allen, Aaron Jones, and Tyler Lockett? A lot of it will depend on whether Aaron Jones is more like week 1 or 2, whether Antonion Gibson can escape the touch gremlin JD McKissick, and whether the Hock continues to ball out. Damien Harris is shaping up to be a good flex option for DIPO, as has Rondale Moore. Robby Anderson needs to take a step forward from his first two weeks, but this is the week to do it against Houston. For McLovin, Calvin Ridley needs to show out against New York, Lockett needs to continue his ridiculous start against a tough Vikings DST, and Robert Tonyan needs to catch a TD from Rodgers. Elijah Mitchell, if he plays, could provide a safe floor for McLovin’s FLEX4 option. This one’s anyone’s game! HOP (0-2) vs Juan (0-2) 53/47 Another close matchup, and a story of differing positional advantages. For HOP, Saquon and CEH are theoretically better options than Miles Sanders and James Robinson. If Saquon is healthy, this is his week against a soft Falcons DST. However, you can’t argue that Juan has the advantage in WR: Davante and DeAndre against DK Metcalf and Chris Godwin. Even though Godwin seems to have stepped into the WR1 role on the TB team, Juan’s rolling with two elite options. On the QB side, I’d rather have the rushing upside of Lamar Jackson than the pocket presence of Aaron Rodgers. If Juan wins, it’s because Sterling Shepard continues to be Daniel Jones’ go-to, Mooney takes a step forward, and Jaylen Waddle produced without Tua. If HOP wins, it’s because the Packers have another bad game, Kenny G takes a step forward against a soft ATL defense, or Michael Pittman manages to produce with (maybe) a backup QB, since Carson Wentz has ankles of aluminum foil. The Dude (2-0) vs Fingers (1-1) 52/48 Last year’s champion continues to impress despite all the trash talking I’ve been doing in these newsletters and in chat. It’s probably because his team was drafted for him by Glory. Anyway, he’s got the advantage again this week against Fingers, led by an intimidating Dak/CeeDee stack and CMC. Sorry Fingers: Brady is good, and Derrick Henry is the king, but Gaskin/Cooper/Evans probably aren’t going to get it done unless you get lucky. Additionally, it seems that Daniel Jones has taken a step forward for the Dude, and Matt Ryan still hasn’t found his stride without Julio Jones. If Kelce continues to be Kelce, and Kittle continues to be first two week of 2021 Kittle, then Fingers wins this easy. Babe (0-2) vs EMCO (1-1) 51/49 The FLEX options for EMCO are much better than Babe’s, but Babe has better starters. For Babe, Kamara needs to improve on his first two performances to be relevant, but historically he’s outperformed Nick Chubb or Jonathan Taylor. Ekeler is solid in the receiving game, and also stacks up well against EMCO’s options. Justin Jefferson is another star who has underperformed, but should be better than A-Rob or Deebo for EMCO. Deebo is the NFL’s leading receiver in terms of yards, and a great later-round snag. Finally, I’ll take Andrews over GeSICKi any day. Fatties has a slight advantage, but only if his starters perform. Sealed (1-1) vs PITCH (1-1) 61/39 In another difference-making game, Sealed is looking for Russ to cook against a tough Vikings DST matchup. Meanwhile, Dalvin Cook is a little injured, in a repeat of 2020/2019 campaign. Javonte Williams continues to share time with MGIII, but he has two studs in Tyreek and Keenan Allen. Kyle Pitts is a bold start, and all of his flex WRs are interesting options: DJ Moore, Mike Williams, Chase Claypool, and DJ Chark. For PITCH, she’s hoping Kyler Murray continues his impressive run, and continues to carry her to victory opposite Zeke, who’s struggled as of late. AJB, similarly, has yet to find his stride opposite Julio Jones. Darren Waller, however, has been solid. Gronk, meanwhile, has been amazing. The Vaqueros have the advantage, but PITCH still has a chance.
