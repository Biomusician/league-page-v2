Disco Chat Vol 5.XVI

The Lowdown Timeskip Edition “I promise I’ve been working just as hard as your MDS’ program offices.” -McLovin

Week 16

EMCO vs Juan Hoo boy. Juan is hoping that Josh Allen can throw more than 7 passes this week, and that Dak scores more than 7 fantasy points after an absolute drubbing by Buffalo last week. Even so, Juan’s been carried by his QBs for most of the year, and that could be the deciding factor this week. For RBs, EMCO’s got this on lock with Mostert and Henry vs Gibbs. She’ll also be rooting against her Jets’ Breece Hall, since Juan’s fielding him in his RB2 slot. In the FLEX spots, she somehow has ETN and Saquon (!) to make up for Jerry Jeudy and Devin Singletary. Those are against Juan’s Warren, “I don’t block anymore” Pickens, Goedert (?), and maybe Roschon? Who knows. It’s gross at the end of Juan’s bench. Maybe we see a Rico Dowdle and his team will live up to their name.

Babe vs McLovin Let’s be honest. My QB strategy hasn’t worked out. Burrow’s out. Lawrence meh. Kyler play calling has been doody (CoD). But that’s fine! I have Nick Mullens, Aidan O’Connell, and Jake Browning! My “Avengers at home” or Chinese knockoff QBs will be facing Lamar Jackson and maybe CJ Stroud. No backup for Babe. Will Josh Jacobs play for my team this week? Do I even want him to? Which version of DJ Moore and the Bears will we get this week? Which version of “ mostly always scoring a TD” Sutton, Jakobi “bleah” Meyers, and “Fumbles” Gibson (maybe Robinson still injured?) will I get? Meanwhile, the fatties really have been corn-fed this season. Lamar may suck some weeks, sure, but he could still blow it up against SF. Or not, maybe (hopefully). JT may be back for Babe. Drake London may as well have been deleted by Arthur Smith. Taysom Hill had 1 target last week. KWIII is elite when healthy. Lots of things that may tilt the scales here, but AJB, JT, and Lamar swing things to Babe and his barnyard assembly.

Sealed vs Double Sealed, I’m sorry. Brocky Purdy, George Kittle, and Deebo Samuel need to combine for 6 TDs for you to win this week. Double’s options are unlimited with Russell Wilson leading the better Cook (James, not Sealed’s Dalvin) and Justin Jefferson into battle. He also has Aiyuk, so you can bet that Sealed will be seething whenever Purdy throws it to him. I don’t know what’s grosser: Sealed’s FLEX options, or a 4ft-long drain-hair monster: Doubs, Alec Pierce, Cade Otton, Parker Washington (who?!) against players on Double’s team that are actually talked about in fantasy circles. If this were a 32-team league, I’d love Sealed’s team. Maybe if Sealed had released any of his players currently on IR, he’d be able to start players you’d actually heard of. He’s lazing his way to the bottom of the toilet bowl; someone give him a wakeup/wellness call!

Pitch vs POP Pitch could really use another White Claw this week to take the pain away from a one-sided matchup. Will she repeat her 2020 last-place performance and join HOP as a 2-time loser? Or will Baker Mayfield repeat last week’s performance with another perfect passer rating and carry her to victory against a lagging Patrick Mahomes and Geno Smith on POP’s side? It doesn’t feel great to have to perform with Najee Harris in your RB stable against Kyren Williams and James Conner. Amari Cooper and Chris Olave against Garret Wilson and Zay Flowers? That’s like the worst WR matchup of the week. FLEX matchups are evenly split, with Ekeler and Jayden Reed winning out for POP against Pitch’s Jerome Ford and Tyjae Spears. Pitch flips the script with JSN and Allgeier against Elijah Moore and Marquise Brown, neither of which has caught a pass this year; you don’t need to check to see if that’s true - POP feels it in his soul. Good luck avoiding last place!

Draft Rundown - First 10 Rounds No, this isn’t a desperate attempt to make up for any weeks I may or may not have missed. I went through the first 10 rounds of the draft this year, and by applying strict criteria for busts/steals (my own thoughts and feelings), I came out with the following rankings: 1. Me. I’m totally objective, and amazing for only drafting 1 bust (Burrow) and 2 steals (Kamara in R8 and Sutton in R10) 2. EMCO. Only two busts: McLaurin and Jeudy. 3. HOP: Two busts (Bijan and Kadarius Toney) and 1 steal (Rachaad White in R8) 4. Juan: Two busts (Breece and Mattison) and two steals: Dak in R3 and Gibbs in R4. 5. DIP: 3 busts: Danny Dimes, Waller, and A-Rich. 6. Double. Busts: Chubb, Sanders, Carr. Steals: James Cook in R9. 7. The Dude: Three busts (Rodgers, Dillon, and JSN) and 2 steals: Ceedee in R2, and Mike Evans the perpetual in R8. 8. Sealed: Four busts (Herbert, Bryce Young, Cam Akers, and Dalvin Cook) and 1 steal: Brock Purdy in R9. 9. Babe: Four busts (JT, Pitts, London, and D. Swift) and 1 steal (CJ Stroud in R8). Going all-in on the Falcons stars (Bijan doesn’t count, according to Arthur) didn’t work out. 10. POP: An amazing six busts: Mahomes, Ekeler, G. Wilson, Rhamondre, Marquise Brown, and Dotson. No steals in the first 10. 11. Pitch: Another six busts: Deshaun “I’m always injured and never good” Watson, Olave, Najee, Dobbins, QJ, and Mike Thomas. I’m so, so sorry they hurt you. 12. Fingers: The worst draft luck had to befall someone, and this year it was Fingers, with an incredible seven busts and no steals: Kelce, Cousins, Dameon Pierce, Calvin Ridley, Javonte Williams, Mike Williams, and Khalil Herbert. Next time, never draft two Williams; I have it on good authority that that’s what sunk your team’s chances. Better luck next year! Late Round Rundown: I briefly looked over the next 9 rounds, and everyone pretty much sucked, except for the guy below. Honorable mentions: Baker Mayfield in R11 (Pitch), Devon Achane at 12.2 (DIP), Nico Collins at 13.11 (also DIP), Rashee Rice at 13.1 (Double), Sam Howell at 14.11 (POP), the RB2 on the season Raheem Mostert at 14.09 (EMCO), the RB13 on the year by Fingers in round 16.3 (Gus Edwards), the #7 TE Taysom Hill at 16.5 (Babe), #8 TE Jakie Ferg by Juan at 16.10. Nice work mining some diamonds from the shit! Best Draft Steal: The TE1 was drafted at 12.08 by The Dude. He’s Sam LaPorta. What a bargain! Waiver Wire Winner: DIP, who picked up Trey McBride, TE9 on the season, for $3 in week 7 after he was dropped by both EMCO and Fingers. He also picked up WR10 Puka Nacua for $7 prior to week 1. If he wins again this year, it’s mostly because of these two moves. Thanks again to everyone for playing this year - always so fun to get back together with the Primo crew to duke it out for the title! Thanks to those of you who are competitive, those of you who are just here for fun, and those of you who just show up to donate $20 to the pot every year - everyone knows who he or she is. Hope to see you all next year! PS: DIP has the championship belt. We can’t let him repeat.
