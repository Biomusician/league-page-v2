With only two weeks of regular season fantasy left (and one week of BYEs!), the playoff picture has clarified a bit at the top, but it’s still a mad dash for the two first-round BYEs and the bottom seed. It’s conceivable that someone could make the playoffs and win it all with a 5-8 regular season record. It would piss the other 5 of us off, for sure, but that would make the victory all the sweeter. The more unfair our victory is in fantasy, the more we relish it. Do I care that one player scored over 30% of my points? My waiver wire add outscored my opponent’s stud? My nobody randomly exploded? Sure, but mostly just to rub it in my opponent’s face. So keep on keeping on, you fantasy assholes. 9-2 U.S. Trash (Commish) can lock in a first round BYE by going undefeated the next two weeks. Instead, I’m just going to post his responses verbatim, and add some colorful commentary. The Land Sharks are an homage to Oceanside Collegiate Academy (It’s a high school) of Mt Pleasant, SC, the original Land Sharks before Ole Miss got big ideas and decided to co-opt it. The mighty Landsharks are struggling a bit this year, they are a mere 6-2 and the 41st best team in South Carolina (Classic middling fish, small pond) . But they terrorize AA Region 6 and do so proudly (Just like I used to pick on my younger brothers) . Olmsted is the name of the one horse town I hail from in NE Ohio (There are like 32,000 people there. I’m not sure that’s quite enough for one horse). I have been playing FF since 2011, so eight years now (OK boomer), and I have two championships: my first year and my fifth year. A title has eluded me in the last few years, but I usually win the regular season. My keen intellect (he’s the humblest) and strategy of drafting only one quality player at each position only backfires on me in the playoffs (we hope so). The Air Force got me into it, my first league was with my Tyndall class. I bet on New Orleans that year and they had a real good year. I love players that add flavor to the league. In the I-Flight league that was Basic, in this league it is Distro and the Commish (Sorry, Basic; you’ve been replaced). The football and the money keep me engaged, but the banter keeps me interested. When you are at the top, you are of course going to get everyone’s A game. But since I am the Patriots of our league (at 8-3, you’re more like the Bills, Ravens, Packers, or Saints) and play the bottom half twice instead of the top half, I guess everything that is good, right, and holy is my rival. I accept being the empire, as long as I get the ring (of Power). Barring all of that, seeing you lose McLovin is always especially...glorious (Same to you). There are many songs that will describe my year this year.
