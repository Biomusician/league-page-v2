2021 Disco Week 12

Disco Chat Vol 3.XII

The Lowdown Shooting Stars It was a week of revivals and impressive performances. Elijah Moore is finally living up to the preseason hype. Aiyuk looks like he’s out of Shanahan’s doghouse. Miles Sanders returned to an Eagles offense that looks to have established the run, and Cam Newton comes home to Carolina to score 3 TDs. Of course, if you had Herbert, JT, J-Jeff, or Ekeler, you were overjoyed. If you had any Jaguar (first, lol; second, you deserve what you get if you started one of Meyers’ cats), Tannehill, Najee, Zeke, or a number of other “starters,” you were disappointed by all the points left on the table. Maybe everyone was saving room for Thanksgiving this week? In any case, your stars were either shooting, or you wanted to shoot your stars. Whether you’re thanking your lucky ones or seeing them, that’s just fantasy football, and you can’t spell starts without star or arts.

Matchup Roundup

HOP (5-6) vs Juan (2-9) 54/46 HOP BYE/INJ: CEH, Ertz Juan BYE/INJ: DeAndre Hopkins Juan, HOP needs this win to stay relevant. Are you going to roll over like a dead dog and give it to him? There’s certainly a snowball’s chance in Italy of that happening, as his RBs and Adams are superior options to what HOP has on tap. Miles Sanders looks rejuvenated in the new Eagles offense, and James Robinson continues to be one of the only things about the Jags worth talking about in fantasy. Goedert is only slightly better than HOP’s Fant (I don’t want-a Fant-a). For the FLEX options, I much prefer what Juan has, with Renfrow, OBJ, and Landry, up against Ingram (will Kamara be healthy?) Jeff Wilson (who didn’t get much done last Sunday), and MVS (maybe he catches another long TD?). Lamar Jackson, if he can get over his sniffles to play this week, is a better option than ancient Aaron Rodgers, if he can knock off the rust from not playing last week. HOP is hoping that Saquon comes strong this week instead of his lukewarm performance this week; you want your running back putting up more yards than the average human life expectancy, not less. The Dude (6-5) vs Fingers (6-5) 78/22 The Dude BYE/INJ: Chase Edmonds, Darrel Williams, CeeDee Lamb (?) Fingers BYE/INJ: Christian Kirk, AJ Green, Travis Kelce, Derrick Henry If you ever want to know whether someone has given up on the season, check which players they’re continuing to hold long after they’ve become irrelevant. Fingers, we know that you love Derrick Henry and are indebted to him for carrying you through the first half of the season. It’s time to Viking funeral his ass and move on to another flier for your bench. Also, the rest of your bench is in shambles: Carlos Hyde, Jeremy McNickelback, Devin Singleyardtary… it’s a wasteland man. You can’t plant rusty buckets and get corn back; churn through the chaff clogging your roster and rebuild - you still have a chance at the playoffs! For goodness’ sake, you don’t even have a starting TE this week! Better fix it before the Dude drops one of his useless bench cloggers to grab the top bench option. Not that he needs to, since he’s starting Kittle, who’s scored in three games straight. The only positional advantage that Fingers has here is Brady over Dak, and maybe Tyler Boyd and Foreman against Higbee and Engram (ew, starting 3 TEs is gross). Still, that could change if CD Lamb is able to suit up for the Cowboys. This one could be a bloodbath between two 6-5 teams. EMCO (8-3) vs Babe (7-4) 75/25 EMCO BYE/INJ: NSTR (Le’Veon Bell again lol) Babe BYE/INJ: Michael Carter, Adam Trautman (lol) Two weeks in a row for EMCO and most of her starters being available means she is looking to crush against Babe’s team, who just lost Michael Carter and Adam Trautman to longterm injuries. Babe will be praying for another all-star performance from Herbert this week, for another TD from Mark Andrews, and for more great games from Ekeler and Justin Jefferson. Then again, EMCO’s hoping for another smash game from JT, Deebo, and Ja’Marr Chase. Both sides of this could go nuclear, but there’s no MAD in fantasy football - only one of these teams will win, while the other sits in radioactive destitution, gazing with empty eyes at the ruins of their roster, and perhaps their chances of a first-round BYE. Sealed (6-5) vs Pitch (1-10) 71/29 Sealed BYE/INJ: James Conner, DeVante Parker, Tyreek Hill Pitch BYE/INJ: Kyler Murray, Will Fuller V, JuJu You’d think that you would be sweating missing TD machine Conner and Tyreek hill, but this match is still heavily tilted in Sealed’s favor. Have you ever gotten up and thought you were late to work, then realized that there was a holiday and you didn’t have to go? That’s basically what’s happened to Sealed. I was worried that my procrastination in shipping the loser’s trophy to Pitch would mean she wouldn’t keep it very long, but it seems very likely she’ll repeat as the league’s last place performer. Her chances nosedive if AJ Brown isn’t able to play this week after sustaining an injury last week. Also, she’s starting three TEs in Waller, Gronk, and Hunter Henry. Good luck finding one on the wire Fingers! She also doesn’t have a second QB to start, so she’s left relying on someone like Gabe Davis or Cedrick Wilson against Jimmy G for Sealed. What a gross matchup. Dalvin Cook and Cordarrelle fill in the gaps for Sealed, along with Keenan Allen and a recently-revived DJ Moore. Will Cam Newton keep him relevant? Will Kyle Pitts be able to succeed as one of the only options available to the Falcons outside Patterson? We’ll see. McLovin (9-2) vs DIP (5-6) 58/42 McLovin BYE/INJ: Calvin Ridley DIP BYE/INJ: Chris Carson (RIP), Rondale Moore, Julio Jones (washed) The worst thing about my lineup this week is that I can’t start more Bears against DIP for the potential mental damage. Unfortunately, I just can’t bring myself to start Kmet over the Muth, who is coming off another solid game. In the RB and QB arenas, I win: Josh Allen vs Tua, Najee against Antonio Banderas, and D’Andre against the worse NE RB. I’ll be rooting against the Red Rocket since he’s throwing the ball the Darnell Mooney, who’s looking to start in DIP’s FLEX opposite Elijah Mitchell for me (if healthy), or maybe Aiyuk (WR1 ROS). DIP’s banking on CD and Amari being out for the Cowboys so that Michael Gallup scores mad points for him against my Mike Williams, who hasn’t really been awesome since week 5. Finally, I’m starting the better NE RB in Rhamondre Stevenson, so we’ll be rooting for/against the Patriots depending on which RB is leading the current drive. Also, DIP is starting ice-cold Matty as his QB2, while I’ve got the incredibly-talented-how-could-this-backfire Carson Wentz - if the Colts decide to throw any TDs to JT, that is. I’m hoping to crush DIP’s playoff push while cementing a week 1 playoff BYE for myself this week.
