2023 Disco Week 6

Disco Chat Vol 4.V

The Lowdown - Boy Bands Aren’t All NFL Teams Bands of Boys? Relevant Spotify playlist Oh man, it’s NSYNC season now - BYE BYE BYE! The Lions, Raiders, and Titans are all resting on their dubious laurels this week, leaving many of you in the lurch. But don’t worry – you either prepared for this by building depth into your roster, or you’re scrambling on waivers trying to find a halfway startable option, like a trapped fly straining against the spider’s web. It’s these week that separate the men and women from the boy bands, so if you’re saying “I don’t want losing to be the Story of My Life,” or you’re considering The End of the Road, just commit and say “I Swear” to start the best players available, because That’s What Makes You(r team) Beautiful. And winning? Well, I Want It That Way.

Week 5

HOP (5-0) vs PITCH (3-2) - 35/65 Significant BYEs: Davante Adams (HOP) In a battle for the ages against teams that have previously finished last place, somehow the team that Babe built that Pitch is piloting is favored to come out on top. Why? She's got a WR corps and QB stable that outclass HOP's. Imagine HOP trying to arrest someone with a rainbow dildo and a rubber chicken, and that's him trying to win this matchup with Bailey Zappe and Zach Wilson as his QBs. Meanwhile, Pitch has a resurgent DK Metcalf, Olave, the Slim Reaper, and, of course, CMC. Tom Brady's not been great, but he's still better than what HOP's got on tap. Now, imagine HOP trying to arrest someone without handcuffs, a taser, or a gun, and that's him trying to win without Davante Adams (who may be arrested on some light assault on a photographer). Then again, he has potential fantasy bazooka/Swiss Army knife Taysom Hill, so maybe that'll make up for it. Still, it's fun to root against the undefeated teams, and it's high time we stopped police violence. Do it for the people, Pitch - strike when he’s weak!

The Dude (1-4) vs Juan (2-3) - 65/35 Significant BYEs: Derek Carr (Juan), Dameon Pierce (Juan), Tannehill (The Dude), Josh Jacobs (The Dude), TJ Hockenson (The Dude) Yikes. With all the significant pieces missing here in a sub-0.500 matchup, it's like watching two marines having a pillow fight over a box of crayons. Still, The Dude has RB depth to survive here, with Saquon and A-Jones, along with Ken Walker and Eno Benjamin. Damn, The Dude has seen his fantasy stock rise over this last week due to injury, and now his RB room looks 100% better. Meanwhile, Juan is still praying for ETN to break out over James Robinson, for CD Lamb to get Dak back, and for George Pickens to cement himself as the WR1 in Pittsburgh with Pickett. Being Pickett's #1 weapon on an offense like the Steelers' is like being a nerf ball launched by a 5 year-old, but I guess it's better than being Allen Robinson for the Rams, whom Juan may have to start in his SF spot, with all his QBs gone.

POP (2-3) vs Fingers (2-3) - 78/22 Significant BYEs: Derrick Henry (Fingers), Robert Woods (Fingers) POP has unlocked easy mode against Fingers, who's missing his RB1 in Derrick Henry. Meanwhile, he's still got Rhamondre (stonks up), Kamara (kinda yikes tbh), Diontae Johnson (budget Pickens), Najee (gross; another Steeler), and is flexing Aaron Rodgers. Fingers is fighting an uphill battle here with his RB room of Antonio Gibson (kick returner extraordinaire) and Khalil Herbert (the RB2 for the Bears). When you're considering putting Will Dissly in your FLEX or Latavius Murray, you know your roster has some depth issues. Sure, maybe Marquise Brown and Terry McLaurin put up enough points to put you over the top, but with how Arizona and the 'Manders have been looking on offense, I wouldn't count on it. Plus, you've got Russell Wilson as your QB1, who isn't even as good as Geno anymore, is playing hurt, and is definitely limited. “Let’s Ride!” just doesn’t hit the same when you’re riding in on an ass.

DIP (3-2) vs EMCO (1-4) – 58/42 Significant BYEs: Goff (DIP), D’Andre Swift (DIP), Jahan Dotson (EMCO) BYEs have struck DIP harder than EMCO this week in a matchup that will determine playoff trajectory for EMCO after a rough start to the season, and a tough loss to yours truly (sorry-not-sorry!). Still, missing Goff doesn’t feel too bad for DIP when he’s got Josh Allen and Jalen Hurts. It’s like being without your 9mm, but you still have a glock and an SMG. EMCO’s love for the Chubb has really panned out this year, and you’ve got to love him over MGIII and Raheem Mostert, who are somehow DIP’s starters alongside Zeke, who looks longer in the tooth every time we see Pollard break off another long run. DIP has his eggs in the young WR basket, with Pittman, Jeudy (not unlimited), and Mooney (sorry Fields/Bears) alongside veteran Lockett. Meanwhile, EMCO is in the unenviable position of starting Devin Singletary, especially if Conner can’t suit up after getting banged up last week. Elijah Moore may crack her starting lineup, in the same way that a piece of eggshell cracks through an otherwise-appetizing omelet. Also, she’s still leaning on Cooper Rush while she waits for Dak Prescott. Will Cooper Kupp and Mike Williams staunch the bleeding enough to carry her to victory? We’ll see whether she can turn this season around and keep pushing, or whether it’s the beginning of the end.

McLovin (3-2) vs Sealed (3-2) – 54/46 Significant BYEs: ARSB (McLovin), Brandin Cooks (Sealed), Darren Waller (Sealed) The BYE is a blessing for Sealed, as it provides him and the Raiders an opportunity to rest Waller after his hamstring injury last week, and he doesn’t have to consider starting an underperforming Cooks. Meanwhile, I’m missing my sun god Amon-Ra, but that’s fine, because Breece Hall breakout season is here, baby! Him and Dalvin Cook can carry me to victory against Sealed’s Ekeler and Pollard. He’s relying on guys like JuJu (EwEw), Duvernay (Whovernay?) Zay Jones (Jaguar. ‘Nuff said), and Olamide Zaccheaus (wee little man) to get him across the finish line, while I’ve got 2nd-year revelation Ja’Marr Chase, PPR monster Christian Kirk, RB1 in Washington Brian Robinson, RB2 in Denver Mike Boone, and Courtland Sutton, who is getting a million terrible targets from Mr. Limited. Come to think of it, maybe going so heavy into Denver was a bad idea. I’m banking on Travis Kelce NOT catching another 4 TDs this week, otherwise my slim margin of probable victory will quickly erode.

Double (3-2) vs Babe (2-3) – 51/49 Significant BYEs: NSTR Nobody’s missing a stud here, and it’s reflected in the matchup: Two middling teams fighting it out to either return to 0.500 (Double), or reach 0.500 (Babe). This one is going to depend on whose QBs ball out the most. Will it be Lamar Jackson and Trevor Lawrence for the Fatties, or Kyler and Joe for the TDs? Babe’s got the Lamar/Mark Andrews stack as well, so if we get a KC situation of 4 TDs to the TE in Baltimore, it’ll be game over for Double this week. For Double, he’s hoping that James Robinson can hold on to his job with ETN getting more involved in the offense, and that Tyreek Hill has a good game against the Vikings. Fournette’s matchup against Pittsburgh isn’t great, but he and Miles Sanders are great options when compared to the Fatties’ Mixon and Montgomery, who have both underperformed this year, not meeting expectations. Double’s not without disappointments on his team either, has he’s got Cam Akers in his FLEX. But, every cloud has a silver lining, since he also has Jakobi Meyers, who’s been quietly pretty great this year, and Amari Cooper, who’s been solid in Cleveland. Finally, I will never fail to point and laugh when someone may have to start a TE in their flex, and the Fatties may be at that point with David Njoku, who’s been seeing a significant target share with the Browns. Good luck to both contenders!
