The Lowdown Alternatively, the Down-low Week 11 was the 3rd-worst week in the league, with teams averaging only 108.8 points. Hopefully you survived, but if you’re thinking of throwing in the towel, there’s still hope! With only two weeks of regular season fantasy left (and one week of BYEs!), the playoff picture has clarified a bit at the top, but it’s still a mad dash for the two first-round BYEs and the bottom seed. It’s conceivable that someone could make the playoffs and win it all with a 5-8 regular season record. It would piss the other 5 of us off, for sure, but that would make the victory all the sweeter. The more unfair our victory is in fantasy, the more we relish it. Do I care that one player scored over 30% of my points? My waiver wire add outscored my opponent’s stud? My nobody randomly exploded? Sure, but mostly just to rub it in my opponent’s face. So keep on keeping on, you fantasy assholes. 9-2 Browns FTW (PITCH) only has to win 1 more week to lock in a first round BYE. 7-4 Anomalies (McLovin) is locked into the playoffs. He has to win the same number of games as Fatties and Raphi Bomb to earn a first round BYE. 7-4 Corn Fed Fatties (Babe) is locked into the playoffs. He has to win one more game than Anomalies to earn a first round BYE, or close a significant points gap for the tiebreaker. 6-5 Raphi Bomb (Fingers) can only miss the playoffs if he drops two games, while HOP or Juan go undefeated. He can earn a first round BYE by winning one more game than Anomalies and Fatties and winning the points tiebreaker (which he currently leads). 6-5 Ain’t That a Mitch (DIP) can only miss the playoffs if he drops two games, while HOP or Juan go undefeated. 5-6 Ain’t Got No Luck (Double) has to win the same number of games as Stars or Inches to edge them out of the last playoff spot. 4-7 Trophy Wife (EMCO) is leading the pack of contenders fighting for a playoff spot. She just has to win 1 more game than Double to make it in, while beating out HOP/Double. She is currently winning the points tiebreaker between Stars and Inches. 4-7 Rising Stars (Juan) could make the playoffs if he does better than HOP/EMCO/Double the next two weeks. He is winning the points tiebreaker against HOP, but losing against EMCO. 4-7 The Couple Inches (HOP) could make the playoffs if he does better than Juan/EMCO/Double the next two weeks. He is losing the points tiebreaker between EMCO and Juan. 2-9 The Dude is playing for the draft pick of his choice next year in the consolation bowl.
