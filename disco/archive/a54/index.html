Disco Week 6 Preview

Disco Chat Vol 2.II

Mid-Season Breakdown Because Our Teams are Broken If you’re still here with all your players healthy and a winning record, then congratulations: We hate you. Fantasy has not been kind to us this year, and whether it’s a lack of preseason games, COVID, or the fact that 2020’s not panning out as well as we’d hoped, the NFL has taken a heavy toll. Basically, the winning draft strategy this year was not to draft RB/WR early, because they’re all injured, and our rosters are waiver-wire Frankensteins heading into Halloween. In any case, there’s still some time to turn things around, if you believe.

Matchup Roundup Hot Takes on Hot Garbage BYE weeks have begun officially, and not just ‘Rona ones! Lions and Packers. Game of the Week: Double (4-0) vs Fingers (2-2) It’s going to be an uphill battle for Double to win without CMC and Chris Godwin, with TJ Hockenson on BYE, but that’s where we are. It’s a chance for Fingers to unseat the only undefeated team remaining in the league, and increase his playoff odds from his current 79%. He’s got another juggernaut team: Russ is cooking, Cook is cooking, and Josh Jacobs is a solid RB1 despite having put up a weaker week 4. His WR corps is kinda weak with AJB still out, but Kupp and Parker should be fine. Can we take a moment to appreciate how Cook has Cook, Cooks, and (Russell) Cooking? Clearly the favorite in this match. On the other side of the ball, you can’t love starting either Drake, Burkhead, or Gage/Crowder. Honestly, Double’s team is in shambles, and would be even worse if he hadn’t managed to snag Mike Davis. Juju, if healthy, can bolster a weak WR corp, and Gage may have increased value with Julio out again, but Double is looking like this year’s Bears: Strange to see them undefeated, and wracked by injuries. Moment of Truth: The Dude (1-3) vs Pitch (1-3) If you’re going to turn the corner and make the playoffs, this is the week to do it. Going 1-4 is basically lights out, so bring it strong! The Browns FTW have the advantage here, even with Chubb nursing a knee injury that slammed him into IR. The Dude lost his RB1 this week as well, with Ekeler sacrificing another hammy on the altar of football injuries. That leaves Jordan Howard as the next man up for the Dude (I guess he could fall into the endzone a couple times) and JK Dobbins for Pitch, who is in a timeshare with Ingram that I want no part of. The gloves have come off on this match, but the hands are broken, so it’s shaping up to be a bit of a slap-fest or pillow fight. Palindrome Special: HOP (1-3) vs Juan (3-1) The Underdogs are not in this matchup, which looks to be 60/40 in their favor, with HOP’s team decimated: Missing Cam Newton, Saquon, Hyde, Kerryon, Shepard, and Goedert. Juan’s missing Tarik Cohen, Mike Williams, Pittman, and Jordan Reed, but those are easier rapids to navigate. The WR1 for the week, DeAndre Hopkins, is looking for blood against the hapless Jets (sorry EMCO), and he’s got the RB1 Zeke looking for a Cowboys win against an easier Giants matchup. HOP’s no pushover though, since you love Ridley and Thielen as a pair, and Kittle is always solid. Deshaun Watson’s looking to bounce back against a softer Jacksonville defense, so there’s hope. The Muddy Middle: DIP (1-3) vs Babe (2-2) This one will be a close call for either team, with Cornfed favored 55/45. It’s a must-win for DIP, as he needs to improve his 52% playoff odds and keep momentum going into BYE weeks. He’s gonna have to do it without Michael Thomas, unfortunately, which leaves him with Allen Robinson and DJ Moore. Moore’s had a rough start to the season with Robby Anderson on the Panthers, and A-Rob is always suspect considering his QB situation (sorry DIP, but it’s true). Babe’s missing Aaron Jones, which means he has to rely on Melvin Gordon the third and Darrel Henderson to get things done on the ground. He’s got his own WR homer in CeeDee Lamb, who flashed this week in a game where Dak couldn’t stop throwing the ball. Honestly, the Cowboys may as well just not send out a defense at this point so they can get the ball back in Dak, Lamb, and Zeke’s hands. Just like the Cowboys, DIP’s looking to improve on a 1-3 record to establish himself in the playoff hunt. Commish Choice: McLovin (2-2) vs EMCO (3-1) It’s not looking great for me this week. I’m missing Davante Adams, Kenny G, and Matt Stafford against one of the league’s stronger teams. She’s missing Rogers, but with how Josh Allen has been balling for her team, who really cares? It’s super Kamara’s world, and EMCO’s just enjoying it, and she benefits from Robby Anderson’s ascendance in the Panthers as her WR2. Kelce is a strong TE option, facing off against whoever I can scrounge off the waiver wire. This one’s 70/30 in her favor, but there’s always a chance in fantasy!
