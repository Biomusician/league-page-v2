2021 Disco Week 9

Disco Chat Vol 3.IX

The Lowdown Thick of It You’re either in the thick of it or you’re sick of it. Since our league failed to unanimously nominate some sort of punishment for the loser we’re just sticking with the annoying trophy that you have to mail to next year’s loser. Depending on where that person is, that’s gonna be some serious postage, so think twice before you completely check out! If you’ve already thought twice, think again - third time’s the charm.

Matchup Roundup

Sealed (4-4) vs HOP (5-3) 69/31 HOP BYE/INJ: Godwin, CEH, Metcalf, Ruggs, Saquon? Sealed BYE/INJ: Russ This is my favorite matchup, because it embodies this week’s theme: Both teams are doing their best to break into the playoff conversation and establish themselves as real contenders. HOP is going to have to find a way to do that without the help of his recently-DUI’d and manslaughter’d young star, Henry Ruggs. Has there ever been a bigger fall from grace since Ray Rice and Deshaun Watson? Probably not. Hopefully he can course correct his team before they end up in the toilet bowl. If Saquon comes back and is healthy, that will be a huge boon, since he’s still without CEH (not that the treasure-turned-trash Chiefs would use him correctly even if he were healthy). For Sealed, he has everyone he cares about, but remains weak at running back. Tyreek is still a star even if the Chiefs offense is a train wreck right now. Babe (5-3) vs Juan (1-7) 72/28 Babe BYE/INJ: “Smooches” McKissic, AB, Logan Thomas Juan BYE/INJ: NSTR Will Juan ever be anything other than an underdog in these matchups? What is the opposite - an overdog? Juan, it’s mostly over, dog. You’ve got a better WR corps, but unless Davante and DeAndre can drop serious points, you’re relying on a 36 year old All Day to close the gap against Kamara and Chuba Hubba. Sure, the Fatties are thin at WR, relying on Sutton with Jeudy back (uh oh), and Marvin freaking Jones, who’s on the Jaguars and therefore mostly irrelevant. Ekeler and Justin Jefferson are going to be nails in your fantasy coffin unless your ragtag bunch of misfits can piece together another win. Can you win at roulette two weeks in a row? Can the broken X-wings take out the Deathstar again? I think they can, which is why this matchup is my favorite of the week. EMCO (5-3) vs Fingers (6-2) 78/22 EMCO BYE/INJ: Fournette, TY McLovin BYE/INJ: Tom Brady, Mike Evans Both players are missing their key Buccaneers, but EMCO doesn’t even need them, since she’s got Mixon and JT to fill the gaps at RB with Fournette out. On the other side of the ball, we’ve got Fingers starting Taysom Hill (?!) and Mike White at QB, in a truly odiferous SF arrangement. That’s why this match is my favorite. Will EMCO try to start Allen Robinson again after Mooney outplayed him last week? I hope not. Matt Nagy is back at HC duties in Chicago, so it’s back to the doldrums for the Bears offense again this week, just like it’s probably going to be Fingers’ 3rd loss this week against EMCO and the up and comers. Glory (3-5) vs DIP (4-4) 74/26 The Dude BYE/INJ: CMC, David Montgomery The Dude BYE/INJ: Chris Carson, Antonio Gibson, Scary Terry, the Hockstrap The Dude gets an in-season BYE, since DIP’s key players are either mostly on BYE or outclassed by their Glory-drafted counterparts, with one glaring exception. Do you know why this is my favorite matchup this week? It’s because DIP doesn’t have a starting TE, as of the time of this being published. So if you read it before he does and want to hurt his chances, just pick up a TE off waivers like he should have done earlier today. Maybe we should cut him a break since he’s doing that big exercise at Aviano. Or maybe we should take pity on him since he’s starting D’Ernest Johnson and Darnell Mooney, along with Jakobi Meyers this week. Meh, nah! I’m rooting for the underdog here, but DIP is praying that Kupp takes a week off, and that the rest of The Dude’s roster underperforms to pull out a W here. McLovin (6-2) vs Pitch (1-7) 68/32 McLovin BYE/INJ: Swift, Lockett Pitch BYE/INJ: Goff, Gronk I’ve got the edge everywhere except WR, which is where the booms usually occur, so I’m nervous this week. I’m hoping that Pitch’s Kyler has another down week, that her AJB comes back down to earth this week, and that Robert Woods is the ‘21 version not the ‘20 version. I’m hoping that Higgins, who I recently traded her, doesn’t crush me after MT declared he was out for the season. And I’m hoping that her QB2, Justin Fields, continues to suffer from the Matt Nagy curse and underperforms against the Steelers on Monday. I’m hoping all of these things because this is my game, I’m favored, and I want to win. That’s why it’s my favorite matchup this week.

Draft Regrets

Ever look back at your draft and figure out where you went wrong? If not, I did it for you, to paint a picture of what could have been. On the flip side, it highlights who got the steals this year. * What do you think the price of a top-12 RB is? Second-round draft pick? What if I told you that the current RB12 on the year was drafted in the 12th round? It’s Leonard Fournette, who was drafted 12.1 by EMCO. Meanwhile, DIP’s gotta be kicking himself because he drafted Robby Anderson, the WR67, at 11.1. Whoops. * This one hits close to home, because when I drafted OBJ at 9.6, he was still on the board. I thought the injury risk was too high, so I left the current WR3 on the season on the board. He’s a 49er, and his name is Deebo Samuel. EMCO scooped him up 4 picks later at 9.10. I remember mocking her pick in person when she made it. Look who got the last laugh! * When you wait until the 4th round to draft a running back, you’re hoping they outperform expectations. Sorry Juan, but your 4.3 pick has not. Miles Sanders is on IR, and sits at RB37. Even worse, the backup RBs scored like a million TDs in the first game without him, and he didn’t score any during the first 7 weeks. At this spot, you could have picked up Kyle Pitts (Sealed), AJ Brown (Pitch), or the RB11 on the year, who was snatched up by The Dude (again, credit to Glory) at pick 6.9 (nice), Darrell Henderson. * That’s not the worst RB luck ever though, because someone managed to come away with the RB19 at 2.10 and the RB55 at pick 5.1 (on IR still). DIP passed up Ceedee Lamb, Chris Godwin, and Deandre Swift to pick up Antonio Gibson and Chris Carson, who haven’t lived up to the hype. * We all understand what it’s like to need time away from work to reflect, but I wasn’t expecting my 4th-round pick Calvin Ridley to step away from football. I wish him all the best, but I also wish that I’d picked up this year’s WR1 in the 7th-round instead. That honor goes to The Dude (Glory, really), who scooped up Cooper Kupp at 7.2. Whatever you paid him to draft your team, it wasn’t enough. * Okay, enough credit to Glory, time to tear him down. His 8th and 9th round picks sucked, and are currently the WR88 and RB70 on the year. He chose the wrong 49er in Brandon Aiyuk, and the wrong Buc in Ronald Jones, who are both hated by their respective coaches for arcane reasons. Their teammates, drafted later at 9.10 (Deebo Samuel, EMCO) and 12.1 (Leonard Fournette, EMCO), were much better bangs for their buck. Really, seeing these results, it’s a surprise that EMCO and Glory aren’t arch enemies or at least nemeses (a fun word to type, even if I had to Google it to make sure it was correct). * RB16 on the year isn’t too shabby for someone drafted in the ninth round (9.1). RB32 and RB108 is pretty bad for two RBs drafted earlier in the 8th. The RB16 is Damien Harris, picked by yours truly, while the 8th rounders are Mike Davis and Raheem Mostert, drafted 8.2 and 8.3 by HOP ‘n’ Juan, which sounds like a bad restaurant. * You know who’s good at football, and is still the TE17 even after having missed 4 games? Gronk, picked by Pitch at 11.7. Bet that Babe wished he’d picked him up at 10.7 instead of Logan Thomas, who got injured even worse. * No bust list would be complete without the WR59 Allen Robinson, picked at 5.10 by EMCO. She passed up players like Amari Cooper (6.6, WR20, Fingers), Cooper Kupp (7.2, WR1, Glory), and DJ Moore (7.3, WR9, Sealed), or even Tyler Lockett (7.6, WR19, Me). Woof.
