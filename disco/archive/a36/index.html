The Lowdown - MILLERTIME No Reattack Required Sometimes, waiting improves the final product: Aging whiskey or wine, waiting for a fish strike on a cast line, and meticulously crafting a polished product after months of consistent work are all good examples. This fantasy newsletter and these finals are not one of these moments. Why is the newsletter so late this week? Choose one or more of the following * Had to be sure the latest COVID news was accounted for. * The subject matter was challenging, and extra editing time was required to produce a polished product. * Waiting for my FAAB fee to pop into my FAAB bank before publishing * I was playing Red Dead Redemption 2 * Drinking * Procrastination forced a flurried fight against sleep, as I am writing it in a hotel room at Nellis after flying over from Venice.

The Finals

Babe vs McLovin 70/30 Babe INJ: NSTR McLovin INJ: NSTR Of course the finals would come down to Cowboys fan vs Cowboys fan! “but MCloVin, ThErE are waY TOO MANY cOWBOyS fanS In THIs LeAGUE AnD This Was BOUND to haPPEN! aLsO rIGgEd!” To you, I say that a) that was as painful to type as it was to read and process, b) We’re just excellent and c) sucks to your ass-mar. To say I have an uphill battle ahead of me is putting it lightly. Josh Allen will have to carry me against Babe’s juggernaut of a team. I’ll need a standout performance from him; even though he’s against a soft Atlanta defense, it’s not guaranteed that he’ll keep his foot on the fantasy gas the entire game, and the Bills could turn to the run after establishing an early lead, killing his value. I’d also need either The Muth or my team’s namesake Cole Kmet to put up decent points - more than decent points - against Mark Andrews for Babe. Mark Andrews is going up against a tough Rams defense, but he’s Mark Andrews. Hehe, I’m in danger. Some of my starts are ugly no matter how I look at them: Lockett and Cooks as my WRs? Eek. Van Jefferson and Aiyuk in my flex? Ew. Maybe having to play D’Andre Swift? Yikes. I hope he can do it coming back from injury/COVID. Ditto for Elijah Mitchell, who’s been out for multiple weeks. For Babe, he’s got stars: Herbert, Ekeler, Kamara (kinda yikes recently tho), Jefferson, AB, Andrews, and RoJo in for a spot start. If only I had saved $1 FAAB for RoJo, so he could be playing on my team instead of Babe’s. That may turn into the critical mistake that turns the tide of the championship. Getting a top-10 RB on championship week for free would be pretty nice! Not all Babe’s players are without question marks though. Can Jefferson be relevant without Kirk Cousins, who has COVID? Will Kamara get his groove back or continue to unimpress? Will AB come back from his ankle injury and be good, or just kinda run around and do nothing since the Bucs won’t need to do much against the Jets? And he has to start DeVonta Smith and Dare Ogunbowale too - Dare against a tough NE run DST, and DeVonta against a shreddable WFT defense.
