2021 Daddy Week 1 Preview and Trade Review

Daddy Issues Vol III.I

The Lowdown New Year, New You You’ll never feel better about your team than right now. All the positive narratives, preseason hype, and moves you’ve made over the offseason have set your team up for success this year, and there’s no way you’re not making the playoffs. Inevitably, though, injuries, coaching, matchups, luck, and reality will conspire to destroy your team’s chances of ever blossoming into anything special. In that moment, remember: It’s not your fault, fantasy is all luck, and you’re going to crush next year. And when you do make the playoffs, despite all the aforementioned obstacles, notch your belt and take it as confirmation that you’re one of the best fantasy football players ever - you deserve it.

This Week’s Matchups Unfettered Optimism/Yet-to-be-Shattered Dreams Game of the Week: Bazinga (0-0) vs The Commish (0-0) 75/25 What better way to transition from rebuild to competition than to beat last year’s defending champion? The odds are stacked against her, but she may prove the Luke/Rey Skywalker (spoilers!) against Emperor Bazinga/Brady. She’s got Dak under center, returning from injury with a star WR corps, up against Brady. Bazinga’s once again rolling out top-3 studs Dalvin Cook and Alvin Kamara, paired with DK Metcalf and Justin Jefferson. Meanwhile, Karen’s got Joe Mixon (who, by some miracle, isn’t hurt yet) and rookie sensation Najee Harris, who will be struggling to accomplish anything behind the Steelers’ rusty sieve of an offensive line. Also, you hate to put up any TE against Travis Kelce, but The Commish has to toss out Dallas Goedert against him, which probably isn’t going to work out. Jeudy and Chark have to compete against the Rob-combo of Allen Robinson and Robert Woods in Bazinga’s flex. We’re rooting for the underdog, but she’s still an underdog - especially without JK Dobbins. Trade Rivalry Row: Ross (0-0) vs DIP (0-0) 60/30 Two of the leagues premiere (or prolific, at least) traders are squaring off to determine whose offseason moves have paid off the most. Ross has a nice advantage on paper, primarily because DIP has to line up his Myles/Miles RB combo (bonus points!) against JT and Antonio Gibson. Woof. Maybe he can close the gap with Davante Adams and Calvin Ridley against Diggs and AJB, but then again, Ross has the positional advantage at TE with stud George Kittle against the Fant of wisdom on DIP’s side. Finally, DIP has the option of rolling out another combo in Chase Claypool/Edmonds. Double Miles, double Chase! There’s a travel ad joke there somewhere, but I don’t see it - just like I don’t see DIP traveling to victory in this matchup. Coinflip: McLovin (0-0) vs Basic (0-0) 50/50 This game could have been played last season, as there have been no major acquisitions during the offseason on either team that are likely to make the starting lineups. Courtland Sutton is outshined by Keenan Allen, CeeDee Lamb, David Montgomery, and Diontae Johnson on the OK Boomers (McLovin), and for Basic, Rashod is hurt, Dyami Brown is irrelevant, and Justin Fields is probably not in his starting lineup over Tannethrill. Basic’s keys to victory lie in Derrick Henry’s continued dominance, paired with Tyreek Hill’s stranglehold on the Chief’s passing game. He’ll also be bolstered if OBJ can return healthy, and if Adam Thielen can continue to demand target share and touchdowns opposite 2nd-year star Justin Jefferson. Meanwhile, McLovin’s relying on CeeDee Lamb and Zeke to ball out with Dak back under center for the Cowboys. It’s anyone’s game! Almost Upset of the Week: Kelsey (0-0) vs Distro (0-0) 60/40 Distro’s riding high(er) after the offseason acquisition of Mike Evans, who, along with Terry McLaurin present a problematic WR corps for Kelsey’s gaing Julio Jones and Chris Godwin. Time will tell whether Julio can produce with the Titans, or whether Godwin will emerge as the WR1 in TB. Distro also has The Hock at TE, up against Logan Thomas, who may have the best QB he’s ever had in Ryan Fitzpatrick throwing him the ball - if the perennially-unnamed WFT decide to allocate targets to their TE. Kelsey also has Josh Jacobs, who may not produce as much with Kenyan Drake in the backfield. Russell Wilson (Kelsey) vs Patrick Mahomes (Distro) is a good matchup, but you’ve gotta give the edge to Patty after the last half of last season, where Russ was, sadly, no longer cooking. What pushes Kelsey’s team over the edge to victory are CMC/Swift against Clyde and Chris for Distro. I don’t think Kenny G and Michael Pittman can close that gap against Jacobs and Lockett. Kelsey would definitely be feeling stronger if he still had Cam Akers available. Or Travis Etienne. Still, hope springs eternal, miracles happen, and fantasy football is fickle! New Kid on the Block: ZPSiko (0-0) vs Jolly Mangina (0-0) 60/40 Let the rebuild begin! Blade is dead; long live ZPSiko! There’s a chance he could turn his first FF match in this league into a win, led by young studs Justin Herbert and Saquon Barkley. He’s also got star DeAndre Hopkins, who he traded for recently with Ross, sacrificing rookie stud Aiyuk(en!), some draft capital, and Damien Harris. That trade gives him the edge in this matchup, as JuJu/Corey Davis probably aren’t getting it done against Hopkins and Kupp. Still, Jolly has recently-resurrected Jamse Robinson and Awesome Ekeler at RB, which stack up well against Mostert and a recently-back-from-the-dead Saquon. If Saquon can’t turn it back up with the humdrum NYG offense, or if Herbert doesn’t show up ready against Jolly’s Kyler Murray, then this game could easily swing Jolly’s way. Otherwise, the FLEX/TE options that ZPSiko has in Andrews, Deebo, and Landry will probably beat out near-waiver-wire sensations Tonyan, Kirk, and Agholor. Eek.

SIGEVENTS You win some... Week’s Best Pickup The Commish: Starting Joe Mixon was tough after the first 3 weeks, and many managers would have looked elsewhere for their RB2/Flex option, but the Commish stuck with it and was rewarded by a 3 TD performance - almost enough to carry her to victory against The Evil Emperor himself, Bazinga. Better luck next time, because you made the right call. Week’s Worst Pickup Mangina: You were tempted by Higbee’s 3 TD performance week 2, even after a pedestrian showing week 3, and he rewarded you with 5.1 points on 3 catches for 21. Meanwhile, your bench was on fire with Big Dairy/Little Kittle/Baby Kittle himself, Robert Tonyan, who used the Packers’ complete loss of all talented WR talent to turn in a 3 TD, 33.8 point performance. That would have been enough for the victory, especially since DIP couldn’t be bothered to put together a contingency plan for Davante Adams, leaving the inactive player on his roster. 🔔Shame🔔 Trade Review Ross and Jolly: DeAndre Hopkins + two 2nds for Damien Harris, Brandon Aiyuk, 1st This trade is even on paper, but one could argue that a rebuild team acquiring a 29yo WR doesn’t make a whole lot of sense. Then again, maybe Hopkins will be enough to push ZPSiko into the playoffs, while Damien Harris ends up going the way of NE RBs of the past and not being relevant (Sony Michel, anyone?) and Brandon Aiyuk ends up injured or busting behind Deebo Samuel and George Kittle. Receivers can still produce into their 30s, and the common advice is to play dynasty FF within a 3-year window, so Hopkins fits that mold. Plus, it’s a trade that acquires proven talent for a couple breakout candidates. Time will tell who the smarter or luckier manager is in this one. McLovin and ZPSiko: Courtland Sutton for 2022 1st/2nd and Quez Watkins McLovin needed WR depth, and he believes 25yo Sutton can be elite after an ACL tear ended his 2022 season. ZPSiko was all too happy to unload an unproven receiver coming off an injury for some great draft capital moving into next season. If Courtland Sutton can’t emerge over 2nd-year WR Jerry Jeudy, McLovin may have mortgaged his future for nothing. Ross and Basic: Michael Thomas and a 2nd for Adam Thielen and OBJ Look, I understand wanting to acquire a receiver like Michael Thomas (2019 version). I also understand wanting to acquire Adam Thielen (2017/18 version) and OBJ (2019 or pre-2017 version). Both of these guys are chasing the past with these players, but at least Thielen had 14 TDs last year, and that’s why I side with Thielen side. McLovin and Distro: Mike Evans for a 2021 1st/2nd (Distro) Distro needed WR depth, and he believes 28yo Mike Evans can continue to produce 1,000 yard seasons, as he has done 7 times before. McLovin had WR depth, and wanted to play the draft lottery with Javonte Williams. MGIII probably keeps most of the touches this year, but next year Javonte may be usable! The two TE for 2nd trades: Ross/Kelsey (Logan Thomas) and McLovin/DIP (Irv Smith) Welp, one of these didn’t work out (sorry DIP) and the other one is still a question mark. I don’t like Logan Thomas’ chances to break out this year, but to each his own. Both of these trades are what Ross once called “trading pocket lint,” Ross and DIP: Miles Sanders, Myles Gaskin, Quintez Cephus for Jalen Reagor, Tyler Boyd, and a 2021 3rd (Pat Friermouth) DIP came out of this trade miles ahead (at least 2). Quintez has been dropped already, as he should be, being the third receiving option on a terrible Lions team behind Tyrell Williams and the Hock. I don’t love Gaskin or Sanders, but without them DIP would have had Chase Edmonds as his RB1, followed by Kenyan Drake. Oof. Maybe they ball out and propel him to the ‘Ship. Ross loves him some young players with upside, and Reagor could turn the corner as a 2nd year WR. Tyler Boyd is older than Higgins or Chase in Cinci, but he’s probably the best WR value there at ADP, and should still have a solid season while Chase remembers how to catch footballs and gain separation again. Draft Crusher The Commish: After grabbing Najee, Pitts, and Chase in the first round, she turned around and grabbed Sermon in the 2nd. Ridiculous draft capital, and good picks. Not perfect though - she drafted Javian Hawkins in the 4th who ended up getting released. Oh well! Honorable mention: Ross for presciently drafting Marquez Callaway in the 4th Dishonorable mention: Bazinga for autodrafting Matt Stafford in the 2nd Rookies to Watch Mac Jones (Jolly), who secured a starting job in NE after Cam was released Ja’Marr Chase (Commish), who was accused of domestic violence by his pregnant GF on IG and dropped most of his preseason passes Trey Sermon (Commish), who could break out of the SF backfield behind injury-prone veteran Mostert Najee Harris (Commish), the talented runner behind a terrible offensive line Zach Wilson (Kelsey), starting QB for the NYJ, throwing to fantasy darling Elijah Moore Justin Fields (Basic), almost-starting QB for the Bears, pending Andy Dalton’s fall Trey Lance (ZPSiko), almost-starting QB for the 49ers, pending his recovery from a finger chip, and Jimmy G’s fall from grace

Side Bet Status In Progress: None, and that’s sad.

Completed: All of last year’s. Bazinga still owes me a whiskey, which I may end up acquiring next year at Tinker, unless we meet at Nellis before then.
