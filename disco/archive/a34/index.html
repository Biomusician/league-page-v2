2021 Disco Week 15

Disco Chat Vol 3.XV

The Lowdown Slacking Two teams decided that week 15 wasn’t worth their time: EMCO and McLovin haven’t even set a decent lineup, and it’s because they don’t have anything left to play for - at least not this week. To Pitch, DIP, Juan, and HOP, you’re playing to not win the last place trophy, which I still haven’t managed to send to Pitch. Meanwhile, The Dude is battling Sealed, and Fingers is fighting Babe to make it to the quarterfinals. We only have 3 weeks of fantasy left before either someone is crowned champion, or everyone catches COVID and the season ends early.

The Quarter-Finals Dance-Off

Sealed vs The Dude 65/35 Sealed INJ: NSTR The Dude INJ: Daniel Jones, Elijah Moore In a 4th and 5th place matchup that is certain to light things on fire, The Dude is starting off with the worm - a tried and true classic that when executed well, is a winner. Dak Prescott is good when he’s good, but if he has an off week, then man can he tank your team. Cooper Kupp is probably what The Dude imagines the lawnmower, shopping cart, or classic disco - timeless, sure-fire paths to dance victory. Meanwhile, Sealed is relying on trendy underrated pickups like Cordarrelle Patterson and James Conner. They’re like the waltz or salsa of dance moves - old, but definitely impressive in their own right. Still, whether Dalvin can pop and lock his way to victory over Kittle’s high kicks remains to be seen. Babe vs Fingers 56/44 Babe INJ: NSTR Fingers INJ: Kenyan Drake The Fatties (3rd) and Sniffers (6th) square off this week in a match that tilts in Babe’s favor, thanks to his strong RB room. Like a group of solid backup dancers, Ekeler and Kamara are standing right behind Babe while he attempts to Dougie his way to victory. If he can’t do it, it’s because two backup dancers aren’t really a crew, and Fingers is bringing the depth with a star-studded flex cast that is basically a flash mob of talent: Hollywood, Kirk (with no DeAndre), AJG (with no DeAndre), and Taysom Hill, who is the new Jalen Hurts of being bad at real football but great for fantasy. It’s like the singer who everyone knows can’t really sing, but can lip sync well enough to shoot a good video. Anyway, Fingers is probably a two-step or line dance kinda fella, and it takes more than two to tango in the semi-finals, so best of luck! DIP vs Pitch 69/31 DIP INJ: Carson, Corey Davis Pitch INJ: Fuller V, AJB, JuJu You know those people who dance their hearts out without really knowing how to dance, not caring what the world thinks? Yeah, neither of these teams is really that person. Pitch’s team are the wallflowers: Kinda just watching the dance hall crumble around them, as Zeke tries to wobble his way through a pirouette, and Alex Collins tries to be relevant behind Penny as the Seahawks try to dance on ice. DIP’s team has a solid chance of rocketing out of the toilet bowl and eliminating his chances of dipping into last place. His team can dance like a Freeman, jump to the Mooney, Gallup to victory, and Diggs deep for extra endurance. All his Mahomes will be marginally impressed if he escapes last place here. Juan vs HOP (58/42) Juan INJ: DeAndre Hopkins HOP INJ: NSTR I imagine this one going like a head-to-head breakdance, something that I’ve seen in movies, YT videos, and a Key & Peele sketch (also on YT). Either way, this match has NOICE potential to be a back and forth. It all hinges on whether main man Lamar Jackson shows up to play for HOP, or whether, just like the OG noice guy in the skit, he gets on a bus to leave. For the QB nursing home, it’ll depend on whether Aaron Rodgers toe troubles are fact or fiction, Davante Adams can catch his or Jordan Love’s passes, and whether Waddle can… - wait, Waddle just got COVID. Guess it’s Ameer Abullah time for Juan? It’s like a group of people are having a dance battle, and you’re just standing in the circle watching, and then your friends thought it’d be funny to push you in the middle of the circle and cheer. That’s Ameer Abdullah right now. I feel bad for him, and worse for you that he’s in your lineup.
