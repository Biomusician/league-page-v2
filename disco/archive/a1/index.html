2021 Disco Week 4

Disco Chat Vol 3.IV

The Lowdown Settin’ Grooves In the mid 19th-century, when the Oregon trail was poppin’, caravans of wagons set off in search of a better life. During this period, it only took three weeks of continuous traffic in order to carve deep grooves, or ruts, into the soil, which could either facilitate or hinder other wagons from crossing through that same path, depending on the topography. Your team has either found its groove or is stuck in a rut, and it’s your responsibility to keep on managing it to victory. Do you ford the river and take a chance on a waiver wire miracle? Do you pay the native Americans and make a trade? Do you suck the poison out of the snakebite wound and drop or trade someone you spent high draft capital on who’s been underperforming? It’s choose your own adventure season, and if your team dies of dysentery and never makes it to the promised land, that’s on you. Speaking of dying before reaching the promised land, Dude, you need to send me your address in Tinker so I can send you a winner’s trophy. PS: I made up the bit about three weeks to make a wagon rut. It probably depends on the surface, rain, etc. That said, most of your teams are definitely in a rut.

Matchup Roundup Get Along Little Doggies One to Watch: McLovin (3-0) vs Fingers (2-1) 53/47 First and second place teams square off in this battle. Both are dealing with injuries - Tee Higgins is out for McLovin, while Fingers is missing Jerry Jeudy. Fingers is hoping that Christian Kirk and Marquise Brown continue to show out, though Hollywood is coming off a terrible showing in week 3 where he dropped at least 3 TD passes from Lamar. Kelce is a clear advantage over up-and-comer Tyler “Honk Honk” Conklin, who hopefully won’t goose me this week. Do I trust OBJ coming off his injury? Not quite, but I may be forced to play him over Patterson, Ty’Son, and my other options. Speaking of other options, I bet Sniffin’ wishes he had someone other than Taylor Heinicke to throw into his QB2 slot. Unless he’s trusting Jameis, it may be a rough one. He’s also hoping that Mike Williams has a good week, Amari Cooper can secure some targets, and that Myles Gaskin can do something in Miami. Lots of unknowns in this game, but plenty of room for movement to happen and the slight advantage to swing. Civilian Special: Babe (1-2) vs HOP (1-2) 60/40 A couple veterans square off in a battle that skews slightly toward the individual with a more impressive beard. Imagine a couple old dudes squaring off in the commissary parking lot with those veteran hats on - that’s this matchup. You gotta like Babe’s team, led by Herbert, Kamara, and Ekeler, who have been on fire to start the year. He also is rostering Chuba Hubbard, who is filling in for an injured CMC. HOP is fighting back with DK Metcalf and Chris Godwin at WR, which stack up well against J-Jeff and Courtland Sutton, but in the TE and FLEX spot, he’s lacking solid options to stand up against AB, DeVonta Smith, Marvin Jones, and Mark Andrews. Noah’s not a FANTastic option, and though Pittman, MGIII, and Zack Moss have given us hope recently, they’re low ceiling options. There’s some hope though, as HOP holds Jane Hurts, who proved that he can be fantasy relevant even in a terrible game. Cowboy Fan Duel: Sealed (2-1) vs Juan (0-3) 60/40 QB Nursing Home? Juan’s team is basically on the coroner’s table at 0-3, while Sealed is riding high at 2-1, led by Dalvin Cook and Mike Williams. You’d think his ceiling would be capped rostering both Chargers WRs, but it’s been a great ride so far. Tyreek hasn’t done much for him yet, but you know the WR1 from 2019 is gonna ball soon. Russell Wilson is a good QB1, but he could be even better if Seattle let him cook. Jimmy G is a ticking time bomb in his QB2 slot, but he doesn’t have better options, unless he wants to slot in one of his two turnover machines: T-Law or Zach Wilson. That’s not to say that Juan has anything better. Big Ben is basically dead, and Rodgers has been sporadic. Additionally, Miles Sanders only had 3 rushing attempts last week, so there’s cause for concern there. Between Davante/DeAndre, his WR core group is solid, but he’s rolling out a lottery TE in Dawson Knox. If Sterling Shepard doesn’t suit up this week, he’ll be in real trouble at FLEX as well. Imagine Juan as an old, broken Cowboy lost in his whiskey, as Sealed as a young buckaroo at the top of his bull riding game. It’s gonna be a rough match. Josh Gordon, your local rodeo clown, is not gonna help you here Juan. EMCO (1/2) vs The Dude (2-1) 58/42 Once again, EMCO’s deep bench should pay dividends against The Dude, whose well-drafted team is like a new car that rolled off the lot and immediately was subjected to Dukes of Hazard jumps and off-roading; it’s gradually falling apart as the PCS takes its toll. Stafford has been an excellent value pickup for EMCO, and Mixon/Chubb provide a stable week to week floor. While she struggles at WR1/2 with Chase and Deebo, especially against The Dude’s little Cooper Kuppa Coffee, Ceedee, and George Kittle, she can close the gap with her QB2 Kirk Cousins, who’s been surprisingly solid this year. Allen Robinson in her FLEX spot is scary, since the Bears can’t seem to get it up on offense, but JT, Cole Beasley, and Leonard Fournette are solid options. For the Dude, he’s playing Kittle and Aiyuk opposite EMCO’s Deebo, so depending on who Jimmy G tosses the football, we’ll see large swings during the SF game against Seattle. The Rams offense is on fire, and Darrel Henderson is set to benefit upon his return from injury. Otherwise, David Montgomery and Chase Edmonds will have to step up to give the Dude a chance at victory. CGO Clash: DIP (2-1) vs PITCH (1-2) 67/33 The most one-sided match on paper, PITCH will be relying on huge performances from Zeke, Kyler, and Waller to compete with DIP’s all-star roster. Will she play two TEs in Waller and Gronk? Will she trust Sermon and Pollard to start in her FLEX? Will she be forced to play Goff over Fields after his soul-crushing performance? I would, due to the mental conflict it would create in DIP rooting for his terrible team (blame Matt Nagy). For DIP, what can we say? Mahomes, Gibson, Diggs, McLaurin, Hockenson have all been solid. Chris Carson hasn’t been great, and is not involved in the passing game, but still provides a decent floor. Other concerns on his roster include Julio, who is currently limited with an injury, Damien Harris, who may put up another dud against the strong TB front, and Robby Anderson, who has yet to do anything after his blockbuster 2-year $26M deal. He also has to rely on the freshly-revived Sam Darnold, or the rookie Mac Jones. Yeah, it’s probably a Darnold week, since Jones will be facing TB’s defense. If Diggs and McLaurin struggle, Gibson sucks on a terrible WFT, and Carson gets limited based on negative game script against SF, then he’ll be vulnerable to a sneak attack from PITCH’s Browns for the win.
