2024 Disco Week 6

Disco Chat Vol 5.VI

The Lowdown Byepocalypse Eve “At least it’s not six teams on leave like it will be next week.” -Oscar Wilde We interrupt our regularly-scheduled matchups to bring you a special edition of The Lowdown: Things I hate and love about your team, and where you won and lost.

Week 6

HOP (3-2) Hate: Your TE room. How are you rostering Kincaid, Everett, and Hurst at the same time? That’s like rounding up an A-4, a Spitfire, and a JSTARS and trying to win the war. Love: Your QBs. Tua and Fields are two top-5 options to this point, and neither offense looks likely to substantially change. Nice value! Miss: Devonta Smith is currently the WR26 after 5 weeks, as the 10th receiver off the board. Maybe he’ll turn it around, or maybe the Slim Reaper will come for your playoff hopes. Hit: Justin Fields. The Bears may be bad, and Fields may not have a job in a year or two, but he’s the QB3 in fantasy to this point.

Babe (4-1) Hate: Kyle Pitts, the TE18, but not as much as Arthur Smith hates him. Jonnu Smith, his teammate, is the TE14 on Juan’s team, and he was a waiver add. Love: Your RB room. A series of fortunate draft picks has left you with 3 top-15 options - 4 if you include Jonathan Taylor! Miss: Jonathan Taylor was your first RB off the board in the 3rd round, and he’s scored 3.4 points for you. That’s more than 1pt/round! Bonus: Kyle Pitts in the 5th seemed like a value, but he’s the TE2 in Atlanta. Hit: KWIII. Everyone was scared stupid that Charbonnet was going to steal everything away from the 2nd year player, but it hasn’t even been a 50/50 split, and you’ve got the current RB8 on your hands. Bonus: CJ Stroud in the 8th, the QB14. Another Bonus: Swift in the 7th, the RB9.

Juan (1-4) Hate: If there’s a team with worse WRs than yours, I can’t wait to find it later in this article so I can mock it more mercilessly. When Jordan Addison is your WR1, you know you screwed up. What happened? Dishonorable mention: Your TEs too. Love: I love that you were able to draft Dak, since you’re a Cowboys fan, but that hasn’t really worked out too well for you has it? Josh Allen is good - probably your best player - but it’s tough to succeed without QB depth. Miss: I wish as much as the next guy that Jahmyr Gibbs displayed production half as amazing as the Lions’ coaching staff when they drafted him so early this year. Unfortunately, he’s the RB39, injured, and David Montgomery has seemingly taken over the backfield. Could things get better? Sure! They could hardly get worse. Hit: Your 12th and 13th round picks were almost prescient: Roschon and Jaylen Warren. If Roschon plays this week in place of an injured Khalil, and Jaylen continues to look better than Najee, then you have some real late-round RB value on your hands. Nice work!

Pitch (0-5) Hate: Your running backs are as bad as Juan’s WRs. Jerome Ford is probably your best option, since Najee turned out to be a steaming pile of Steelers. But at least you’ve got Tyler Allgeir and Tyjae Speaks to back him up at least - solid top-40 options! Also: Why do you have 4 mediocre TEs? Njoku, Gesicki, Irv, and Chig are not going to help you - then again, maybe the waiver wire won’t either… Love: You drafted Ja’Marr Chase, your only top-10 option this week. So good job I guess? Maybe he can put the team on his back. Miss: Your homer pick of Massage Watson at 2.1 when Tua, Cousins, Geno, etc. were still on the board may come back to haunt you. Let’s see if your team’s champion comes back to play this week against the 49ers, or whether he’ll suffer a “setback” (money still guaranteed btw) that rolls him to the next week. Also: Najee. Dobbins (injury). JSN (whoops). Hit: Jerome Ford in the 16th. I guess you didn’t have to drop 50+ FAB for a starting running back after Chubb went down. Instead, you drafted Ford late, because you just didn’t have enough of that dynamite Cleveland offense yet.

DIP (3-1) vs POP (2-2) Holy Chad Hennessys batman! So many things went right for DIP early in the season: Puka Nacua, David Montgomery, Nico Collins, Anthony Richardson - it’s like looking a best of draft day super values, and I hate it. Even anchor Daniel Jones isn’t enough to drag this team down. Meanwhile, POP’s Patty Mahomes (worse than Zach Wilson last week btw) is looking to make the difference with Sam Howell, hoping that A-Rich’s 44% completion rate (#2 fantasy finish anyway) will finally tank his fantasy value this week. Garrett Wilson sadface. Noah Gray? Desperation play with the Muth out. Not lookin’ good for the dogs to pull one out here.

Babe (3-1) vs Double (3-1) Clash of the Titans (the historical ones, not the NFL team) this week, with two 3-1 teams squaring off. It’ll be close, with Babe missing KWIII to level the playing field against Double’s starters. Looking at their QB2s, that may be the difference maker position: Babe’s got up and comer CJ Stroud who looks amazing, against Double Derek Carr, who looks like the opposite of Jameis Winston (per FFBallers, Jameis is “chuck down” and Derek is “check down”). Lamar’s gotta be better than Double’s Russ (ltd), and while D’Andre Swift and Pacheco aren’t mountain moving superstars for Babe, they’re going up against James Cook and Miles Sanders for Double. The WRs favor Double, but who knows where the ball will go in SF, which means that Aiyuk could have a down game. If JT doesn’t start this week, it could get ugly for Babe quick, but if he comes out swinging, it could carry him to a dub.
