2021 Disco Week 16

Disco Chat Vol 3.XVI

The Lowdown Homestretch And then there were four, three of which are Cowboys fans. Coincidence, or just a natural outcome of winners picking winners? Commissioner bias? Anyway, after unimpressive performances from most of our teams, two of you squeaked by into the quarterfinals, while two of us (me and EMCO) thanked our lucky stars that we didn’t have to play anyone this week. If the finals had been this week, EMCO would have beaten me by 0.3 points. Heartbreaker avoided. If we had all played each other this week, then the consolation bracket would have won out, with Pitch and Juan both putting up the top two scores, avoiding the last place trophy. That means it’s down to DIP and HOP. HOP would be looking to repeat his performance from the league’s first year, while DIP is looking to make his mark as a first-time Disco Downbeat. On the championship side of the house, everyone’s looking to score their first championship, while I’m looking to repeat my 2019 performance (I know, rigged). Tough loss by Fingers against Babe, with fewer than 3 points separating them, while Sealed ran away with his match against The Dude after he decided to abide with 92 points. It’s okay though - It’s not Vietnam; it’s fantasy.

The Semi-Finals

EMCO vs Babe 55/45 EMCO INJ: Fournette (Not playoff Lenny) Babe INJ: NSTR If this were Jets vs Cowboys it’d be over, but it’s a Jets fan against a Cowboys fan here. Judging purely by team loyalty here, EMCO wins - she owns 1 Jets player (Crowder, gross), while Babe owns zero ‘Boys. In fact, he has a Jets player too: Tevin Coleman (grosser). Judging by fantasy teams, here’s how it breaks down. EMCO has a better QB in Stafford vs Burrow, and a better RB1 in JT vs Ekeler, who hopefully is no longer injured and full-up for week 16. Meanwhile, Kamara for Babe edges out Mixon for EMCO (he was saving his week 15 points for week 16, right?!). For WRs, Jefferson is a clear better #1 than Diontae Johnson, while Chase could be better than DeVonta Smith, as long as he doesn’t repeat his week 15 stinker. Mark Andrews should crush Mike Gesicki in the battle of the M-name TEs. The FLEX position is where EMCO’s team pulls slightly ahead: Chubb, Deebo, Dillon, and Gage against Penny, Chuba, Marvin, and “Three First Names” Nick Westbrook-Okhine.” Okay, fine - I’ve never met someone named Westbrook or Ikhine, but you’d have to be some kind of psychopath to start the WR72 on the season. Going purely by regular season stats, EMCO is favored too; she’s won one more game than Babe’s team has, and has the top points scored by far - more than 190 more than Babe over 15 games, an average of 12.6 (repeating of course) per game. Neither team has won a championship in this league before, so both are willing to do whatever it takes to win this game - as long as “whatever it takes” is setting their best lineup, cursing at their TVs, and watching one or more of the games. Truly, fantasy is a heavy burden, and these two teams have carried it well into the final two games of the season. EMCO needs this victory because the Jets are terrible and it’s her only chance at tasting victory. Babe needs this, because after our Cowboys lose in the first round of the playoffs again, it will be our only solace. Sealed vs McLovin 60/40 Babe INJ: NSTR Fingers INJ: Kenyan Drake How the turntables have turned. Cordarrelle Patterson, the current RB8 in PPR, was traded to Sealed for Mike Williams, who is now out for the season, on October 5th. It was highway robbery. It was the best trade I ever made. I was bragging from the fantasy rooftops. Until I realized I was the one robbed, it was the worst trade I ever made, and I was actually just wearing the emperor's new clothes. Or was I? If Cordarrelle puts up a stinker this week, then it was all part of the plan, and I was just paving my way to the championship with an impossibly-prescient, forward-looking week-15 focused trade. I’m gonna need that poisoned pill to start working now to have a hope at besting Sealed, since the only positional advantage I have (and it’s a big one) is Josh Allen at QB over Russell Wilson, who’s been cooking like a Navy chef short on supplies - also my Jalen over his Jimmy is a good feeling. Again, judging purely by how many Cowboys are on our teams, it’s a clear Sealed victory, as he has Dr. Shchultz in his TE position, while I’m left rocking my namesake Cole Kmet. Live by the Bears, die by the Bears. Mostly, it’s die by the Bears, so I’m probably sunk there. Meanwhile, I’m praying for Brandin Cooks to have another impossible 2TD game here against Keenan Allen, and for Aiyuk to somehow produce behind Deebo and a resurgent Kittle. Other notables for Sealed include Javonte Williams, who’s really gotten things going without MGIII, and James Conner, another old RB who everyone else had written off. 5th Place: The Dude vs Fingers 50/50 It’s the first no money game from previous playoff hopefuls, and Fingers’ depth may bring him a win over The Dude’s Dak and friends lineup. Cooper Kupp, CeeDee Lamb, and George Kittle weren’t enough to propel The Dude’s team to the finals, and I place the blame for this purely on Glory McDonough, as should he. Angry phone calls incoming! If you were wondering where all the fantasy relevant Cowboys players were, they’re in this matchup: Dak and CeeDee for The Dude, and Amari Cooper for Fingers. 7th Place Pitch vs Juan The nursing home is looking to retire against the soft, homogenous, lukewarm porridge amalgam that is Pitch’s team. Not much to cut your teeth on here, but at least Juan can salvage some fantasy dignity after going 4-10 in the regular season. For Pitch, week 15 was a watershed moment. After squeezing water from a rock to get her one win during the regular season, she showed out in the first loser’s match to cement DIP or HOP’s fate. A win here would double her post-season win total as compared to her regular season, and give her team its first win streak of the year. We like to root for the underdogs, so go for the win, Brownsftw. LastPlace DIP vs HOP Here we are - quite possibly the most fun match this week, as we look to crown the season’s loser. How the mighty have fallen! DIP went from having a real shot at winning it all, to fighting to not be last. Will HOP repeat as the Disco Downbeat, or will we crown a new court jester?
