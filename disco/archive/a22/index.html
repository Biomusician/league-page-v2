2021 Disco Week 2 Preview

Disco Chat Vol 3.II “Sweet Dreams Are Made of These”

Crushing Reality Crusher or Crushee? Confirmation bias runs rampant in week 1 for winning teams, while losing teams dismiss poor performances as “flukes,” “bad games,” “slow starts,” or “one-offs.” What side you’re on depends on how your team performed, and how the adversary performed. If you won, revel in how your fantasy skill carried you to victory in the all-important, tone-setting first game of the season. You chose the right players, made the right start/sit calls, made prescient waiver adds, and all of it was instrumental in your victory. If you lose, comfort yourself in the fact that luck can play a large role in your fantasy football team’s finish. No matter how hard you try, which pair of socks you wear, or how many bad trade offers you send out, you can’t prevent a Ravens or SF RB from a season-ending injury. Or both GB Aarons from sucking. Speaking of sucking, PITCH or Babe DM me your Tinker address so I can send you the loser trophy. Ditto the Dude, so I can send you a freshly-minted winner’s trophy.

Matchup Roundup It’s Football Time! Lots of analysts are still holding onto their pre-season rankings, hoping that their guys will emerge. There have already been significant injuries, so some of you are suffering and trying to rebuild. Still, it’s too early to give up! Upset Special: McLovin (1-0) vs HOP (0-1) 62/38 HOP cannot afford to repeat his last place performance from two years ago, but he has a chance to turn things around against McLovin and the Kmet Me Bros. If Aaron Jones has another bad game for Kmet, or if Josh Allen has another pedestrian day, or Najee continues not to live up to the hype, then HOP is poised to strike with Lamar Jackson, CEH, Saquon (if healthy), and big-play DK Metcalf. For HOP, Godwin has cemented himself into the TB #1 WR role, and Fant should see more usage with Jerry Jeudy out. MG3 is a surprisingly strong option in Denver, and he also has Mike Davis and Kenny G, who had respectable week 1 offerings. Then again, McLovin’s rolling out Higgins, Swift, and Cooks, so it’ll be an uphill battle for sure. There’s upset potential here, but only because McLovin has the advantage. The Cowbear Kerfluffle: DIP (1-0) vs Juan (0-1) 55/45 DIP beared down week 1 to eke out a win, but he’s still 5th based on points scored. He’ll need strong performances from Patty Mahomes, Tony Gibson, and Chris Carson to put up a win against Juan’s old and broken Aaron Rodgers and Ben too-hard-to-spell-Burger. I seriously think A-Rod just came back to GB for a year to spite them with terrible games. Miles Sanders and James Robinson are ho-hum options that Juan will likely win in spite of, not because of. Then again, he does have Davante and DeAndre as his one-two receiving punch, and Thielen in the FLEX is strong as well. If Sterling Shepard continues to produce, he can be a strong FLEX as well. On DIP’s side of the ball, it’s hard to argue that the Hock is a poor TE option, and Diggs/McLaurin/Julio are no slouches either. It’ll be a close one, and will depend on if week 1 losers (read: Packers) can turn things around in week 2. If they do, DIP will be feeling double pain: Losing in fantasy and watching the Packers win. Civilian Special: Fingers (0-1) vs Babe (0-1) 52/48 Someone’s coming out of this game 0-2, so you know this’ll be a good fight. Though Babe has positional advantages at RB (Kamara/Ekeler over D-Henry and Gaskin) and WR (Jefferson over Amari Cooper), the depth pieces will likely turn the tide for Fingers, who is still sniffin’ for his first win. Travis Kelce, especially, will likely be key to his victory, though Mark Andrews for Babe is a strong option - just not godlike like Kelce. Babe is hoping Sutton continues to look solid and soaks up the missing targets from Jerry Jeudy’s absence, and that rookie DeVonta continues to step forward with Hurts in Philly. LASIK Jameis rounds out the bottom of Fingers’ roster, facing Joe Burrow who has to face a tough Bears defense. SNCO Scuffle: Sealed (1-0) vs EMCO (0-1) 53/47 Both teams could middle out at 1-1, or we could have another undefeated and another unwinning team, depending on how this shakes out. Los Vaqueros have a slide edge here due to their WR corps; give me Tyreek and Keenan over ARob and Diontae (almost injured!). Sealed is hoping Chase Claypool sneaks more targets away from Diontae and JuJu this week, and that Mike Williams lives up to his draft capital again this week and doesn’t go down with an injury, as is his custom. Gesicki is a yuck option for TE that EMCO should not feel comfortable starting, while Kyle Pitts saw decent involvement in the Falcons trashcan fire of an offense. Finally, Sealed has to pick between Jimmy G, who had a TD vulture by Lance last week, Zach Wilson, who looked solid but not super impressive, and Trevor Lawrence, who put up a Jameis-of-old-like line of 3 TDs and 3 INTs last week against a terrible Texans team. Newsflash: DEN will be a much tougher matchup, and TLaw is unlikely to succeed there. Hopefully, Sealed manages his team better than Urban is managing the Jags, who are poised for some more great draft picks. Tinker Tussle: The Dude (1-0) vs PITCH (1-0) 61/39 After overcoming the blowout special in a week 1 exhibition against EMCO, PITCH is looking to take her momentum, led by Kyler Murray (and despite Ezekiel Elliot) to knock The Dude out of the early undefeated club. The Dude’s RB’s, CMC and DMont, are definitely an advantage over PITCH’s Zeke and KHunt. DMont over KHunt all day! I’ll take AJB over CD in their WR1 spot, but it’s close. For WR2, Kupp over dependable Robert Woods puts The Dude over the top. They both have elite TEs: The Dude’s got Kittle, and the Browns has Waller, who caught 10 of his NINETEEN targets last week. Bonkers. It may come down to their FLEX options, and I think the Dude has the edge here, with Darrel Henderson, Chase Edmonds, and maybe Aiyuk/Hardman to round out against JuJu, Gronk, Gabe Davis, and Austin Hooper. Gets kinda ugly there at the end - BYE weeks are gonna be fun!
