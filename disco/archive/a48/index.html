2024 Disco Week 5

Disco Chat Vol 5.IV

The Lowdown Bye Time “Everyone has a plan until BYEs punch them in the face.” -Oscar Wilde

Week 5

HOP (2-2) vs The Dude (2-2) Both teams have starters on BYE, but The Dude is missing Mike Evans and Chris Godwin, forcing him to consider starting DeVante Parker and JuJu S-S alongside KJ Osborn. But, if anything can help him come back, it’s CMC, who has something like >30% of his team’s fantasy points. The only other RB in the top 10 of their team’s fantasy points is Bijan. Meanwhile, HOP may touch Kadarius Toney (don’t break him) and Jerick McKinnon to sub in for Zach Charbonnet and Rachaad White. He’s got Bijan. Battle of the RBs, baby!

Juan (0-4) vs Fingers (1-3) The Bears of this year are Juan’s Josh and Rookies, who are still waiting for the offseason expectations to pan out for his team. Sure, he’s got a ton of rookies, but it takes points to win games, and Gibbs, Tank Dell, Jordan Addison, and Roschon have a lot more potential than points. Josh Allen just hasn’t made a difference yet. But it’s not like he’s facing the league goliath, and he’s got no one on BYE. Fingers is 1-3, and Calvin Ridley has underperformed for him while Kirk Cousins has once again been okay, despite losing games. In fact, Kirk Cousins is the perfect champion for his 1-3 team. Bears vs Vikings - get up for it.

EMCO (3-1) vs Pitch (0-4) I thought Juan was the Bears but then I saw Pitch’s team and realized that he’s the Panthers. Pitch is the Bears this week, with Deshaun, Baker, JSN, Amari Cooper, and David Njoku all on BYE - that’s what happens when you draft the entire Browns offense, homer! Darius Slayton and Tyler Allgeier are filling in alongside Ryan Tannehill, and Zeke is gonna fill in for QB like he filled in for center on last year’s last Cowboys play. Meanwhile, EMCO’s looking to lay down the law with Hurts, Henry, ETN, and Evan Engram, who has surprisingly been the most consistent player on the mystifying Jags offense this year. With Saquon back, she could be nigh unstoppable (just like Zach Wilson wishes he were).

Sealed (2-2) vs Me (3-1) Welp, Herbert’s on BYE for Sealed, but that means Keenan’s on BYE for me - let’s call it even on the hamstringing here. Sealed may have some of the youngest QBs with Brock Purdy and Bryce Young in his lineup, while I’m hoping Joe Burrow can get sacked half as many times as Fields did last week and someone find Chase and Tyler Boyd this week. Do I feel great about my DJ Moore and Jaylen Waddle? No, no I don’t (Just kidding - I knew DJ Moore was gonna smash tonight and I’m totally prescient in starting him!) - especially against Sealed’s Davante Adams and Deebo Samuel. Our grossest starts will either be Antonio Gibson, Dalvin Cook (Breece no pitch count!), or Michael Gallup - time will tell!

DIP (3-1) vs POP (2-2) Holy Chad Hennessys batman! So many things went right for DIP early in the season: Puka Nacua, David Montgomery, Nico Collins, Anthony Richardson - it’s like looking a best of draft day super values, and I hate it. Even anchor Daniel Jones isn’t enough to drag this team down. Meanwhile, POP’s Patty Mahomes (worse than Zach Wilson last week btw) is looking to make the difference with Sam Howell, hoping that A-Rich’s 44% completion rate (#2 fantasy finish anyway) will finally tank his fantasy value this week. Garrett Wilson sadface. Noah Gray? Desperation play with the Muth out. Not lookin’ good for the dogs to pull one out here.

Babe (3-1) vs Double (3-1) Clash of the Titans (the historical ones, not the NFL team) this week, with two 3-1 teams squaring off. It’ll be close, with Babe missing KWIII to level the playing field against Double’s starters. Looking at their QB2s, that may be the difference maker position: Babe’s got up and comer CJ Stroud who looks amazing, against Double Derek Carr, who looks like the opposite of Jameis Winston (per FFBallers, Jameis is “chuck down” and Derek is “check down”). Lamar’s gotta be better than Double’s Russ (ltd), and while D’Andre Swift and Pacheco aren’t mountain moving superstars for Babe, they’re going up against James Cook and Miles Sanders for Double. The WRs favor Double, but who knows where the ball will go in SF, which means that Aiyuk could have a down game. If JT doesn’t start this week, it could get ugly for Babe quick, but if he comes out swinging, it could carry him to a dub.
