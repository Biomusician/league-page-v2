The Lowdown Just a Couple More One of you is only 2 weeks away from a fantasy championship. After all the injuries, the pickups, the difficult decisions, and the unfair matchups you’ve faced, you deserve it more than anyone - at least, that’s what you’ll be thinking. The rest of us know the truth: You’re just the luckiest among us, your roster is shit, and if we had another chance at it, you’d lose 9 times out of 10. And I guess you can just say to the rest of us, “Screw off whiner; I won the ones that mattered. Better luck next year.”

This Week’s Matchups Four Enter; Two Remain Anomalies (McLovin) 10-4 vs U.S. Trash (Karen) 10-3 (55/45) With the loss of Mike Evans, Anomalies will have to dig deep to win against the U.S. Trash, who largely avoided Sunday’s injury bloodbath. McLovin’s Chris Carson will see an increased workload with Penne out, and as long as those increased touches don’t result in more fumbles, that’s good news. Meanwhile, The Commish has Kittle coming off one of his most commanding performances of the season, and with the Chark attack out of commision, she looks to John Brown for another big performance. Hot Chubb Time Machine (Kelsey) 9-4 vs For Whom Odell Tolls (Ross) 8-6 (75/25) The Cinderella story continues, as Ross defies expectations and squeaks into the quarterfinals. It’s about as likely as the Cowboys or Eagles winning a playoff game this year. He’s playing against this league’s Niners/Saints in the Time Machine. Just because Kelsey enjoys positional advantages everywhere doesn’t mean it’s a lock. Maybe Chris Godwin won’t thrive without Mike Evans on the other side. Maybe Russell Wilson has another bad game. Maybe CMC scores fewer than 20 points this week. Haha maybe those first two happen, but not the third. Still: “So you’re saying there’s a chance?”

GM of the Week “Chicka chicka yeah! Fake ID, fake ID!.” - McLlovin I’ve been playing fantasy for 8 years, and have not even made it to a single championship. I keep playing for the same reason people keep drinking, playing the lottery, and rooting for the Redskins; it’s a dangerous mix of destructive habit, blind hope, and concentrated self-hatred. It’s also why my team is named the Anomalies, in that every year I hope my team outperforms expectations instead of crushing dreams. ABM buddies at Tyndall convinced me fantasy football was a better choice than dungeons and dragons, and while I’m still not sure they were right, it was definitely the smart social choice. The best footballers are value players, like the perennially underrated QBs who get taken in the 7th round - guys like Tony Romo and Jameis Winston who will be fine for your team most weeks and won’t break the draft bank. I hate players who are self-interested to the point where it hurts their team - guys like Zeke, Bell, AB are good, and everyone wants to get paid, but c’mon… My league rival is Bazinga by default because Kelsey, DIP, and KQII are too nice, Distro is too bad unfortunate, Blade is too disinterested, Karen and Ross are married, and Basic’s not been the same since he went to recruiting school.
