2021 Disco Week 10

Disco Chat Vol 3.X

The Lowdown Nothing Makes Sense In a week where nothing met expectations, WRs went crazy (but often not in a good way), the Jags beat the Bills (their first US win since 13 Sep 2020), the Broncos beat the Cowboys, the Chiefs beat the Packers, and the Giants beat the Raiders. This is the first time that four teams in first place lost to opponents with <0.500 records in the last decade. The MVP conversation has been blown wide open with Derrick Henry out, Kyler Murray out, Dak struggling, and KAaron Rodgers’ (Kirk Cousins’ new BFF) COVIDiotic press release. Another fun fact: Week 9 featured five Super Bowl rematches: Packers-Chiefs, Jets-Colts (lol), Cowboys-Broncos, Rams-Titans and Patriots-Panthers. The Chiefs, Colts, Titans and Broncos all got revenge, I guess. DPJ and James Connor are tearing it up, just like everyone predicted. But, my favorite moment had to be Josh Allen sacking, picking off, and recovering a fumble from Josh Allen.

Matchup Roundup

DIP (4-5) vs HOP (5-4) 51/49 DIP BYE/INJ: Toney, Mooney HOP BYE/INJ: Saquon, Kenny G HOP, you can let Ruggs go now. Why is he still on your team? That’s like hugging an anchor while the ship’s sinking. You’re 5-4, but a knife’s edge away from dropping this game to the big Dippers. HOP’s got the QB advantage in Lamar Jackson, while DIP’s hoping that Patrick Mahomes can get out of his slump and play good football again. He’s also hoping that Damien Harris clears the concussion protocol, otherwise, he’s starting D’Ernest Johnson and Antonio Gibson, which is the sauerkraut of RB starting lineups: Gross and fermented. When HOP’s not-all-star lineup of MGIII and MIII (Mark Ingram II) are kicking your team’s butt, you know you need help. HOP also has Mike Davis starting maybe, which is ass. Let’s hit the high points: For HOP, it’s Lamar, Pittman, Godwin, and Metcalf. For DIP, it’s Diggs, McLaurin (maybe), and the rookies Rashod and Devonta. The resurgent Ryan rounds out DIP’s lineup against a terrible-at-real-football-but-good-for-fantasy (TARFBGFF) Jalen Hurts. McLovin (7-2) vs Juan (1-8) 60/40 McLovin BYE/INJ: Brandin Cooks Juan BYE/INJ: Miles Sanders Another week, another potential Juan upset game. Where did he go wrong in the draft? Probably in his first 4 picks (Adams, Rodgers, Hopkins, Sanders), all of which have had less-than-perfect seasons. He’s also hoping that KAaron can play this week after his doses of homeopathy and ivermectin. If he manages to stop horsing around, maybe it will work out. Adams is definitely an upgrade over Tyler Lockett, though I prefer Mike Williams over Adam Thielen by a smidge this week. It’s in the RB department where he needs improvement. Robinson and Howard against Najee and Aaron Jones isn’t great. I’m really hoping that the Packers can establish the run against Seattle, and aren’t reliant on the passing game to score points. For FLEX options, it’s 50/50ish. Swift and Mitchell are preferred over Waddle and Renfrow, while Hopkins and Landry outshine Aiyuk and Jefferson. I clearly have the more dedicated QB2, Carson Wentz, who recently stated that he’ll miss the birth of his next child to play on Sunday. Definitely #wtf, but an upgrade over Teddy B. If I lose this game, it’ll be a shame, because I’ll have no one to blame but myself. What tips it in my favor is Pat Freiermuth - the Muth is looth baby! Sealed (5-4) vs Fingers (6-3) 55/45 Sealed BYE/INJ: DeVante Parker Fingers BYE/INJ: Derrick Henry =(, Tyler Boyd Without Derrick Henry, Fingers dropped a close game to EMCO last week, and he may drop another one to Sealed here, thanks to the non-effort of Myles Gaskin, who turned 20 opportunities on the ground into 30-something yards last week. Maybe Kelce will bail him out against Pitts, but Sealed has the TD master James Conner sitting in his flex waiting to score another 3-5 TDs this week on as many carries. In another RB positional advantage, Sealed has surprise journeyman Cordarrelle Patterson in his RB2 spot, after a trade where he gave up Mike Williams (not my proudest moment losing out on the RB7 in PPR). The most interesting part of both rosters is that they have 3 players whose names start with the same letter: Marquise, Mike, and Myles for Fingers and James, Jimmy, and Javonte for Sealed. Will the Js or the Ms triumph? My money’s on the team that’s missing their big D, king Henry. EMCO (6-3) vs Pitch (1-8) 74/26 EMCO BYE/INJ: Mixon, Ja’Marr Chase Pitch BYE/INJ: Fields, Fuller V, JuJu, Higgins, Kareem Hunt If Juan’s team is a dumpster fire, then Pitch’s team is a trash safari, especially with so many players on BYE for her this week. First, some roster mockery for both teams. EMCO, what is Allen Robinson still doing on your bench, especially after he got beat out by Mooney last week? Stop trying to make fetch happen and drop him. Also, your hope springs eternal for Bryan Edwards. The most egregious hold has got to be the Red Rocket, who’s done nothing since Fields took the field, and won’t see it again unless Fields gets injured. For Pitch, please pitch out Colt McCoy if Kyler starts this week, and maybe decide who of Hooper or Gronk is going to stay on your bench, and who’s getting cut. I’ll allow ancient David Johnson to remain, but only because your starting options are either on IR or on BYE, leaving you with Zeke and Pollard (power pair) in your starting RB spots, and forcing you to flex Hunter Henry, the Wee Little Man (Zaccheaus), and Sony Michel. EMCO’s got you flat-out beat in the flex. The only bright spots on your roster are AJB and Waller. Still, it’s fantasy football, and the Jags can beat the Bills, so who knows anymore. The Dude (4-5) vs Babe (6-3) 60/40 The Dude BYE/INJ: Danny Dimes, David Montgomery Babe BYE/INJ: Burrow, Logan Thomas The Dude is beating out Babe positionally everywhere except RB2 (Kamara vs Henderson) and a couple flex spots (Marvin Jones and McKissic against Elijah Moore and Higbee). Still, all is not lost, as Kittle’s not a sure bet against Andrews, Ekeler could easily outscore CMC (well, maybe not easily), and The Dude’s Dak may have another disastrous game against a soft Atlanta defense. If they do, his CeeDee Lamb pick will suffer as well. Still, he’s got the safety blanket of Cooper Kupp to fall back on, and even though Babe has Justin Jefferson working against a solid Chargers defense, it probably won’t get him over the edge. This matchup hinges largely on how DAL/ATL, MIN/LAC, and LAR/SF go. The Dude’s got Dak, Kupp, Lamb, Kittle, and Higbee, while Babe’s rocking Herbert, Ekeler, and Jefferson. Finally, The Dude is sending in the Carr with Derek and the Wallers, while Babe’s leaning on the Mac truck in NE to get things done.
