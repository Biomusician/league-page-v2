2023 Disco Week 3

Disco Chat Vol 4.II

The Lowdown - Pittman Edition Yeah, So I Missed a Week It happens to all of us, even your commish. Except I wasn’t down with a bum ankle or a hammy, but TDYs and random acts of Tinker. In any case, I’m back, with little-to-no long term effects, other than sadness that the volume number won’t match the week number this year. Oh well! Enough with the intro; let’s dive into the matchups!

Week 3

McLovin vs HOP (55/45) HOP has me beat at RB with #1 overall JT and a resurgent CEH vs my underperforming Dalvin Cook and Breece Hall, who hasn’t hit the ground running with the Jets yet. I blame EMCO almost exclusively for this. Chase Claypool also didn’t get off to a great start against the Browns, and I have Stafford with rocks in his elbow or something against Tuddy Tua. Still, I like my chances, with Chase stacking up well against Deebo, and Amon-Ra hitting against Thielen. We probably have the grossest QB2 matchup in the league, with his Mac Jones punting against my Daniel Jones, who hopefully won’t trip over his own feet this week.

DIP vs Juan (70/30) DIP at 2-0 is facing off against the 0-2 Hopes and Dreams, and it’s not looking great for Juan to improve to 1-2, especially with Pickens putting up only 5.4 points for him. Diggs, Allen Robinson II, and Aiyuk will have to put up monster numbers alongside Justin Herbert to get him over the top. Or My Name is Jeff Wilson will have to step up to outscore DIP’s D’Andre Swift, who had one of the best speeches ever recorded in a Hard Knocks, and is a gritty Lion. DIP’s got Gabe Davis. He’s got Michael Pittman coming off injury. And of, course, he’s got Darnell Mooney because he’s a homer. Finally, he has the #1 and #2 QBs this week in Josh Allen and Jalen Hurts. It’s uphill sledding both ways in the snow with a headwind for Juan to come out on top this week.

The Dude vs Fingers (65/35) "Let's ride!" was supposed to be the rallying cry, but instead it’s what Sniffin has been yelling into the TV every time Russell Wilson doesn’t complete a pass, which has been a lot. We have a matchup between two winless teams on tap here, and looking at their rosters, it’s easy to see why. Fingers has Keenan Allen (injured), Antonio Gibson (who is relevant only because his challenger at RB got shot), Derrick Henry (who hasn’t panned out), and the aforementioned Unlimited. Meanwhile, The Dude struck gold with a late-round Saquon who he’s hoping shows up in week 1 (not week 2) form, J-Jeff and Tee Higgins, and Garrett Wilson. Then again, if Josh Jacobs doesn’t play, he may be forced to choose one of Ken Walker, Eno Benjamin, or Nico Collins to start, who are to flex spots like circus peanuts or candy corn is to Halloween.

EMCO vs Sealed (51/49) EMCO really does love the Chubb, especially after last week’s blow up performance. She’s got her work cut out for her this week against Ekeler/Javonte for Sealed, especially with Kelce and Waller starting for him as well. Can Zach Ertz turn in a bonkers game at TE? Probably not, but he won’t goose her team either. With only 17.3 fantasy points from Chubb, she’ll be hoping Cooper Kupp and Mike Williams go off, against Sealed’s Brandin Cooks and JuJu. And I lied when I said we had the worst QB2 matchup, because EMCO started Trubicuit against Geno Smith and that’s garbage tier.

Babe vs Pitch (68/32) Oh man, you love to see it: The husband/wife duo square off to see who will start the season 2-1, and who goes under 0.500. Sorry Pitch, my money’s on the Fatties to make this happen, led by Lamar Jackson, AJB, and a late-round dart throw on Michael Thomas that seems to have worked out. Plus, he’s got Mark Andrews against your Kyle Pitts, who hasn’t really panned out these first couple weeks. Yes, you have CMC, but David Montgomery has been surprisingly sturdy in a terrible Bears offense these first couple weeks, and you really only have advantage in your deeper FLEX options. Julio Jones may not play this week for the Fatties, so he may have to FLEX Logan Thomas or Marvin Jones, both of whom should make you throw up in your mouth a little. If you lose, then it’s Babe’s fault for drafting you a bad team - remember that throughout the season.

Double vs POP (73/27) Patrick Mahomes and POP - name a more iconic duo. Well, maybe Pikachu and POP, for reasons that aren’t clear to me. After the Browns/Steelers turned out to be a low-scoring carnival ride of a game, the Dogs will need some Dawg in ‘em after three of his starters put up disappointing weeks (Najee, Diontae, Friermuth). You, sir, are a fool for investing so heavily in a Trubisky-led offense. Still, Waddle may bail you out with another multiple TD performance, though he’ll have to do it against a stiff BUF secondary. Really, you’re just relying on your boy Mahomes to steer you to victory, because Curtis Samual and Greg Dortch probably aren’t gonna get it done after Double’s Amari dropped 19.6 on you from Cleveland. Finally, Rodgers may be last season’s MVP, but he’s throwing to a bunch of nobodies, and even though Double’s Joe Burrow has been turnover central these last few weeks, he’ll probably rebound against the Jets, who are like the trampoline of DSTs (sorry EMCO, but it’s true).
