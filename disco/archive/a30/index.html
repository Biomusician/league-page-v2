2021 Disco Week 11

Disco Chat Vol 3.X

The Lowdown It’s Too Late Rhamondre and D’Ernest and Jakobi, oh my! Like the Patriots WR who set a record for most number of yards gained without a TD (1,522), Babe and Fingers are racing towards their first ever championship chance. And, just like Jakobi Meyers, they may be relying on backups and garbage time to do it - but hey, that’s fantasy football. Maybe you’re like me and you once were all that like Cam Newton, and are coming back and trying to be relevant again with your old self. Or maybe you’re just like The Dude (Glory)/Antonio Gibson and know you’re so much better than your fantasy performance this season is letting on. Or maybe, finally, you’re like Juan, who is probably old, but is still doing some pretty solid work. Or maybe you’re just Miles Sanders/PITCH, and things never really got going for you this year, but you’re still going to step back into things and keep fighting and hope your team finds a way to be relevant. In any case, you may feel like it’s too late to lose your week 1 playoff BYE, but things are pretty tight this year. Anyone still has time to make the playoffs (even you DIP), and anyone can still fall from grace - I was going to say “even you, HOP,” but you’re barely hanging on at 5-5.

Matchup Roundup

DIP (5-5) vs Juan (1-9) 54/46 DIP BYE/INJ: Julio Juan BYE/INJ: OBJ? Juan, DIP needs this win to stay relevant. Are you going to roll over like a dead fish and give it to him? After Damien Harris only put up 7 on TNF, Juan’s got a chance, relying heavily on his Davante Adams and Aaron Rodgers stack to get him there against Patrick Mahomes and Antonion Gibson for DIP. All of those players have had disappointing and monster games over the past few weeks, so it’s anyone’s guess as to who’s got this one bagged. DIP’s lacking depth, which shows as he’s relying on a few young players in Mooney and Bateman to get the job done against higher-ranked options like Renfrow and Landry. If this game comes down to who does better between Cam (truly a QB Nursing Home choice) and Tua (DIP), it will be a perfect game. This is young vs old - will the generational team win? Babe (6-4) vs Fingers (6-4) 63/37 Babe BYE/INJ: Courtland Sutton Fingers BYE/INJ: Derrick Henry (Let him go, man) Oh baby, we’ve got a 6-4 showdown! This was anyone’s game going into Thursday night, but now Babe has some work to do after Mac Jones only put up 11.38 fantasy points as his QB1. Tom Brady is going against his kryptonite the Giants, but you gotta imagine he at least tosses a couple TDs, even if teams have historically enjoyed running against the NYG. Ekeler and Carter are far and away better options at RB for Babe than Josh Jacobs and Gaskin for Fingers, but the TE (Kelce vs Andrews) and FLEX options (Cooper/Kirk/Foreman vs Marvin/Marquez/McKissic) tilt in Fingers’ favor, and give him the edge going into Sunday’s game. Kamara and Herbert could be the saving grace for Babe, if they come back healthy and play like their elite talent suggests they could. EMCO (7-3) vs Sealed (6-4) 80/20 EMCO BYE/INJ: NSTR (Le’Veon Bell lol) Sealed BYE/INJ: Cordarelle Patterson DeVante Parker, Tim Patrick, Javonte Williams Once again, this game went a lot more heavily in EMCO’s favor after the Thursday game went through, thanks to Sealed leaving in Cordarelle Patterson, who was declared out prior to game start. It also doesn’t help that his TE, Kyle Pitts, only put up 4.4 points. Russ, Tyreek, and Dalvin will have to be cooking on Sunday to get him back even with EMCO’s studs, who include JT, Chubb, Deebo, Chase, and Mixon, alongside Fournette and Diontae Johnson. It’s possible, but starting the week with a big fat zero in one of your starting spots hurts almost as much as getting a wax strip ripped off your hairy ass. And it would also hurt missing the playoffs by just one game if Sealed loses, or missing a week 1 playoff BYE if EMCO loses. The Dude (5-5) vs Pitch (1-9) 68/32 The Dude BYE/INJ: Chase Edmonds, Cooper Kupp, Tyler Higbee Pitch BYE/INJ: Fuller V, JuJu, Higgins, Kareem Hunt PITCH, please. You got last last year - try to be better this year. You’re not favored against the Dude, but he barely drafted or managed his team, so please pray that Kyler plays, that Zeke is better than Pollard, that AJB smashes, that Tee Higgins outperforms Chase, and Darren Waller performs like the TE you drafted, not the TE he’s been the last 10 weeks. Ew, you’re also starting Tony Pollard, so that’s not great. Woof. For The Dude, you’ve gotta be happy with Dak, CMC, and Montgomery headlining for you, with DeeDee, Kittle, and some rookie talent in your FLEX spots. I feel like the entire outcome of this match will be decided by how the Cowboys decide to score your points. This is the match that the Hand household should be watching, and hoping (for max Hand happiness) that the Cowboys smash in the run game not the pass game. Pitch, you can play spoiled and ruin the Dude’s playoff chances here. Don’t pass up this opportunity to be relevant in our league. McLovin (8-2) vs HOP (5-5) 55/45 McLovin BYE/INJ: Aaron Jones, Calvin Ridley, Van Jefferson HOP BYE/INJ: MGIII, Noah Fant HOP is still not favored, but Rhamondre didn’t smash as hard as I expected with Damien Harris back in the lineup. A lot of this will come down to which QBs perform better. Will Lamar Jackson continue to kill it against a soft Bears DST, or will Josh Allen toss a ton of points against the prancing Colts? Will Jalen Hurts continue to be good at fantasy while being bad at football, or will Tannehill continue to produce without Henry and Ridley? The WR advantage goes to HOP, with Godwin and man-baby Metcalf against BOB (Boom or Bust) Lockett and Cooks (not the RB). I am praying to the God of Canada that the Muth is Luth on Sunday, and that Ertz does nothing. Ertz/Hurts isn’t a stack, but it would be fun if it were. Mike Williams is unexciting for me, but I’ve got Najee and D’Andre against Saquon and Mark Ingram (lol). If Kamara plays and is healthy, then Ingram will be back to backup status. It’s going to be a close game, and HOP needs this one to stay alive in the playoff hunt.
