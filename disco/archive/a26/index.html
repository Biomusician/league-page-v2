2021 Disco Week 7

Disco Chat Vol 3.VII

The Lowdown Pocket Lint Sometimes when you put on an old sweater or jacket, the pockets are full of dryer lint. Other times, you’ll pull out an old, crumpled $20 bill. It’s never happened to me, but I’ve seen it in a lot of movies. Well, think of this newsletter as an old quarter you found in your favorite pair of Saturday pants - not super useful or life changing, but a finger on the scale that tips it towards being a better day. That day is about to be ruined, just like your fantasy hopes this week. If you haven’t already noticed, your entire team is on BYE or injured. Also, it’s too late to fix it, so buckle up and take that L on the chin.

Matchup Roundup The Great BYEPOCALYPSE HOP (3-3) vs PITCH (1-5) 62/38 HOP BYE/INJ: Saquon, CEH, Zack Moss, Kenny G, Laviska, Terrace Marshall PITCH BYE/INJ: Zeke, Pollard, Mattison, Fuller, JuJu, Gabe Davis, Gronk HOP’s team literally only has one bench player, and Noah Fant already played, so he has no fill in if someone pops up on the injury report today. PITCH isn’t doing much better, with only Jared Goff to fill in for Kyler Murray if his recent injury gives her cold feet. Olamide Zacheaus is on her bench, but his name is almost as terrifying as the prospect of putting him in your starting lineup. The pocket lint that lines the jackets of these lineups includes Kenneth G and Mark Ingram for HOP, and recently-acquired Tee Higgins (goodbye, my sweet prince), Trey Sermon (doghouse), and Justin Fields (Da Bears) for Pitch. Verdict: Brown sportcoats with large balls of velvet pocket lint EMCO (4-2) vs Juan (0-6) 62/38 EMCO BYE/INJ: Cousins, Dalton, Chubb, TY, Cole, Diontae J, Jared Cook Juan BYE/INJ: Big Ben, James Robinson, Adam Thielen, Cedrick Wilson, Dawson Knox No real superstars are sitting out for either team, but the starters are uninspiring as well. One has to ask whether Juan has just given up on this team and this league; they’re the equivalent of wearing a sport coat with no pants, and with his starting DPJ on Thursday (0 points), he’s completing the look with a clown nose. Davante Adams and Deandre Hopkins may not be able to save his team’s chances, and he may move to become the first 0-7 team that I’ve ever played in a league with. We may have to create a special award to recognize his lack of achievement. On the other hand, EMCO is being forced to start Allen Robinson and AJ Dillon, and maybe even Bryan Edwards. Verdict: Comfy jackets with pockets filed with paper bits that have been through the wash. DIP (4-2) vs Fingers (5-1) 52/48 DIP BYE/INJ: Carson, Diggs, Gallup, Kadarius Fingers BYE/INJ: Singletary, Amari Cooper, Jeudy Neither team is missing much here, and the BYEPOCALYPSE is leaving both teams largely untouched. Sniffin wins is like a fashionable dude at a bar, with a complete outfit topped by the eternal Tom Brady, the dashing Derrick Henry, perennial-but-unexciting Mike Evans, and the stunning centerpiece Travis Kelce, who really brings the entire outfit together. Meanwhile, DIP is missing the watch that tied his whole look together in Stefon Diggs, forcing him to scramble to grab the old Fossil watch he bought when we was back in college trying to look fashionable on a budget (Jakobi Meyers). Can Patrick Mahomes carry him to a victory? Can the hat he found at Goodwill (D’Ernest Johnson, 23.8 points) work with the rest of his lineup and drive him to fashion greatness? Time will tell. Verdict: Thrift store getups with old grocery lists in the pockets. McLovin (4-2) vs The Dude (3-3) 55/45 McLovin BYE/INJ: Josh Allen, Najee Harris, MT, Trey Lance, Mike Williams, Curtis Samuel, Dan Arnold The Dude BYE/INJ: Dak, CMC, David Montgomery, Sammy Watkins, Emmy Sanders, CeeDee, Kittle This is like watching a male fashion show, except the models are just wearing swimsuits instead of entire getups - that, or they’re just coming out in Hanes shirts and Underarmor shorts because their luggage was lost during their flight over. The Dude only has 3 starting options available, and they all suck (RoJo, Amon-Ra, Rudolph), and I have none. I’m starting Corey Davis and maybe even Captain America (Chris Evans), and it sucks. Meanwhile, the Dude has to rely on Aiyuk, Khalil Herbert, Robby Anderson, and Mecole Hardman. This is just the worst of both teams. Verdict: Used socks filled with tiny rocks Babe (3-3) vs Sealed (3-3) 50/50 Babe BYE/INJ: Russell Wilson, Trevor Lawrence, Latavius, Dalvin Cook, Keenan Allen, DJ Chark, Chase Claypool, Dalton Schultz Sealed BYE/INJ: Baker Mayfield, Justin Herbert, Austin Ekeler, Marvin Jones, AB, Parris Campbell, Justin Jefferson, Logan Thomas I thought I was hard up for starters, but that was before I saw both of these teams. Oof. Imagine that you get mugged while walking down the street, and your clothes get stolen. Then, a homeless individual takes pity on you, and dresses you in the clothes that they’ve decided are past their prime and don’t need anymore. That’s what starting Nelson Agholor, Jalen Reagor, Zach Wilson, and DeVante Parker is like. Shame, Vaqueros. Meanwhile, Babe’s team is sporting the hospital gown that is Michael Carter, JD McKissick, Mac Jones, and Marquez Callaway. Just gross, and definitely leaves little to the imagination for how ugly this week is. Verdict: Stained hospital gown vs homeless hand-me-downs
