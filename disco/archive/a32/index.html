2021 Disco Week 13

Disco Chat Vol 3.XIII

The Lowdown Luck Be a-Lazy How unlucky have you been this season? Just for fun, I went back and examined the first 10 RBs off the board in redraft this year, and then went to see how many had missed weeks due to injury or sickness. Unless you drafted Najee, Ekeler, or Antonio Gibson there, you’ve been disappointed. So, there’s only a 30% chance of not being completely screwed; you can either take solace in the fact that you’re not alone, or hate everyone who still has depth at running back. If you had either Swift, Waller, Jacobs, Lamb, CMC, Sermon, the Muth, Deebo, or Dalvin, you were probably not very thanks-giving. If you had one of the four 100-yard receivers in the Cowboys game (Renfrow, DeSean, Gallup, Wilson) then you were happy. Ditto Pollard, Cordarrelle, Mixon, Jarvis, Fournette, OBJ, Van J, or a Titans RB. Finally, RIP to the Cam, Hurts, and Engram owners. Really, fantasy is all about managing disappointment; it’s an exercise in resilience that we continue to volunteer for every year. Our teams never quite live up to our expectations, but we soldier on. TYFYS everyone. o7

Matchup Roundup

HOP (5-7) vs Babe (7-5) 55/45 HOP BYE/INJ: NSTR Babe BYE/INJ: Kamara, Chuba, AB While it’s still theoretically possible that Babe could secure a week 1 playoff BYE, he’d need to overcome a 200 point deficit with EMCO’s team to get there, and win both games, with EMCO losing both games. More realistically, he’s fighting to secure a playoff spot with two 5-7 teams trying to claw their way into the playoffs, and The Dude sitting at 6-6 right on the edge of the playoff cliff. HOP has a chance to better his chances here, with positional advantages at QB (Lamar vs Herbert), and FLEX (Metcalf/MGIII/Ingram/Kenny G vs a horrific Sutton/Marvin/Logan/McKissic for Babe). Injuries and BYEs have wrecked Babe, and he’s going to be hard pressed to put up enough points, heavily leaning on Ekeler, J-Jeff, and Mark Andrews to close the gap for his lack of depth talent. Sealed (7-5) vs Juan (63-9) 70/30 Sealed BYE/INJ: Dalvin Cook, DJ Moore Juan BYE/INJ: Rodgers, Davante, Jarvis Landry Juan’s chances of playing spoiler are spoiled without his two big GB players, Rodgers and Davante. Even though Sealed is missing Dalvin and DJ Moore, he’s heavily favored against Juan’s ragtag group. Sealed has positional advantage at QB, RB, and WR, and even though his depth falls off a little after that, it’s no pushover: Javonte and Chase Claypool are fine options, and Dalton Schultz continues to put up good numbers with Dak. James Conner and Cordarrelle form an unlikely pair of stars against the higher-drafted James Robinson and Miles Sanders for Juan. Ultimately, Juan is hoping that Waddle puts up another star performance, Renfrow going for another 100 yards, and OBJ continues to show out with Danny Dimes. Juan, you can’t make the playoffs anymore, but you can ruin someone else’s hopes and dreams, and if that’s not fantasy football at its core, I don’t know what is. McLovin (10-2) vs Fingers (7-5) 53/47 McLovin BYE/INJ: Tannehill, Aaron Jones Fingers BYE/INJ: Derrick Henry (RIP) Once again, we have a 7-5 time vying to keep a playoff spot. I’ve already got a week 1 BYE locked up, but I would love to make Fingers’ journey to the playoffs harder, and let a weaker team squeak by into the big matches so it’s easier cleanup later. He’s hoping that Tom Brady bounces back against a softer ATL team, after a game where Fournette scored all the TDs. He’s also hoping Evans and Hollywood show out, and that Kelce outperforms whatever garbage TE I can muster off the waiver wire. Sure, I’ve got more depth that he does with my WRs and RB, while he has to start Jeudy, Kirk, and Singletary. I Jacobs and Gaskin can get going against Washington and the Giants, respectively, then maybe Fingers has a chance to solidify his playoff push. EMCO (9-3) vs The Dude (6-6) 63/37 EMCO BYE/INJ: Chubb, Deebo The Dude BYE/INJ: CMC, Chase Edmonds EMCO loves Chubb, but he’s on BYE, so she’ll be relying on JT and Mixon to get it done for her against Darrel Henderson and David Montgomery for the Dude. This is her chance to lock up a week 1 BYE opposite McLovin, while the Dude is fighting for his playoff life. Both players are missing starters, but the depth of EMCO’s roster is shining through. The Dude’s hoping for a classic Kittle game where he scores a bunch of points, not a this season Kittle game where he draws a lot of defensive attention but doesn’t get a lot of targets or catches. He also has the WR1 on the week, Cooper Kupp, so there’s that. Emmanuel Sanders is one of the lone brighter spots in his FLEX, as EMCO has him outclassed with Fournette, and a recently revitalized Gage and Hilton. This one has big playoff indications, so hopefully both teams show up ready to scrap! DIP (5-7) vs Pitch (1-11) 76/24 DIP BYE/INJ: Carson, Julio Pitch BYE/INJ: Her entire bench DIP needs this win, and fortunately he’s against the front-runner for repeat last-place finishes, Pitch. Is it her fault that the only positional advantage she has is at TE, where she’s got Gronk going against the Hock for DIP? Yeah, it probably is. Sure, she couldn’t have predicted that her entire team would be injured, but now here she is, relying once against on a simultaneous Zeke/Pollard start to get it done against Mattison and Gibson for DIP, and Higgins/Gabe Davis against Diggs and McLaurin. She’s also starting washed star David Johnson, and flexing Hunter “I hope he scores a TD here” Henry. Jared Goff rounds out her unexciting roster against DIP’s Tua, who just set a record for consecutive games with >30 passing attempts and >80% accuracy. Pitch, you’ve got your work cut out for you; I hope you beat DIP because that would be just perfect fantasy football
