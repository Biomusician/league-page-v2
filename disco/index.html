Draft Issue · 2026-08-30

The Lowdown

Disco Chat is back on the air for the 8th season, and the initial picture is exactly as cluttered as you’re used to with BC3. Here’s how every season actually starts. When an ACS (especially if it used to be an OSS) first stands up, it’s chaos: every track is ambiguous and half the returns are spoofed or erroneous, Our draft this year was the same. Twelve of us dropped 228 tracks on the scope in one night, every single one declared friendly by the (battle?) manager who put it there.. The season is the judge; by Halloween we’ll know who spent the draft deccing chaff. I am solidly middle-of-the-pack per my rankings, which balances my core belief that I am a fantasy drafting god with the reality that I’ve finished last more times than I’d ever admit publicly.

The full draft board, with every pick’s delta against consensus rank, is on the site, and so is the archive: every recoverable issue of Disco Chat. Good luck to everyone aside from my opponents, to whom I wish a season of very average (or below average) performances. Godspeed everyone! Matchup write-ups next week!
