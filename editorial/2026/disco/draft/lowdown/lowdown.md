# The Lowdown

## Vol 7.I: Establishing the Picture

Disco Chat is back on the air for Volume 7, and the initial picture is
exactly as messy as you'd hope.

Here's how every season actually starts. When a CRC first stands up, the
scope is chaos: every track is ambiguous, half the returns are dupes, and
three different controllers swear their correlation is the right one. Then
the picture settles, and you learn which tracks were real threats and which
ones were a flock of geese with good PR. A draft is the same event. Twelve
of us dropped 228 tracks on the scope in one night, every single one
declared friendly by the manager who hung it there. The season is the
settle. By Halloween we'll know who spent August tracking geese.

Three correlations deserve the early call-out, and Highlander rules say the
commissioner goes first.

One: I drafted four Jacksonville Jaguars. Tuten, Washington, Meyers,
Strange. That is the only four-player NFL stack in this league, which means
I looked at a Jacksonville offense and said "yes, more of that, four times."
I am totally objective and this was a great idea.

Two: TheLovelands took Jahdae Walker at pick 145, which is 244 picks ahead
of his consensus rank; the next-boldest pick in the whole draft cleared
consensus by only 141. Either they know something the entire scouting
community missed, or we just watched a man ford the river with the wagon
fully loaded. We've been settin' grooves on this trail since Vol 3; you all
know how the crossing usually goes.

Three: while the room was reaching, Roster 6 harvested quarterbacks.
Daniel Jones 68 picks after his consensus rank, Malik Willis 58 after his.
In a superflex league! Somebody read the league settings twice.

The scoreboard goes live Thursday. The full draft board, with every pick's
delta against consensus rank, is on the site, and so is the archive: every
recoverable issue, back through the wagon-ruts era. The BYEpocalypse will
arrive on schedule this fall; consider yourselves warned. Good luck to all
twelve of you, except my Week 1 opponent, who I wish a season of very
average performances starting immediately.
