# Three Lowdown frames for the Surfeit Draft Issue

## 1. Assumption-Based Planning (institutional / Futures frame)

Premise: a draft is a published list of planning assumptions, and the season
is the red team. Every roster in this league just went on record: the Entente
assumed San Francisco and Buffalo stay healthy, Roster 4 assumed defenses are
a streamable commodity, I assumed tight end is optional until round 14. Open
on the doctrine, walk three assumption classes, land on the receipts: this
league now tracks takes, and False Assumptions will be reading them back all
season. Payoff turn: the wargame starts Thursday, and somebody's assumptions
die first contact.

Carries from PREP: the Jordan James reach, the TE-at-14 self-roast, the
zero-DEF roster, tracked-take infrastructure.

## 2. First exercise after the reorg (shared Air Force experience frame)

Premise: Week 1 of a brand-new league is the first exercise after a
squadron reorg. Everyone has a shiny org chart, nobody's checklists match
reality, and the schedule doesn't care. Develop with the two co-managed
crews working out C2 relationships in real time, the five rosters that
haven't named themselves yet (the unit patch is still at the print shop),
and the drafts as hastily-written OIs. Payoff: the exercise white cell
(me, the newsletter) grades everything.

Carries from PREP: co-managed teams, unnamed rosters, draft anomalies.

## 3. Interwar rearmament (extended historical analogy frame)

Premise: drafting before Week 1 is force structure procurement between the
wars: you are betting steel today on the war you think is coming. Carriers
or battleships; RB depth or a Chicago passing bet; two defenses or zero.
Develop three procurement bets from the actual draft, concede the
counterargument (interwar planners mostly guessed wrong and won anyway with
industrial depth, i.e., the waiver wire), payoff on whose fleet is built for
the wrong war. Turn back: Week 1 is the shakedown cruise.

Carries from PREP: concentration bets (Entente), divestment bets (Roster 4),
timing bets (Mahomes at pick 114).
