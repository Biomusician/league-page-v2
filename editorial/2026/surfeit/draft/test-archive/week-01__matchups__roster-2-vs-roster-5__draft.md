## The Chicago Syndicate vs Goff Season

Both of these teams waited on quarterback; only one married an entire offense while waiting. The Syndicate holds Caleb Williams, Colston Loveland, and Luther Burden, a three-man Bears stack whose QB arrived at pick 96, a full 29 spots below FantasyPros' consensus. When Chicago cooks, this roster eats twice. When Chicago is Chicago, there's also a Denver annex (Dobbins, Sutton, the Broncos DST) to keep the lights on, plus Mike Washington taken 50 picks early as the swing at a lottery ticket.

Goff Season diversified instead: Jonathan Taylor at 1.04, the Herbert-to-McConkey stack, then Goff himself at pick 137, 28 below sticker, as the insurance QB. Two starting-caliber quarterbacks for the price of one late-round sandwich. The kicker went 95 picks early, but kickers are a faith-based community. Lean Syndicate if the Bears show up; lean the field if they don't.
