# Rough outline - "Every Draft Is a List of Assumptions"

1. **Premise up front (conclusion first).** A draft is a set of published planning assumptions; the season exists to red-team them. This league now writes its assumptions down, which is going to be a problem for all of us.

2. **Three assumption classes, announced as three** (his think-in-threes,
   used because it genuinely fits):
- *Concentration bets* - L'entente Discordiale holds three 49ers and three Bills; two NFL games a week now carry most of that season. Jordan James at pick 123 against a consensus rank of 254 is the same bet with the volume turned up.
   - *Divestment bets* - Roster 4 (name pending) drafted zero defenses in
     fifteen picks. Divest-to-invest is real doctrine; this is divest-to-vibes.
- *Timing bets* - self-roast FIRST: I assumed tight end is optional until round 14, and the league median said round 7. Then Swifty Mahomey, who wore Mahomes' name all August and let him sit until pick 114.

3. **Concede-then-answer.** Still, assumption-heavy plans win wars all the time; the waiver wire is industrial mobilization, and half these bets will get bailed out by October.

4. **The receipts turn.** The difference this season: assumptions are being
   tracked. False Assumptions will read them back, verbatim, with dates.
   Mine included.

5. **Payoff + transition.** The wargame starts Thursday; somebody's
   assumptions die first contact. On to the hardware and the capsules.

6. **Close generously, then undercut** (issue-level close lives in the final
   section; the Lowdown hands off to the issue).
