## Wild SeeKats vs the Discount Warehouse

Week 1 opens with a clean philosophical split. The SeeKats, our only two-headed command structure, drafted like an audit was coming: Gibbs at 1.01, A.J. Brown at 2.10, and never a skill player more than 14 spots off FantasyPros' consensus board all night. That is the most disciplined card in the league, and with two managers signing every pick, somebody was always checking the math.

The Discount Warehouse drafted like the fiscal year was ending. Trevor Lawrence arrived 37 picks below sticker, Josh Jacobs 28, Terry McLaurin 24, Tee Higgins 18. Two of the draft's three biggest bargains live on one bench now. Whether a roster assembled entirely at clearance prices actually starts well is the question Week 1 gets to answer; consensus boards don't score points, but they don't fumble either.

The SeeKats bring the surer floor and a 1.01 that requires zero imagination. Still, if the bargain bin hits, the Warehouse turns a slot-10 draft into a top-3 roster. Good luck to both crews, and to whichever SeeKat loses the coin flip on lineup day.
