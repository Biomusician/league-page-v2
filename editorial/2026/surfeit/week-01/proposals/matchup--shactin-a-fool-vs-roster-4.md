## SHACtin a fool vs the No-DEF Department

Force Design says divest legacy capability and accept risk where the fight lets you. The No-DEF Department read that memo and divested an entire warfighting function: fifteen picks, zero defenses. That is not a typo; the roster carries seven wideouts, a kicker taken at pick 149, and no DST at all. Bold. The rest of the portfolio explains the confidence, because Brock Bowers at pick 29 was 11 spots of value, Marvin Harrison at 89 was 20, and the Dak-to-CeeDee stack came in 17 below sticker.

SHACtin a fool went the other direction and double-stocked the divested capability: two defenses, Seattle and Houston, an attrition reserve for a position most of us stream. Around them sits a genuinely balanced force: Bijan at 1.02, Lamar at exactly sticker price, a Dart-to-Nabers stack bought 23 spots cheap, and Kyle Pitts as the round-11 TE flier.

One team accepted risk; one team bought redundancy. Week 1 tells us whose risk assessment survives contact.
