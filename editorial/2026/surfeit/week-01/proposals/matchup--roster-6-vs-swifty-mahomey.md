## The Motor City Annex vs Swifty Mahomey

Swifty Mahomey painted the tail flash before the jet was delivered. The team wore Patrick Mahomes' name all August, then let the actual Patrick Mahomes sit on the board until pick 114, twelve rounds in, seven rounds after the league's median first quarterback. The airframe finally arrived 12 spots below sticker, so the paperwork calls it a value; the flightline calls it a scheduling adventure.
