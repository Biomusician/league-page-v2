Disco Chat
Vol 3.VIII
  

The Lowdown
Set Phasers to Stun
Starship Troopers, Dune, Star Wars, Star Trek, Spaceballs - there are plenty of legendary space movies. Your rosters, on the other hand, are 1) not full of space, 2) not legendary (with a few exceptions), and 3) aren’t full of superstar talent (again, exceptions). Part of the reason we like space movies is because they tickle our imagination with what might be, and that’s a large reason why you continue to play this game - you fantasize that one day, that championship could be yours. It’s the middle of the season (almost), and some of you are much closer to realizing that reality than others. In any case, you made it past the BYEPOCALYPSE, which is fantasy football’s The Empire Strikes Back, so here’s hoping your team can spin it into a Return of the Jedi, or that at least your team’s Force Awakens.
  

Matchup Roundup


Fingers (6-1) vs HOP (4-3) 63/37
HOP BYE/INJ: Lamar Jackson, Saquon, CEH, Ruggs
Fingers BYE/INJ: Kenyan Drake, Josh Jacobs
After a close victory over PITCH, HOP is back with a vengeance tempered only by his dearth of running backs. He’s trying to face Derrick Henry and the Gas Man (Myles) with the motley crew of Kenneth Gainwell and MGIII. That’s basically like taking a squad of broken X-wings and a crazy force kid with the decrepit Millenium Falcon against the Death Star. Fingers also has the Emperor Tom Brady, and Darth Vader (Travis Kelce) on his team, along with a recently-returned Jerry Jeudy. If HOP manages to pull this win off, it’ll be just like the Republic winning against the Big Bad Empire.
EMCO (1-6) vs Juan (0-7) 52-48
Pitch BYE/INJ: Kareem Hunt, Will Fuller V, JuJu, Waller
Juan BYE/INJ: Davante Adams, Lazard, Knox
This match is the Spaceballs of the week. First, we have Juan, who after going 0-7 in a not particularly close match last week, is forced to go without Davante Adams, who got COVID, and the Allen Lazard he picked up off the waiver wire for $20, who also got COVID. Oof. What this means is that he’s starting the likes of Zach Pascal and Jarvis Landry, with middling options in Aaron Rodgers and Teddy B. Pitch’s schwartz is definitely bigger in this matchup, as she’s starting powerhouses Kyler, Zeke (and maybe Pollard!) AJB, Gronk, and then… a lot of random players - kinda like the aliens in the background of the cantina scene. No one really knows who Kalif Raymond is, and David Johnson is basically an old Rancor at this point, but they’re a scary bunch - at least if you have to start them.
EMCO (5-2) vs McLovin (5-2) 56/44
EMCO BYE/INJ: NSTR
McLovin BYE/INJ: MT
If last match was Spaceballs, this one is definitely Dune: How it turns out will turn on the performances of just a few players. Will my Paul (Josh Allen) lead the rest of my team (Aaron Jones, D’Andre, Mike Williams, Ridley, etc) on a holy crusade against the evil empire? Or will the combined might of Matt Stafford, JT, Mixon, Chase and Deebo, Gesicki and co, and Diontae unseat me? Cole Beasley is definitely Baron Vladimir Harkonnen, and Nick Chubb can be Glossu Rabban, the less bad evil dude who is always reporting in to his goopy, levitating uncle.
Sealed (3-4) vs The Dude (3-4) 50/50
Sealed BYE/INJ: Russ
The Dude BYE/INJ: Carr, CMC, Montgomery, Kittle
Injuries have not been kind to The Dude’s roster. Perhaps it’s karma for letting Glory draft his team while he gallivanted off to Oklahoma. In any case, he’s still without his two starting RBs, and his TE1. His 3-4 record is a testament to the prominent rise of Cooper Kupp, and solid performance of Darrell Henderson as his fill-in RB1. Evan Engram plays a bit part at TE, but he’s basically like one of those scruffy rat creatures in Star Wars - more scenery than plot device. The Vaqueros have a solid shot at this one, especially if Trevor Lawrence can cobble together a decent game. Sealed is really hoping Russ gets off IR soon, given that it’s left him relying on Jimmy G as his QB2, who may lose his starting job to Trey Lance sooner rather than later, given the 49ers poor record.
Babe (4-3) vs DIP (4-3) 56/44
Babe BYE/INJ: Logan Thomas
DIP BYE/INJ: Chris Carson, Bateman
Last game was a 3-4s battle, and this one is a 4-3s battle. Once again, someone’s moving to 50/50 on the season, but in this case, it’ll be a downgrade for someone rather than an upturn. For DIP, he’s really hoping that Patty Mahomes is more like Luke Skywalker than pre-Vader Anakin, because last week was a lot of terrible; it was definitely NOT podracing. Damien Harris and Antonio Gibson are like the two podracers that joined together to race the same pod, and ended up crashing or being used for comic relief - they’re not great, but not terrible. Stefon Diggs and Terry McLaurin, however, are like the Han and Chewy of this crew, while Hock probably slots in as Princess Leia, and Julio Jones as Admiral Ackbar, because you never know whether starting him will work out or not. For Babe, He’s got Worf and Geordi in the Alvin/Austin 1/2 punch, but he’s also got Michael Carter as the ½ punch. Will Courtland Sutton continue to be relevant now that Jeudy’s back, or will he be Sarlaac food against the WFT?
