Disco Chat
Vol 5.IV
  

The Lowdown
Life is Pain; FF is Life
Injuries, underperforming expectations, pain, and constant unfairness: These are the components of every fantasy football season - at least for your team. Why do you play? No one can answer that question except for you. Is it masochism? Optimism? Did you do really well one year and get hooked on that championship feeling? If you’re the latter, then you’re like that restaurant who’s still open today, proudly displaying “Voted Best in Town 2005” on their wall. Those laurels must be mighty comfy!
  

Week 4


Juan (0-3) vs Babe (2-1)
Here’s a list of good things I can say about Juan’s 0-3 team: You have Josh Allen, picked up Tank Dell, and you’re not the Bears. Sorry about the rough start bud - you’re gonna have to hope Dak remembers how to play football against teams that aren’t the Cardinals to have a hope of coming on top against Babe and his resurgent KWIII, CJ Stroud, and a surprisingly-relevant Adam Thielen, whom it’s rumored is older than Jimmy Graham and Tom Brady combined. Then again, he may be starting Taysom Hill and Josh Downs, so it’s not a completely uphill battle for you, especially if Najee Harris continues to be more terrible than Jaylen Warren, and Jordan Addison finds ways to be great alongside the other Minnesota WRs. Will you stay unwinded like the Bears, or pick up a win like the Texans? We shall see.


Fingers (1-2) vs Double (2-1)
Fingers has managed to phalange his way to a losing record despite having superstars like Kirk Cousins on his team - oh, and that guy from the Chiefs that Taylor Swift is dating, who’s been a bit of bum early in the season but seems to be performing okay now. Joe Mixon has not been shooting up his opponents nearly as much as his front lawn, and Calvin Ridley hasn’t been caught sports betting, but outside week 1 it hasn’t been great for him in Jacksonville. Meanwhile, Double’s off to a 2-1 start despite having Russel Wilson as his QB, most likely due to James Cook being surprisingly relevant in Buffalo. Justin Jefferson doesn’t hurt either. We could have 2 2-2 teams after this one!


EMCO (2-1) vs POP (2-1)
Outside of Garrett Wilson (ew b/c Zach) and a few depth pieces, POP’ll be fighting for a win against a surprisingly solid group of humans on EMCO’s roster: Jalen Hurts, ETN, and the eternal Derrick Henry, who may rival Frank Gore’s longevity in the league. It’s a bit of a conundrum though, since POP’s Geno is tossing to EMCO’s DK Metcalf. EMCO has managed to assemble 3 top-10 options at RB this week, with Raheem Must-start rounding out ETN and Henry. Impressive, when POP’s trying to toss in Kyen Williams, Josh Kelly, and Kenny G (Gainwell not Golladay). Who’s going back to 0.500? My money, if I Calvin Ridley’d my own league, would be on POP.


The Dude (1-2) vs Sealed (2-1)
How is Goff the #18 QB on the week? He’s a superstar, practically - at least that’s what you’d gather based on The Dude’s draft strategy this year! Mac Jones, who showed promise for him week 1, has thusfar continued to be unimpressive, and has to face a Dallas DST who has consistently applied QB pressure and takeaways (unless they’re facing superstar Cardinals and Josh Dobbs). Maybe The Dude should pick up Josh Dobbs instead? The Dude is also rocking Hock alongside LaPorta, who’s scored probably 8x as many points as Kyle Pitts ever did. Also, Mike Evans and Chris Godwin continue to help him due to a resurgent Baker. For Sealed, Herbert finally put up a game-winning performance last week, and Purdy continues to score 2 TDs and throw for like 200 yards, which is as pedestrian as it comes. If he can toss it to Kittle and Deebo more, that’d really help Sealed out this week - stacks on stacks!


DIP (2-1) vs Pitch (0-3)
I don’t know if it was too many White Claws, bad luck, or family sabotage that took Pitch to 0-3, but it sure wasn’t Chris Olave and Ja’Marr Chase, who’re the lone bright spots on this roster. So much hope was poured into these draft choices: Homer picks Deshaun and Jerome and Cooper, Najee “bad at football” Harris, and Michael Thomas have not performed as Browns/Steelers/Saints fans hoped. For DIP, meanwhile, Anthony Richardson has cleared the protocol and is facing the Rams, while Tony Pollard still isn’t broken. Tyreek probably won’t score 70 points again this year, but he’s still good. But with Puka, Nico, and superstar Zack Moss on his side, there’s no way he isn’t favored to keep pitching strikeouts this week against the Claws.


HOP (2-1) vs McLovin (2-1)
It’s at this point I realized we don’t have any 3-0 teams, thanks to Joe Burrow letting me down - otherwise, it’d totally be me! If Tua puts up half as many points as the Dolphins RBs did last week, then my chances of improving to a 75% win rate are practically nonexistent. HOP’s got Bijan, whose team sucks but is basically the entire offense. He’s got Dalton Kincaid, who is like a budget Sam LaPorta. He’s got Christian Watson, who is a WR for the Packers, and he’s got DeVonta Smith, the slim reaper in Philly alongside AJB. Sure, I may get smoked at QB, RB, and TE this week with my poor drafting choices, but at least I don’t have to choose whether to start Justin Fields in my SF spot like HOP does.
