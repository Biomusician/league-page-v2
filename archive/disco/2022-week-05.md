Disco Chat
Vol 4.IV
  

The Lowdown - Gold or Mold?
Sold on the Old?
A couple weeks of poor performance from a previous stud is easy to dismiss, like when your dog has an accident in your house - he’s still a good boy. But, if your dog necessitates a cleanup event three days in a row, you have to wonder if something’s wrong, or your dog is trying to tell you something. DJ Moore, by the way, is that dog. So is Terry McLaurin, and maybe even Jonathan Taylor and Derrick Henry. “Better days are ahead,” say the owners, but only Father Time will tell whether your favorite player’s best year was yesteryear.
  

Week 5


Cold vs Paroled (Babe vs HOP: 70/30)
Mixon hasn’t lived up to expectation and can’t deliver in the red zone. Drake London is having issues with Marcus Mariota tossing him the rock (who wouldn’t?), and Michael Gallup’s gotten off to a sluggish start. Will Trevor Lawrence thaw after last year’s awful performance? Lamar Jackson has been white hot, but it’s only been enough for two wins. Let’s see if Babe can go from fantasy freezer to hurt locker. HOP’s angry after losing the league two of its three years, but he’s been on his best behavior since. Will CEH and Michael Carter drag him back into the depths, or will his new positive influences Davante and Deebo keep him out of a life of fantasy crime? One thing’s for sure, he wants zero percent of whatever it is Joe “Skinny” Flacco is peddling.


Bowled vs Bold (POP vs Juan: 50/50)
Hitting Najee and Alvin K as your RB1/2 is like hitting seven out of nine pins, but ending up with a split. Can Patty Mahomes bring home a spare? Surely, Jaylen and Diontae aren’t going to get it done as his WRs - there’s a reason he’s eighth place right now with two wins. Still, maybe Rhamondre steps up to polish his 12-pounder, and Rashod Bateman finally steps into his WR1 role in Baltimore to strike out on his own. Meanwhile, Juan’s swinging for the fences with CD and Stefon alongside Dameon and My name is Jeff. Herbert is great, but Derek Carr is just a bold choice for a QB2. Isaiah McKenzie, however? Pickets? Etienne? Aiyuk? Bolder than a man standing in front of a rolling boulder.


Rolled vs Gold (Fingers vs Double: 65/35)
Rolled as in crap, as in steam-, as in how Fingers may come out this week against Double and his squad. Russell Wilson has not been rolling. Derrick Henry’s been deposed. Marquise and Terry haven’t been scary at all in the WR spots, and Damien/Antonio are more like court jesters than kingsguard. Meanwhile, Double’s hit a lot on some preseason question marks: Fournette, Robinson, Tyreek, Everett, Sanders, and Cooper have all shown that they can do it. Sure, Cam Akers hasn’t shown it like Robinson has, but there’s still time for the Rams to figure it out. One team’s leaving this game over 0.500, and the favorite is Double.


Blindfold vs Mold (EMCO vs McLovin: 56/44)
Looking at EMCO’s roster, it’s like she pulled her picks out of a hat; that’s the only way I can account for her haphazard picking of studs and duds. For every Chubb, there’s a Justin Fields. For every Cooper Kupp, there’s a James Connor, and for every Zacch Ertz, there’s an Elijah Moore. And we won’t even talk about Dak or Trubisky. Meanwhile, my team is moldy because I’m sitting on them waiting for the breakout. Stafford and the Rams will figure it out. Dalvin and the device are coming for you! Breece Hall has to be good as the rookie 1.01. Irv Smith and the Vikings - the breakout is coming! Amon-Ra may be the only early bloomer here, but I’m a believer.


Lol’d vs Fold (The Dude vs DIP:: 50/50)
When I saw The Dude’s roster (Saquon, A. Jones, J-Jeff, Tee, TJ Hock, Josh Jacobs) I was confident that he was crushing it. When I saw his points for (most in the league), I confirmed it. Then, when I looked at his 1-3 record, I had to laugh because despite his draft, his team has somehow managed to come up short 75% of the time. Meanwhile, DIP took a nice, flat piece of paper and has folded it into an origami monster. MGIII? Eek. Jamaal Williams? Also a yikes. Zeke? What is this, the RB room from hell? Gabe Davis had a good week 1, Jeudy looks terrible along with the rest of the Broncos offense. Josh Allen stands alone as the one fold that hasn’t been creased back and forth into oblivion.


Uncontrolled vs Sold (Pitch vs Sealed 51/49)
Between all the FB sunrise/set pictures and deployment life, I don’t think Pitch has done much beside set a starting lineup. While I’m too lazy to check how many waiver moves she’s made, I do notice that even with CMC, KHunt, Metcalf, and Kittle she’s managed to get her way to 2-2, like a blissfully unaware aircraft flying through a ROZ, somehow managing to avoid the HIMARs shots as well as the aircraft operating there. For Sealed, I’m sold on Geno Smith after 4 weeks of great performance, though I’m not sold that Darren Waller and Travis Kelce was a good draft strategy going forward. Fortunately, Ekeler bought himself back into the RB3 overall this year with last week’s performance. At 3-1 in 2nd place, I buy that he could snag a win here, even with a slightly-unfavored roster shot.
