Matchup Roundup
Hot Takes on Hot Garbage
Some of us came out swinging in the draft, taking big names with big potential, some of us took big names, and some you are potentially sitting on last place out of the gate - but that’s okay! Everyone loves an underdog, and we especially love taking $20 of the underdogs’ money every year. Read on to find out where you fall (and where you fail).
Number 10
The Fatties land here for me. Picking Patty Mahomes seems like an easy choice, but when it leaves you with Aaron Jones as your RB1 (AJ Dillon anxiety anyone?) and AJ Green as your WR2 (if he can even get out of preseason without getting injured) leaves me with major question marks. Love Darren Waller as the TE pick, but Raheem Mostert and Matt Breida are uninspiring flex options.
For the bench, Goff’s not a bad backup, and I like CeeDee Lamb and Justin Jefferson’s chances to make a splash, but the rest just turns my stomach. Darrell Henderson? Alshon Jeffery? Golden Tate? These bench options would have been good two years ago, but now they’re unexciting and unsexy. You have your work cut out for you Babe.
Number 9
AKA Team Dallas, Juan’s team takes the #9 spot not because the Cowboys are bad (We got rid of Jason Garret after all!) but because Juan’s team is bad. I love Dak and Zeke, and Blake Jarwin has sneaky upside, but Stefon Diggs and Mike Williams are starting options with more question marks than plus-ups. Chris Carson is a great RB2 if he can hold onto the ball (and his job), and DK Metcalf may ball out in his flex, but we shall see.
Tua may be a complete non-factor this year, and Phillip Lindsay and Tarik Cohen may be fine bye-week fill-ins, but there is little upside to ride. N’Keal Harry, Pittman Jr., and Gronk may all not pan out. We’ll know after the first couple weeks whether Juan’s big gambles paid off, or whether he’ll be playing catch-up on the waivers the rest of the season.
Number 8
How the mighty have fallen from last season. Joining her husband in the bottom 3 is Pitch, led by Lamar Jackson, Julio Jones, and… a bunch of other players. Chubb is fine I guess, and Bell could ball, but I don’t love him as an RB2. Keenan Allen could fall completely off the radar with his new QB, and Devin Singeltary and Jarvis Landry (if uninjured) are *yawn*.
The depth is provided with week-1 wonder Sammy Watkins, injury-prone Will Fuller, possibly-relevant JK Dobbins, and a potentially-revitalized OJ Howard. Unfortunately, the most notable thing about Pitch’s team, other than having the second-best WR and QB, is that she drafted two DSTs. Hate to break it to you, but you won’t have to put one of your DSTs on the COVID IR slot if one member gets COVID. Stream ‘em!
Number 7
The Dude clearly Breesed through this draft, taking strong options at WR, RB, and TE, but falling off near the end of the draft with too many lottery tickets and not enough solid production. Derrick Henry and Austin Ekeler are a great 1-2 punch as RBs, and I actually like his nominal WR2 (Robert Woods) better than his WR1 (Amari Cooper). His flexes are guys I would want on my bench to wait and see: Rookies Jonathan Taylor and Cam Akers may take time to develop to provide relevance.
Instead, his bench highlights are 2 QBs (Cousins and Wentz; apparently we’re playing SF?), Jordan Howard (one of the unsexiest picks this year), Zack Moss (deep sleeper), Allen Lazard (Packers passing offense LUL), Darius Slayton, and Tyler Higbee backing up Mark Andrews. Braindon Aiyuk may prove relevant now that Jalen Hurd is out for SF, but he didn’t know that when he drafted. Hopefully his luck will continue, and the guys in front of his guys will continue to fall out to make room for his talent to bloom. Otherwise, it’s gonna be another long season for The Dude.
Number 6
I like the starters, hate the bench for Fingers’ draft. Russel Wilson? Unlimited. Dalvin Cook and Jacobs? Prime. AJ Brown? Sign me up. Cooper Cupp? Uh, I guess. DeVante Parker is one of the last men standing in Miami’s WR corps, so I guess he catches a lot of passes, but John Brown is where I start drawing the line.
This is where the wtf starts: Sony Michel? Vomit emoji. Damien Harris? You’re making the classic NE RB error. Tom Brady? Not in NE anymore. Carlos Hyde? Behind Chris Carson and Rashaad Penny. Jerry Jeudy is the sole light of upside in an otherwise uninspired bench. You’re gonna need some waiver electricity to get this team back on track.
Number Fife
Someone had to take Travis Kelce early, and that someone was EMCO. Can’t argue with her drafting Josh Allen, Kamara, or Terry McLaurin, all of whom I love this year, but taking Mike Evans as her WR1 may haunt her with Chris Godwin and Gronk over in TB soaking up targets. David Johnson has a lot to prove as her RB2, after a terrible old-man/broken back showing last year. And TY Hilton as her second flex option just leaves me yearning for a higher-upside, taller, faster option, who gets injured just a little bit less.
Aaron Rodgers is fine as a backup option, but there’s not a lot to love in James White or Nyheim Hines as backup RB options, due to question marks in the Patriots backfield and the Colts’ other RB options. Gesicki is a good backup TE wildcard, but I don’t love Perriman or Edelman. Christian Kirk is consistently undervalued this year, and while I won’t own many shares with my doubts about Kyle Murray, she may strike fantasy gold!
Number Fourp
Just outside a podium draft finish I (McLovin) give myself number 4 (number 1 in my heart). I waited on QB, but Big Ben and Stafford could both ball out this year after resting up from last season’s injuries. Mixon is part of a revitalized Bengals offense, and Gurley has the job all to himself in Atlanta. Davante Adams and OBJ are elite WR talents, and I feel #blessed to be able to start them alongside transcendent options like Tyler Lockett and Michael Gallup.
Of course, they won’t start every week. When byes hit, I have RoJo II of the Bucs, and Pollard/Mattison backing up their star players in case someone comes down with COVID. The Zeke/Cook owners seem pretty confident that their starters won’t miss any games… Robby Anderson, Kenny Golladay, and Marvin Jones have star-shooting upside, and I’m pretty happy with how things shook out.
Number Threep
After beginning with autodraft, HOP snatched victory from the jaws of defeat with a top-three finish. Love his RB and WR options: Saquads, Fournette, Thielen, Calvin Ridley, with Kittle as his TE, and Ingram/Emmanuel Sanders as flex options. Stronk.
Cam Newton is an intriguing backup in New England, and… I actually hate most of his bench apart from Mecole Hardman maybe. I mean, Tevin Coleman? Kerryon Johnson (in a brace recently)? Boston Scott? Antonio Gibson has some upside - as much upside as any RB for the Washington Football Team could, I guess - but it’s still a long shot he’ll put up many points. Still, the algorithms have spoken, and your team’s taken top-3 honors. Now don’t disappoint again like last year. Give that trophy to someone else!
Number Two
A double is two, and Double takes second in the draft grades, led by CMC and Kenyan Drake at RB, Godwin and JuJu at WR, Evan Engram at TE, and Matty Ice at the helm. Conner and Chark are strong flex options, if Conner can stay healthy and Chark maintain his momentum from last year.
Kyler Murray has a lot of question marks, but I don’t mind having him as a backup QB. Adrian Peterson is a silly choice, Marlon Mack has little upside, Justin Jackson and Jamison Crowder will provide some weekly production, while Diontae Johnson, H-Ruggs, and TJ Hock could provide some lottery ticket upside if they pan out. Solid team dude. It’ll make it that much sweeter when we take you down.
Number Uno
It was close, but I’m giving it to DIP with his prescient picks. What he lacks in QBs (though he has 3, they’re all low-ranked), he makes up for in upside. Miles Sanders and CEH? Yes please. Michael Thomas and Allen Robinson? I’d take it in a heartbeat (and DIP did). DJ Moore and Courtland Sutton are both solid WR3s, and his bench is littered with upside.
My favorites warming the pine pony are Tyler Boyd and Kareem Hunt, both of whom could provide startable options on the week, or rise to become every-week starts depending on how their teams shake up. There is no one on his bench I hate, and I hate that that’s true about his bench. I’d love to sit on it with the likes of Latavius Murray, Joe Burrow, Anthony Miller, Deebo Samuel, Parris Campell, or Preston Williams. Even Jalen Reagor could become an every-week start.
I hope you realize that makes you the top target! No pressure on performing the rest of the season - we definitely won’t lord over you every single loss you take. This is fantasy football, and anything can happen after the draft, especially this year. Thanks for playing, thanks for drafting, and please join me in taking down these top 3 fantasy teams this year!
