Disco Chat
Vol 5.II
  

The Lowdown
We’re Back, but Baby…
To those of you whose seasons are over already, I’m sorry; this is what fantasy football is. Diontae is gone. Aaron is gone. Dobbins, Andrews, Kelce, Freiermuth, Lockett, Williams, Ekeler, Aaron Jones, Jakobi… I don’t know what to tell you if you drafted these players, other than this: You should’ve drafted different ones; we wish you the best next year.
  

Week 2


HOP (1-0) vs PITCH (0-2)
After a strong showing week 1, HOP is looking to repeat, even if he has to start the truly awful Justin Fields, who the Bears coaching staff admitted may have been “under-utilized.” You know who’s not being underutilized? Bijan, HOP’s hero in his RB1. It gets gross quick though, with Treylon, Rashid, and maybe even Toney starting in the FLEX spots. Meanwhile, Pitch is rocking Olave, Njoku, Cooper, and Watson. Her team is basically the Browns, which is why it sucks. Not even the ghost of Michael Thomas can save you now.


DIP (1-0) vs Juan (0-1)
Is Anthony Richardson the real deal? DIP will be set for success if AR has as much affinity for football as the Packers QBs have for ruining other franchises. Tyreek Hill probably won’t repeat his ridiculous 200+ yard week from last time, but he’s an advantage over Jordan Addison’s paltry 14.7 points put up Thursday night by Juan. Alex Mattison bombed at 2.4, and Dallas Goedert underperformed with 5.2. It’ll be an uphill battle against Waller, Nico Collins, waiver wire darling Puka, and Tony P of the Cowboys on the DIP side.


Fingers (1-0) vs The Dude (0-1)
Kirk Cousins is certainly diving into fantasy, with a 4-TD week 1 finish that has Vikings fans calling for him to be traded to the Jets, because they hate doing well but still losing. Take a page from the Cardinals and be more like that. CMC is an advantage for the Dude, but Stefon and a rejuvenated Calvin Ridley have the edge against CeeDee and Tee, especially given CD is going against a Jets team that may somehow score negative points this week against a solid Cowboys D. The Hock put up 22.10 though, so if Kelce sits, Fingers will be scrabbling for a backup.


EMCO (0-1) vs Double (1-0)
Can we just take a moment, everyone, to remember the preseason Jets hype? Dalvin Cook. Aaron Rodgers. All the talent from last year. Robert fucking Saleh. But it seems like the hardest knock came after the season started, and their defense was suddenly not godlike, and suddenly people found themselves rooting for Zach Wilson again. He may be fuckin’ your mom, but he’s also tossing your team the rock, so what choice do you have? EMCO has Allen Lazard, so I guess her Jet is still okay. Meanwhile, I confirmed in person this week that Double had no idea it was SuperFlex again this year until halfway through the draft, which explains why Derek Carr and Russell Wilson are his team’s unlimited QBs. Good luck buddy!


Sealed (0-1) vs POP (1-0)
Normally, I’d expect these teams to be undefeated after week 1, but just like the Chiefs, Sealed fell to 0-1 after Herbert, Cook, and Robinson let him down week 1, along with Deebo and Kittle. Was investing in the SF passing game this much a mistake? Probably so, since he didn’t draft Aiyuk, who stole all the points last week. Still, I like those options better than POP’s Zay Flowers and Pat “The Moose,” who will be creating points gaps that Patty Mahomes and Geno Smith will be looking to close. If Rhamondre can overcome the Pats RB curse and actually do something, maybe POP can go to 2-0. Or maybe he’ll just go the way of Elijah Moore and complain his way into irrelevance the rest of the season.


Babe (0-1) vs McLovin (1-0)
Two prior champions and current Cowboys fans square off in a pretty event matchup that will be decided by how well the depth pieces on the roster perform. Babe is off to a good start with Swift going for 25.6 vs MIN, but AJB underperformed as the Eagles’ passing game flagged. Who’s starting Allen Robinson or Courtland Sutton this year? Desperate people, that’s who, and I count myself among them, while looking for Joe Burrow to surpass his 7 point performance from last week. Yikes. Meanwhile, I have to face down a resurgent KWIII, while hoping that Babe’s Lamar Jackson is truly cooked and has Russell Wilson’d himself into fantasy oblivion in Baltimore. Good luck to Babe and the WRs of years past that he’s starting: I’m sure OBJ and Thielen would love to retire instead of playing for his team.
