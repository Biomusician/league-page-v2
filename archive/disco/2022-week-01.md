The Lowdown - VULTIME
Fight’s On
You're perfect: Your draft was prescient, your team will be undefeated, your players will score 20 or more points every week, and the championship will finally (or once again) be yours. The fantasy landscape is freshly-fallen snow blanketing a well-manicured lawn behind a white picket fence in a gentle neighborhood bordering a cheery forest in a... you get the idea. The fact is, the reality of the season is going to carpet bomb it all during the tornado-spawning hurricane that is fantasy football, and it starts tonight. So hitch up those boots, throw on the battle rattle, and get ready - war is hell, and it's Highlander rules! 
Also, there are 12 of us now so this takes me 20% longer to write, and I love each of your teams 83% as much as last season.
  

Week 1


HOP vs The Dude (50/50
Holy moly, it's the battle of the mediocre QBs, as Tua and Mac (HOP) battle Kirk Cousins and Ryan Tannehill (The Dude). It's the new (not) hotness vs the near-middle-aged and middle-of-the-road. What HOP lacks in QBs, he makes up for in Jonathan Taylor, who squares up well against The Dudes Aaron Jones. Two middling tight ends here too, with Shultz going against the Hock. I give the FLEX edge to HOP this week, with Lazard (if he's healthy), Gage, Thielen, and Carter stacked up against Josh Jacobs, Tyler Boyd, Nico Collins, and Garrett Wilson. Justin Jefferson is the Dude's answer to Jonathan Taylor, and we'll see how the superstars shake out the rest of these okay rosters.


Hopes and Dreams vs Browns FTW (55/45)
Alright, we got some QBs here! Herbert is more than a hope or dream, and ol' reliable Tom Brady is back to football for Browns after his brief foray into The Masked Singer. It's RBs vs WRs in this matchup, with CMC and Penny being superior options for Browns against ETN and... wait a minute - I lied. Hopes and Dreams has this in the bag, because he has rookie preseason 3rd-round rocketship hyped Dameon Piece who will single-handedly carry his season. But if not, he has Stefon Diggs and CeeDee to carry him against a trash-tier Seattle offense'd DK Metcalf and the Slim Reaper. Then again, Browns has Kyle Pitts to help. She also has Kittle, but he's (of course) already injured. Groin. Sucks. Derek Carr>son Wentz to round things out.


Sniffin' Wins vs Breece Lightning (48/52)
"Let's ride!" is the rallying cry of the Sniffers, who have newly Denver'd Russell Wilson leading the way against a perennially-injured Matt Stafford. Will Derrick Henry be as awesome as he was last year at 28 years old? Sniffin sure hopes he keeps his nose for the endzone. Ol' reliable Keenan Allen will be running in the WR1 spot for Sniff, along with Hollywood Brown, but I've got Ja'Marr Chase. I'm hoping Breece will have a Chase-like season in his rookie RB year, but it's the jets (sorry EMCO, but you know it's true). Sniffin's depth drops off a bit though, with players like McLaurin, Damien Harris, and Robert Woods rounding it out for the Snozzes.


Luv Da Chubb vs Double TDs (51/49)
Another close one! EMCO's got better RBs, TE, and FLEX, while Double's got two star QBs, and a bit better depth players. His success relies largely on Tyreek Hill panning out in Miami, Cam Akers recovering from his Achilles injury, Albert O riding with Russ in Denver, and Amari Cooper panning out with the Browns. That's a lot of maybes - yikes! Meanwhile, EMCO's got another Chubb this year (repeat!) and has a homer jets pick with Elijah Moore. Is Joe Flacco an upgrade over Zach Wilson and can he propel Moore to fantasy greatness? Probably not, but I'd still take him over Amari Cooper this week. She's hoping Jahan Dotson pans out for her too, and isn't a useless rookie WR like so many not named Ja'Marr Chase are.


Vaqueros vs Boner Dogs (50/50)
What the fuck is that name. Paired with the Pikachu team picture, I can't suss (my SAT word for the day) it out. But what's really sus is the Dogs' WR room. Apparently he went with WR2 quantity over quality. Diontae Johnson (with a terrible QB), Jaylen Waddle, and Rashod Bateman aren't gonna get it done against Mike Evans and Brandin Cooks. He also has another Steeler in Najee Harris, so maybe he just likes investing in mediocrity. In fact, he also has Rhamondre Stevenson on another awful offense (awfense), the Patriots. Matt Patricia and Joe Judge calling plays? Yikes. Will Kelce show up to play this year for Vaqueros? Will Ekeler continue to fend off James Cook and score another 20 TDs this season? Lots of questions in this game, but I give this one to the Cowboys fan (no bias btw).


Just Can't Bear It vs Fatties (55/45)
The returning champion turned hard mode on this year, investing hard in yesteryears' talents Michael Thomas and Julio Jones alongside question marks Drake London and Trevor Lawrence. He's also using David Montgomery against DIP in his RB2 slot for the mental damage, though it'll be an uphill battle for him and Joe Mixon against D'Andre Swift and... DIP is also using shivving Babe with his own knife in his RB2 slot with Zeke. If you two agree to swap RB2 points this week prior to this Sunday, I'll allow it and do a manual scoring change. I prefer Zeke (no bias btw). DIP listens to the samefantasy podcasts I do, so Iove his flex options (Mooney, Jeudy, MGIII) morethan Babe's (Julio, Drake, Jarvis, Marvin). Also, give me Pittman and GabeDavis over AJB and MT any day, acronyms/brevity notwithstanding. May the best man twist the knife in this one.
