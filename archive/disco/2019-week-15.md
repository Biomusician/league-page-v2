GM of the Week
“Chicka chicka yeah! Fake ID, fake ID!.” - McLlovin
I’ve been playing fantasy for 8 years, and have not even made it to a single championship. I keep playing for the same reason people keep drinking, playing the lottery, and rooting for the Redskins; it’s a dangerous mix of destructive habit, blind hope, and concentrated self-hatred. It’s also why my team is named the Anomalies, in that every year I hope my team outperforms expectations instead of crushing dreams. ABM buddies at Tyndall convinced me fantasy football was a better choice than dungeons and dragons, and while I’m still not sure they were right, it was definitely the smart social choice.
The best footballers are value players, like the perennially underrated QBs who get taken in the 7th round - guys like Tony Romo and Jameis Winston who will be fine for your team most weeks and won’t break the draft bank. I hate players who are self-interested to the point where it hurts their team - guys like Zeke, Bell, AB are good, and everyone wants to get paid, but c’mon…
My league rival is PITCH because her team has performed well above expectations, which was supposed to be my team’s thing. I expected her team to play just like the real Browns, and they’ve been playing like the Patriots (DST). But things never go the way we expect.
