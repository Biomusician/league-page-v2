Vol 4.VII
  

The Lowdown - The Mighty
Risen and Fallen
Javonte. Breece. Lance. Keenan. Dobbins. Akers. Pitts. Taylor. Najee. Deebo. Dak. Stafford. Mitchell. Clyde. Jeudy. Sutton. Russ. Robinson. Dillon. Juju. Moore (pick your flavor). If you haven’t yet felt a twinge of regret, then you should buy a lottery ticket, walk through an active minefield, and then collect a prize, because that’s basically how you’ve navigated the first half of the fantasy season. Also, you don’t play in nearly enough leagues, because it’s almost impossible to escape that peanut butter spread of players - but if you did, I’m super jelly. I hope you enjoy your Dameon Pierce/Christian Kirk sandwich filled with Geno Smith, topped with a heaping portion of Taysom Hill, with a side of gritty Jamaal Williams. If you called any of those shots, then congrats, you sultan of Saquon, titan of Travis, colossus of Cooper - you’re one bad injury week away from joining the kings of crash here in the dumpster dive. Also, there are like 100 teams on BYE next week, so if you think you’re hurting this week, you ain’t seen nothing yet.
  

Week Se7en


HOP (6-1) vs POP (4-3) - 45/55 The Dr. Seuss Special!
Significant BYEs: CEH (HOP), Mahomes (POP)
How the mighty have fallen! HOP is no longer undefeated, and POP is hoping to hop on that negative momentum to hand HOP his second loss. Careful man - you could get arrested. Did you hear what happened to Juan? Yeah, me neither; he’s that good. Tua enjoys a rare positional advantage with Mahomes out and old man Rodgers starting for the Dogs alongside Davis Mills. I’m throwing up in my mouth a little typing that. Meanwhile, POP’s spelling Tua with Waddle in his WR position, alongside a recently-returned Hopkins. On the other side, HOP’s spelling Rodgers with Lazard, so the reverse stacking is real this game. Ugliest start of the game is a toss-up between Tre’Quan, Ehlinger, or Mills.


Double (5-2) vs Juan (4-3) - 60/40
Significant BYEs: Herbert (Juan), Everett (Double)
Juan’s Hopes and Dreams are alive after toppling top police dawg HOP, but he’ll have to take down Double without his main firearm, ol’ hand grenade Herbert. That means he’s holding up Derek “Capgun” Carr against Joe “Bazooka” Burrow, and starting a WR or something against Kyle Murray, who now has the aforementioned Hopkins back. With Everett on BYE, Double’s turning to Albert O to score fantasy points on the Denver offense the same way you might turn to an ST and ask them to control 4th+5th FI DCA on an etch-a-sketch. It’ll be fine. Juan’s got the titans at WR and RB, but Double’s RB2 aren’t pushovers, and Tyreek is a wildcard to explode on any given week. Grossest start of the week has gotta be Isaiah McKenzie, Alex Pierce, or maybe DJ Moore. I’d rather Rondale for Juan than DJ for Double. Also, the D in DJ Moore stands for Denniston - look it up!


DIP (4-3) vs Fingers (2-5) - 83/17
Significant BYEs: Keenan (Fingers)
Fingers, those aren’t wins you’re sniffin’ on those fingers of yours. Wash your hands. First, find a way to get Pickett, Russell, Latavius, and Corey Davis out of your starting lineup, then take a bath, treat yourself to a spa day, get a mani/pedi, and come back next season ‘cause man it looks rough. DIP’s Josh Allen/Jalen Hurts QB room isn’t even looking forward to facing you, any more than an MMA fighter would want to fight a homeless person. Derrick Henry is an island of unblemished skin in the middle of the abscess of Fingers’ team, while Jerry Jeudy is a mole on DIP’s Mona Lisa. 


EMCO (2-5) vs Pitch (3-4) – 58/42
Significant BYEs: Mike Williams (EMCO)
EMCO doubled her win total last week, and she’s looking to catapult herself into the playoff mix against a recently-redeployed Pitch. But with Brady, Pitts, and AJ Green potentially starting for her, the AWACS may not be the only thing off station these days. Donovan PJ? Marcus Mariota? The ugliest starts section writes itself here. Meanwhile, Chubbalova McD may be rocking Justin Fields and KJ Osborn, which is nothing to write home about either, unless you’re writing from a WW1 trench about how the bread was stale instead of moldy. Still, Chubb and Kupp write many wrongs, even those wrongs called Elijah Moore.


The Dude (2-5) vs Sealed (4-3) – 93/7
Significant BYEs: Ekeler (Sealed), Kelce (Sealed)
I’ve actually never seen a matchup where one opponent completely outclassed the other at every position across the board until now. I’d call it an uphill battle, but that doesn’t describe a toddler staring into the teeth of a tyrannosaurus. The Dude has 6 players ranked inside the top 10 at their position this week, and KW3 stonks are to the moon. Cousins and Ryan Tannehill are the wonderbread of his sandwich of excellence. Meanwhile, Sealed only can rep 2 top-20 players, 2 top-30 options, and then Darren Waller at TE, leading a veritable army of unremarkables. I’ll never stop pointing out someone starting Olamide Zaccheaus. Jameis isn’t starting, so I can’t wait to see who makes an appearance in his SF spot. Will it be Mark Ingram, Rex Burkhead, or Ben Skowronek? The less gross options are all on BYE. Welp, you win some, and then you lose some terribly, and Jandrew’s fate is Sealed.


McLovin (4-3) vs Babe (2-5) – 51/49
Significant BYEs: NSTR
Last year’s champ isn’t going out without a fight, and at 2-5, he needs every win he can get to secure a spot in the playoffs and a chance at a championship repeat. Meanwhile, here I am like one of those businesses sporting a “best small onion shop of 2012” and no other awards, having won basically 100 years ago in this league, trying to relive my glory days when I could throw a football over them there mountains. Lance and Breece? Who needs ‘em when I got Mike Boone and Matt Stafford. That hurt to type. Lamar and Trevor aren’t going easy on me for Babe, and I may be just another obstacle on his path to glory this year, but I’m going out swinging (flailing?) with Irv, Engram, and Claypool!
