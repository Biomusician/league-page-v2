The Lowdown
Getting Real
It’s finally playoffs. For some of us, this was a foregone conclusion and we’ve spent the last few weeks preparing. For others, take your pick of Hunger Games, Running Man, or the Titanic. Either way, we all know where we stand. You may not be piloting the playoff team you deserve, but it’s the one you need - at least, the one you needed to make it here.
  

This Week’s Matchups
The ‘Ship is Sailing
Teams on BYE: Browns for the Win, Raphi Bomb
Ain’t That a Mitch (DIP) 7-6 vs Corn-Fed Fatties (Babe) 7-6 (63/37)
After a strong start to the season, the Fatties have to contest with a hot DIP. You hate to be relying on Kareem Hunt and Jamaal Williams as your RB2 and FLEX in the playoffs, like Babe is. Then again, DIP is trotting out Sony Michel and Alshon Jeffery. OBJ and Aaron Jones have both been hit or miss this season, so Babe’s not out of it, but DIP has the advantage here, even with Travis Kelce bolstering Babe’s roster at the TE position. Last time these two met, Babe pulled off a 127-105 victory.
Anomalies (McLovin) 8-5 vs Rising Stars (Juan) 6-7 (68/32)
You love when your opponent runs to the waiver wire for a skill player for your playoff matchup, but that’s what Juan did, calling on the gods and Tarik Cohen to carry him to victory. And while Saquon and Mark Ingram are favored against Bell and Singletary, they haven’t been quite as consistent lately. It’s likely that either team will use this match as validation that they won the early Saquon/Conner trade the two teams made this season. Juan crushed McLovin 150 to 66 when these teams first met in week 1, and he won again in week 10 135-112. McLovin’s looking for his first win against the Rising Stars in a favored matchup.

The Highlights
Week’s Best Decision
DIP has the distinction of being the only person I know to be brave enough to start Mitch Trubisky for 25 points, then promptly dropping him for a better QB. It won him the match, and a spot in the playoffs. Good choice on the drop.
Start of the Week
Dak Prescott wasn’t looking like a great start in the first half, but be pulled out enough points (21) to carry Rising Stars (Juan) to victory against HOP, securing a playoff spot.
Five Dolla Winner
