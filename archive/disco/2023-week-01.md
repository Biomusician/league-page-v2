Disco Chat
Vol 5.I
  

The Lowdown
We’re Back Baby!
We may PCS, but Disco never dies. Thanks everyone for returning to another year of the Disco redraft dynasty! We’ve all won and lost games we didn’t deserve, but just like the CRC program, we’re back again to bang our nuggets against the wall, searching for victory and validation on our totally-unique and not-stolen-from-a-website rankings that we developed based on months of personal research, tape study, and good old gut feeling. Or, just do what the CRC program office does: Take the path of least resistance and then blame your failure on “the system.” Am I salty after a year at the 752 OSS dealing with ACC staff and a bunch of crusty civilians? Maybe, but not as salty as 11 of us are gonna be after losing this fantasy football league. Buckle up buttercups, it’s gonna be a wild one.
  

Week 1


HOP vs Sealed
The cops are looking for Sealed this year, led by a 1-2 punch of Bijan and Aaron Jones. Honestly, it’s the RB depth that’s carrying HOP’s squad, 8thPlace, which is looking to improve upon their moniker. Hopefully they do a better job than the Commanders did after they redesignated from the WA Football Team. WAFT. The Rangers are on tap with Herbert, and a strong WR corp of Davante Adams, Deebo, and Kittle. Yes, Kittle’s a TE, but when he has a good/great game, he’s basically another WR on your roster. What a man. Not a lot of opening night action on this game, unless HOP calls up Jerick McKinnon for some reps out of the Chiefs backfield.


Juan vs Double
This one’s a fairly even matchup between Double and Juan, with Josh Allen presenting a gigantic QB gap for Russell Wilson and the gang on Double’s side to make up. However, the RB, WR, and TE battle will be won by Double, who’s flexing with the Chubbalubbadingdong, Miles Sanders, J-Jeff, Aiyuken, and Mark Andrews. But, if Amon-Ra and Jahmyr Gibbs pop off on Thursday night for Juan, maybe he’ll create a points deficit that Double can’t climb out of. Or maybe Double will just forget to set a lineup since he’s currently traversing the US en route to Robins to help with whatever multi-floor TOC-whatever they’re standing up there. Good luck!


Fingers vs Babe
For a one-time champion, Cornfed isn’t starting this year out with a bang, and is favored to lose against the Finger wiggles, who are somehow Diving into Fantasy even though they’ve been underwater for some time. If Fingers and co. don’t crack under the pressure, they’ll be enjoying an advantage conferred by superiority at RB and TE, with Mixon and Pierce leading the charge against Babe’s AJB and Lamar Jackson. Will Travis Kelce un-hyper extend his knee? If so, then maybe Fingers can defeat Babe’s Kyle Pitts, who’s coming off a year that went about as well as Russia’s war into Ukraine. Best of luck to Pitts (not Russia) on turning it around. The depth pieces on Fingers’ side should push him over the top, but just like Russia saw, sometimes the worst-laid plans don’t go quite as expected.


EMCO vs DIP
I love EMCO’s team here, and The Chad Hennessys are dipping deep to stay competitive against a team led by Jalen Hurts and Derrick Henry. Danny Dimes was okay last year, and maybe Darren “The Walrus” Waller will co-animal with him to turn him into NY’s GOAT, but with Tony Pollard and David Montgomery playing in his RB spots, it’ll be an uphill battle. As DIP do, his WR depth is decent: Tyreek, Diontae, and the Walrus stack all make a potentially-formidable foe, but we’ll see if the preseason bets pay off against EMCO, who’s chosen to go with ol’ reliables Saquon, Derrick Henry, Metcalf, McLaurin, and Etienne.


The Dude vs McLovin
Remember when The Dude won this league? Yeah, me neither - my memory doesn’t go back that far. I have the clear advantage at QB and RB, but the CeeDee/Tee combo on the other side scares me a little, probably because it rhymes. Will Jared Goff and the Lions start off strong on Thursday night? If so, then The Dude may abide this week and take an early win. Or will his ill-advised combo of Mike Evans and Chris Godwin (bold strategy Cotton) drag him down to the depths of despair, like his namesake after the rug was taken? We shall see.


Pitch vs POP
The two Ps square off in a totally PP matchup. On paper, POP’s squad is looking to pop off against a poor QB (Deshaun Watson), as well as Paker Mayfield (poetic license). However, Pitch has Ja’Marr Chase and Chris Olave, and POP’s Garrett Wilson and Jahan Dotson on the other side are looking mighty inadequate against that star power. Meanwhile, Pop’s looking for Patrick and Pat (Friermuth) to pad his chances of winning against Pitch. Will she catch an early break and pull out a purportedly impractical victory here? I’m rooting for chaos, so I sure hope so!
