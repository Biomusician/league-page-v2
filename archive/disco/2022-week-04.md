Disco Chat
Vol 4.III
  

The Lowdown - Sink or Swim
Drive to Survive
How you feel about your team largely depends on how your draft picks have panned out in the first few weeks. HOP’s chances of remaining undefeated this entire season are about as good as my convincing him that I’m a cop IRL, Austin Ekeler recovering his redraft value, or getting a Justin Jefferson owner to trade him at a discount. Three weeks in, it's time for cutting rates and cutting bait: Will Kyle Pitts ever get involved in the offense like he deserves to be? Will the Jaguars be good this year? Will your team? Unless you're 3-0 like HOP (11 of us are not), then it's still kind of up in the air. But remember: The Giants were like a TD away from being 3-0 too, so don't get too comfortable.
  

Week 3


DIP vs HOP (64/36)
It’s Dick In Pants vs Hooked On Phonics this week in a battle of the tri-acronymical names. I’m pegging this (Deadpool style) as the week that HOP takes an L (also Deadpool style, probably), and goes and pulls someone over for speeding or jaywalking or something as a result. The reason? I just don’t think CEH is as good as his top-10 fantasy finishes this year suggest, that Dalton Schultz can be good with Cooper Rush, or that Allen Lazard can be a WR1 with an aging Aaron Rodgers. Plus, he may be starting Russell Gage, and Tom Brady isn’t timeless either. Finally, three words: Zach Wilson sucks. For DIP, his success hinges largely on his QBs: Josh Allen and Jalen Hurts, once again the QB1/2 on the week. It won’t even matter that he’s flexing Lockett, MGIII, and Jeudy, because he can get away with murder with his QBs. It’s like getting copped then flashing your military ID, or so I’ve heard…


Sealed vs Juan (61/39)
Welp, Juan didn’t go to 0-3 last week, eking one out against DIP’s gang, so there’s some hope, even if he’s relying on Jeff Wilson and ETN to get it done. With Herbert (minus a couple ribs) and Carr in at QB, he enjoys a large advantage against the Vaqueros’ Jameis and Geno, who are a match made in fantasy hell. If we could choose a team that’s the antithesis of DIP’s it’s Sealed’s. Ekeler’s no longer a top option, but Sealed is relying on him against Pierce to get it done; if he can’t, then maybe the bell should begin to toll for a true fantasy great (CAO 2021). Diggs and DeeDee are in at WR for Juan, while Mike Evans and Brandin Cooks square off for Sealed. Even with Dak out, CD proved he could do it last week. Even though Juan is not favored here, I give the match to him in a come from behind victory because even though both are Cowboys fans, only Juan cared enough to actually draft any players from his favorite team.


EMCO vs Fingers (55/45)
The first thing that stood out to me after taking a look at EMCO’s lineup was that I was wrong about believing it couldn’t get any worse than Geno and Jameis. EMCO is proving me wrong by starting Justin “Yips” Fields and Mitch “Also a Terrible Prior Bear” Trubisky. But, with Dak out, what can she do? Start Cooper Rush against Washington? Maybe, but that’s like choosing the last wilted nugget of lettuce for your salad, or the hours-old last piece of pepperoni pizza at the pizza bar. For Fingers, he’s hoping that David Montgomery will be out again so Khalil Herbert can smash, that Derrick Henry finds a way to run against the Colts (who beat the Chiefs somehow), and that Antonio Gibson continues to hold onto the football while Brian Robinson recovers. Neither of these teams will ride comfortably while the injuries play out, and Russell Wilson will need to play out for Fingers to have any chance of bringing this season to a successful conclusion.


The Dude vs Babe (50/50)
The Dude is really digging deep to find a way to philosophically deal with starting 0-3 after winning the championship last year. This year, he’s just pissed that the rug’s been pulled out from under him, with Justin Jefferson and Tee Higgins, his supposed headliners, turning in a couple duds in recent weeks. Sure, he’s got Doubs for GB, but who knows whether Rodgers will trust the rookie any more than he trusts the COVID vaccine moving forward. But that’s just, like, my opinion man. For Babe, another contender last year, he really needs oldies Michael Thomas and Julio Jones to keep not being injured to have a strong chance, while he looks to late-round steal Trevor Lawrence to bolster his chances at QB. He’s got Lamar Jackson, who had an amazing week 3, while he waits for Mixon and Dobbins to do something significant in his RB spots against Saquon and Aaron Jones for The Dude. This matchup may hinge on whether the GB offense gets rolling for the Dude, or whether the Ravens once again carry Babe to victory.


Pitch vs HOP (45/55)
Pitch pitched out against her hubby last week, but she’s got a chance to improve to 0.500 this week against the Osteoperros(is), which is POP’s team’s name translated to spanlatin. Speaking of translating, drafting Patrick Mahomes, Najee Harris, and Alvin Kamara have not translated to winning moves thus far for POP, while dart throws like Rhamondre, Curtis Samuel, and Greg “The Torch” Dortch have. POP’s team is the only team I know of to have three Steelers (Najee, Diontae, and the Muth), and the only way it could be grosser is if one of them were ‘Biskey. Meanwhile, Pitch is hoping that Pitts will finally start to return his draft day value, along with CMC and Kittle, who have been busts so far as well. Fortunately, K-Hunt, DeVonta Smith, and Olave have been moving the chains for her against her opponents, at least well enough to get to 1-2. Marcus Mariota and Tom Brady, meanwhile, are neither helping nor hurting, and won’t hold a torch to Mahomes and Rodgers (and, of course, Dortch).


Double vs McLovin (57/43)
Breece Lighting is going to pull it together for me eventually, even if it’s not this week. Again, I still blame EMCO. Dalvin Cook, even if he’s wearing The Device, can still smash, and Matthew Stafford will be fine as soon as he realizes he has more than 1 WR (Kupp). Sure, I’ve got Amon-Ra and Courtland Sutton, but Russell Wilson has thrown fewer TDs than Geno Smith this year, so I’m not holding out for huge upside. Meanwhile, Double has Burrow, Fournette, and Robinson headlining for him alongside Tyreek and Amari Cooper. His roster could be good last year and this year, and is better than his 1-2 record indicates. You know who isn’t though? Cam Akers, who continues to see larger workloads, but just doesn’t look the same as James “The Achilles Steal” Robinson. Come to think of it, Double’s all in on the Achilles injured RBs. Kyler is decent even without Hopkins, and is much better than Daniel Jones is for me.
