The Lowdown
Oregon Trail Edition
You thought last week was the Oregon Trail edition? No way, that game was good enough for repeated playthroughs, and your teams are either terrible enough for me to keep hammering them, or good enough that I can keep making up favorable Oregon Trail comparisons. Sometimes, jokes stop being funny after you make them enough times, and there’s that one guy who doesn’t know where that line is. Well, here’s to not being that guy, because Oregon Trail is awesome and we haven’t gotten close to crossing that line yet.
Speaking of beating a dead horse, Dude, you need to send me your address in Tinker so I can send you a winner’s trophy.
  

Matchup Roundup
Round ‘Em Up
Packing: HOP (2-2) vs The Dude (2-2) 75/25
In Oregon Trail, you could ignore half of the stuff that was up for sale in the store. All you needed was dried fruit, vegetables, and maybe some spare wagon parts if you were feeling spicy. Did you ever buy extra bonnets, winter clothes, candy, or shit like that? Then you were probably bad at the game. HOP’s team is dried fruit and vegetables, and The Dude is packing hopes, dreams, and candy canes. DK Metcalf has already shown up big for HOP, while Kupp and Henderson have predictably provided some early morale that won’t get it done later when hunger strikes and there aren’t enough bullets to hunt buffalo (Ws). Particularly embarrassing choices include Aiyuk, Emmy Sanders, Sammy Watkins, and wtf is CMC doing in your FLEX when he’s doubtful? CMC is basically like packing grandpa’s corpse in your wagon because you want to bury him in Oregon, and then your entire party dies because you should have packed 200 more pounds of dried fruit and vegetables.
Hunting: Fingers (3-1) vs Juan (0-4) 60/40
Did you ever try hunting buffalo with a freaking pistol? Maybe your rifle got lost in the river, or maybe you ran out of the right bullets and you just needed food so bad, and the buffalo stampede started and so you ran out and started blasting. Those mfers just wouldn’t die, no matter how many pistol shots you wasted into their gigantic noggins. Welp, that’s where you are, Juan; you brought a pistol to a herd of buffalo, and your chances of getting stampeded are much higher than bringing home the W of buffalo bacon. In the battle of old QBs, you’re losing: Rodgers v Brady. Robinson and Damien Williams probably don’t get it done against Derrick and J-Jacobs. Hopkins and Adams are basically your only hope; you have two rifle shots to make it happen. Otherwise, Dawson Knox, Waddle, Sanders, Renfrow, and Big “hold ball for half a second behind a terrible O-line before tossing it to Najee” Ben aren’t going to get it done.
Fording the River: EMCO (2-2) vs DIP (3-1) 50/50
Sometimes the river is only ~3 feet high, and your chances of making it over seem pretty dead even. Does a rogue wave tip you, or does the soft current make it easy to make it over without paying the indigenous peoples through the nose in dried fruit and vegetables? EMCO and DIP are at the river, they’re both packing lots of sundries, and they’ve gotta make a decision about how to cross the river to collect their W. Mahomes has a tough matchup against BUF, and Collins didn’t perform for DIP in TNF against the Rams. DIP’s wheels are a little stuck in the mud so far, but he’s making progress. Meanwhile, EMCO’s Stafford start left her leaning a little into the current - she’s not in trouble yet. The oxen pulling DIP’s team are Diggs, McLaurin, and the Hock, while EMCO’s looking to Diontae, A-Rob, and Jonathan Taylor to shoulder the load. We’ll see which team makes it over the river come Monday night.
Snakebites: McLovin (3-1) vs Sealed (3-1) 62/38
Sometimes, everything is going great with your expedition until disaster strikes: Snakebite! Even though you always know sucking the poison from the wound and washing it with whiskey is the best option, whether your unfortunate expedition mate/best friend/sibling makes it through the next day or two seems largely up to chance. In this case, both of these teams are doing well, but both have been struck by terrible TNF performances: Sealed only got 10 points from Russel Wilson before his hand was bitten by the injury snake, while Tyler Lockett was struck by DK Metcalf’s 2x TD poison and only scored 8. Can Aaron Jones and Najee Harris suc the poison out for McLovin, or will the whiskey of Swift/Cooks/OBJ keep infection at bay? Will Dalvin Cook and Corderalle Patterson rub salt in the wound, or will Tyreek Hill travel back in time to apply antivenom and win the week automatically for Sealed? Time will tell which wagon team makes it to a week 5 victory in Oregon.
Party Dispute: Babe (1-3) vs Pitch (1-3) 60/40
When food (wins) begins running short, you haven’t crossed the mountains before winter, the buffalo herds have thinned, the wagon tips in the river, and you lose Will Fuller to dysentery, Logan Thomas to a buffalo stampede, and Gronk to gangrene, tensions within a wagon party can bubble over, even between a married couple. Kyler and Justin Herbert are looking on, mystified, as Babe and Pitch debate whether they should’ve even started on this trip, while their children Kamara and Ekeler (Babe) and Zeke/Hunt (Pitch) look on, with big, worried eyes. “We’ll be fine,” says Darren Waller, adjusting his ten gallon hat as he rides alongside, eyeing Mark Andrews with distrustful eyes. Trey Sermon and Tony Pollard ride in the wagon FLEX spot, their frail bodies swaying to and fro as the wagon Pitches over the uneven trail. “What do you mean we have to take a turn in the starting lineup? We’re not cut out for walking.” They steal a peek over at Courtland Sutton, who is looking a little worse for wear as he shambles alongside the horses. Meanwhile, Joe Burrow and Justin Fields, who have sold themselves as experienced guides even though they’re both new to this, share a knowing look before barking out orders to break for camp.
Bottom line, unless Kyler and Waller can ball, Babe’s wagon is the one making it home.
