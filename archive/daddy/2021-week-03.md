The Lowdown
“I’m Just Here so I won’t Get Fined.”
It’s great to be a part of something great; being in a place where everyone is striving to be their best, do their best, and will win at all reasonable cost raises the skill level of all participants, and creates a deep feeling of teamsmanship and esprit de corp. I’m describing, of course, my daughter’s soccer team - we’re nowhere close to that. This is fantasy football, and most of us couldn’t care less what happens to the other teams, or whether that one guy gets beat with soap socks. We’re only 2 weeks into a 17-week season, which by my (calculator’s) math means 11.7% of the way done. That’s definitely a large enough sample size to know that Kittle is washed, Kenny G is injured forever, Saquon is toast, Danny Dimes is God’s gift to football, and Urban Meyer is the worst NFL coach since Adam Gace and the Clapper. I hope you’ve drawn your final conclusions and made those critical roster moves; otherwise, you may want to start planning for next year.
  

This Week’s Matchups
Hot Ones
Texas Pete (747 scovilles): Bazinga (1-1) vs Jolly (0-2) 82/18
I once knew someone who wouldn’t eat anything with pepper on it, because it was “too spicy.” Granted, she’s my 3 year-old daughter, but still - that’s what this matchup is: pepper. Bazinga’s teams is like Aaron Rodgers and the Packers after getting blown out week 1, coming into Detroit with pent-up anger and rage and an abundance of fantasy points. Kamara did nothing for him. Amari did nothing for him. Dalvin Cook was just fine not amazon, and DK Metcalf has been a slow start. Not this week. Sorry Jolly, but Kyler Murray may not be able to save you, especially if you’re forced to start injured Eli Missile in your lineup. Even with JuJu’s maybe increased role with Diontae out, it’s gonna be an uphill battle. Take some joy and solace in every pass that Bazinga’s Brady throws to your Gronk, but know that your matchup is probably going to end in a blandslide of flavorless blowout.
Sauce Bae Skinny Habañero Sauce (2500 scovilles): McLovin (1-1) vs Karen (0-2) 67/33
People see “habañero” in the name and assume it’s gonna crush, but it’s got homeopathic quantities of habañero - just enough to make you feel brave or proud when you get it down. McLovin’s enjoying positional advantages at every position, though there is cause for concern: Aarons could go full week 1 and put up like 5 points. Zeke could continue to cede carries to Pollard. Diontae could be out with his knee issue, which Mike Tomlin said was “good, not to be confused with great.” That’s a perfect description for Karen’s team, which young stars Najee, Pitts, and Chase. DJ Moore has come out the gate strong this year, and Chark flashed in week 1, but crashed in week 2. Based on pattern analysis, this could either be a flash or splash game, but I wouldn’t play him in cash games.
Maple Wood Smoked Onion Hot Sauce (7900 scovilles): Kelsey (2-0) vs Ross (0-2) 58/42
Just like the sauce, this matchup seems comfortable and delicious on paper: a 2-0 team going against a 0-2 team should be a smash, right? But hiding behind that facade are two tangy teams tilted to tangle. Sure, Bishop Sycamore has CMC and Lockett, and sure Godwin has looked like the clear #1 in TB thusfar, but there are questions about Julio’s role in TEN and Higgins’ injury this week. Plus, I give the kicker edge to Ross, who’s starting the piquant Younghoe Koo. Can one yeet footballs with one’s feet? If so, that’s exactly what I expect from Koo. JT and Gibson are fine starts for Ross, especially if JD McKissick takes a step back and lets AG get all the work we know he deserves. Stefon Diggs should be fine, and Damien Harris looked amazing last week in his boss running TD. Kelsey has the edge, but Ross has an in to take down the league’s first-place team, if Kittle does more than very little.
Adoboloco Fiya! Fiya! Hot Sauce (77k scovilles): Zoomer (2-0) vs Basic (2-0) 60/40
Here we go! The Fiyas referenced above are two-by 2-0 teams vying to stay undefeated, and potentially steal the top spot from Kelsey. Basic claims to have been lucky his first two weeks, but Derrick Henry is the king, Tyreek Hill is elite, and the rest of his team has managed to play the adobos to their locos. Zoomer’s favored here because Cooper Kupp has been even mas loco, with finishes as the WR10 and WR1 on the year. Plus, he’s got DeAndre Hopkins, who’s been no slouch with Murray tossing him the adoboskin (okay, that was a stretch). Mike Williams and Deebo Samuel (the current NFL yardage leader) round out an excellent receiving corps that’s going to be tough to overcome in a PPR format. Both teams are currently slotted to start middling defensive options: Zoomer’s got the Chiefs against the Chargers (while starting Herbert and Mike Williams; lol), and Basic’s got the Niners against Green Bay. I guess he hopes they’re playing week 1 GB and not week 2.
The Last Dab (2M+ scovilles): DIP (1-1) vs Distro (1-1) 56/44
The spiciest matchup of the week is brought to you by two 1-1 teams, competing for a chance at >0.500. Not sure how much Mylage DIP is gonna get this week with Tua’s broken ribs in Miami, but Sterling Shepard has been sneakily effective opposite the oft-injured Kenny G. If the Giants can figure out Saquon, maybe they’ll have a halfway-decent offense - which is exactly what each of these two teams is trying to piece together. Speaking of piecing together, you could put Chris Carson and CEH (Distro’s RBs) together, and they still wouldn’t equal a CMC, Derrick Henry, or Aaron Jones from last week. CEH has graded out poorly these first couple weeks, but there’s still hope for the breakout. Like TJ Hockenson, who’s been killing it in Detroit. Lamar Jackson, Davante Adams, and (maybe) a resurgent Ridley will have to carry DIP against Patty Mahomes, Terry McLaurin (gross QB), and a Mike Evans who benefits from AB being put on the COVIDs list. Just like hot ones, someone’s gonna get their ass kicked in this one, and there’s gonna be a lot of sweating it out. I’m rooting for DIP, because Distro threatened to beat me with a soap sock. Karma’s a bitch!
