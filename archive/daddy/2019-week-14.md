Getting Real
It’s finally playoffs. For some of us, this was a foregone conclusion and we’ve spent the last few weeks preparing. For others, take your pick of Hunger Games, Running Man, or the Titanic. Either way, we all know where we stand - well, everyone except Ross and The Commish, who have no idea what bets they may or may not have made.
