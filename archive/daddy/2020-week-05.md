Daddy Issues
Vol III.I
  

The Lowdown
Everyone You Love is Hurt
If you’re still here with all your players healthy and a winning record, then congratulations: We hate you. That, or we don’t care because your team is trash and you’re joining the Jets in tanking for Trevor. Fantasy has not been kind to us this year, and whether it’s a lack of preseason games, COVID, or the fact that 2020’s not panning out as well as we’d hoped, the NFL has taken a heavy toll. You may have noticed that I haven’t been doing newsletters. I blame COVID, or something. That, and like 3 of you read these and I’ve been busy AF. Anyway, here’s hoping your rosters are waiver-wire Frankensteins heading into Halloween. In any case, there’s still some time to turn things around, if you believe.
  

This Week’s Matchups
Injuries and BYE Weeks and COVID
Game of the Week: Ross (3-1) vs Kelsey (2-2) 75/25
Two of the contenders duke it out this week, though the loss of CMC, Chris Godwin, and Julio Jones tilts the scales towards a Ross victory. He’s got deep talent, with waiver wire adds like Antonio Gibson and Myles Gaskin backing up a trending Kareem Hunt and Rookie JT. When you can bench AJB and OBJ and still field talent like Hopkins, Thielen, and Diggs, your team is in good shape. Kelsey’s looking for a miracle led by Russell Wilson, Josh Jacobs, and maybe another 3 TD week from Lockett. He may need one.
Playoff Mixer: Distro (2-2) vs Jolly Mangina (2-2) 75/25
With Golladay and Hockenson on BYE for Distro, he’ll be relying on Jimmy Grahampa to get things done. I would never trust a Bear to get it done under Matt Nagy, but to each his own. Young talen like Mahomes, CEH, McLaurin, and Brown pair with TY and Randall Cobb to get things done. Oh, and he still has Josh Gordon on his bench for some reason. So fetch. On the other side of the ball, things are looking grim. When your RB2 is Zack Moss, you need Jesus - both to save your week, and your team running back depth. Ekeler’s injury and AJ Dillon’s BYE makes RB tough for the Mangina this week, though Kyler and Robinson may put up monster weeks to push him over the top and into the playoff conversation.
The Denzel: DIP (2-2) vs Denzel (2-2) 65/35
It was fun to make fun of Denzel, but now Saquon, Bell, and Mack are all injured on his team and it feels like kicking a man when he’s down. Seriously, he may have to start Chris Thompson or Damien Harris this week, and that just feels bad. There are some bright spots on his roster though: Herbert has had a decent rookie showing, Deebo Samuel didn’t reinjure himself on his return, and Aiyuk looked pretty decent! DeVante Parker continues to impress, and Jarvis Landry is a better QB than Baker Mayfield. They’ll all have to get it done to beat DIP’s team though - though, if Calvin Ridley can’t figure out how to catch footballs without Julio on the field like he did this week, it may give him an easier shot. Davante Adams is on a recovery BYE for DIP, but he should win this match handily.
Almost Upset of the Week: McLovin (3-0) vs The Commish (0-4) 65/35
Can Deshaun Watson be as effective without Hopkins? Was Hopkins for DJ a fair trade? Will being without Chubb and Mike Williams help the Commish win? The answer to all of these is probably not. Joe Mixon stocks are up after a monster week 4, as are Will Fuller V’s, who not only managed to have a decent fantasy day, but also managed not to get hurt. The Chark attack is back as well, and DJ Moore is looking to get out from under Robby Anderson’s shadow to help the Commish win an unfavored matchup.
Beatdown Special: Bazinga (4-0) vs Basic (1-3) 85/15
You win some, you lose some, and then you just get straight up Jets’d in some. I don’t think Basic is a worse GM than Adam Gase, but when the only player left on your roster from this year’s rookie draft is KJ Hamler (of CeeDee Lamb, Tee Higgins, and Cole K-meh-t, you’ve gotta be asking yourself some questions right now. Starting AJ Green as your WR2 is like driving cross-country in a 90s model Ford Fiesta; you can probably do it, but you don’t feel great about it. Meanwhile, Bazinga’s driving team Tesla/Lambo/Star Destroyer: From Kamara to Cook, A-Rob to DK “Touchback” Metcalf, Kelce to Cooper - you like what you see. And somehow, he still has the NE DST rostered, even though they lost like 16 guys to COVID and injury.

Week’s Best Decision
The Commish: Starting Joe Mixon was tough after the first 3 weeks, and many managers would have looked elsewhere for their RB2/Flex option, but the Commish stuck with it and was rewarded by a 3 TD performance - almost enough to carry her to victory against The Evil Emperor himself, Bazinga. Better luck next time, because you made the right call.
Week’s Worst Decision
Mangina: You were tempted by Higbee’s 3 TD performance week 2, even after a pedestrian showing week 3, and he rewarded you with 5.1 points on 3 catches for 21. Meanwhile, your bench was on fire with Big Dairy/Little Kittle/Baby Kittle himself, Robert Tonyan, who used the Packers’ complete loss of all talented WR talent to turn in a 3 TD, 33.8 point performance. That would have been enough for the victory, especially since DIP couldn’t be bothered to put together a contingency plan for Davante Adams, leaving the inactive player on his roster. 🔔Shame🔔
