Nobody has touched a roster since the draft. Every team is still exactly the force it designed on draft night, which is itself worth knowing — this page fills up the moment that stops being true.

Once moves start, Force Flow reads each one against the roster it changed: what the team was short of, what it gave up, what the move cost in FAAB, and whether the positional rank actually moved. Moves that do not survive that reading get labelled, not hidden.
